## 1. Three different objects in the mainline

Let e denote content, r its author/role qualification, c a complete condition,
and x=Phi(e,r,c) the selected contextual K/V carrier. A fixed target observation
is F_q(x). For a declared author evidence distribution P_u the measurement is

    S_q(u) = integral F_q(Phi(e,r,c)) dP_u(e,r,c).

Encoding geometry Phi, target-reading law F, and author composition P are
different objects. Measuring a discrepancy in Phi does not establish a human
trait; fitting F more accurately does not identify Phi or P. Psychological
interpretation needs the corresponding external/behavioral evidence later.
Joint choices and condition responses remain available, not deleted as noise.

## 2. Product-design coordinates and actual mixed evidence

On a declared balanced 2x2 actual design, signs s,o are +/-1. The endpoint
table has the exact unique expansion

    x_so = m + s a + o b + s o t,
    t = (x_++ - x_-+ - x_+- + x_--)/4 = W/4.

This is a four-point product-design/Fourier decomposition, not a low-rank
assumption about all language. A changing t across expression conditions may
share a span even if individual axes rotate. Conversely comparable author
contrasts may survive even if Euclidean carrier spans do not transport.

Common-token IDs and absolute positions make the vector comparison meaningful

population scale. Rank is a finite-span property, not number of personality

## 3. Why writer interaction is not the same as reader interaction

For a nonlinear F, define I=sum_so s o F(x_so). Even t=0 can give I!=0: for
the scalar example x_so=s a+o b, F(x)=x^2, I=8ab. Thus a target interaction
does not by itself prove a mixed writer direction. HM104 measured both W and
I. HM105 additionally measures whether the actual W has source-shared support
in exactly the same current coordinates.

Let Pi be a source-only orthogonal projector and R=(Id-Pi)W. Modify only t:

    x*_so = x_so - s o R/4.

All content and owner marginal means remain unchanged, and W*=Pi W. By the
fundamental theorem of calculus, wherever F is differentiable,

    I-I* = (1/4) sum_so integral_0^1
                 J_F(x_so-lambda s o R/4) R d lambda.

For our finite normalized attention function differentiability holds. This
is an exact explanation of the diagnostic: removing a large Euclidean
direction need not change a target much if that direction is weakly read.
Small Euclidean displacement can have large effect under a sensitive reader.
The numerical line interior can be off the actual text manifold; the formula
does not assert a natural cognitive path or unique neural mediation.
HM105 evaluates both endpoint tables directly, not an integration precision
grid or a target-score fit. The diagnostic cannot be advertised as a cold
prediction, because the actual application endpoints supply R.

    S(P+) - S(P-) = I/4.

Therefore the loss of jointly qualified response is also the loss of a declared

These positive laws are designed profiles, not real measured personalities.

The proper next theoretical decision is not 'raise accuracy'. It is whether
