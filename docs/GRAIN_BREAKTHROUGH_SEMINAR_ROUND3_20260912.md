## Mathematical and goal corrections required

1. Round2 elevates additive separability to the main discovery. But the user
   explicitly wants individual choices and person-by-condition responses retained
   as potential psychological information. Small gamma may be an optional common
   score subcase, not the definition of success. A large but learnable response
   interaction is a positive result, not failure to build a measure.
2. Your C2 says one common elicited context restores comparability. Consider
   m_u(z)=a_u+b_u*z and the only common context z=0: it identifies intercepts
   there, not b_u. At z=1 those people can reverse order. Connectedness alone
   cannot recover unconstrained interactions; distinguish prediction at the
   shared context from identification over a target range. Do not equate
   observation support with exogenous selection.
3. A response-derived topic is a descriptive conditioning coordinate. It does
   not identify the offered choice set. Selection preferences require either
   known opportunities or explicitly limited claims about observed composition.
4. Learned evidence e=constant trivially makes gamma zero. A construction method
   needs a nondegenerate, target-relevant objective, not only invariance or
   author identification. Human criteria are imperfect but multiple forms of
   criterion evidence cannot be replaced by agreement among LLM readers.
5. We must distinguish a new theorem from an inherited identity, a useful new
   method combining existing mathematics, and a new empirical regularity.

## Candidate synthesis to improve, not merely endorse

Develop a constructive theory that learns which contextual language evidence
can support which psychological comparisons, while preserving the distinction
between choice, conditional response and state dynamics. The distinctive
candidate is a joint rule for discovering evidence AND choosing/linking the
observation design that exposes it, with a mathematically explicit change-of-
design law. Classical instruments start with defined items and response scores;
open language makes both the item/evidence boundary and elicitation part of
construction. P8-P12 are strong competitors, not simplistic straw baselines.

LLM vector interventions may discover evidence-use candidates and teach an
explicit extractor, but the final person measure must be grounded in human
criterion relations. They can cause us to rebuild GRAIN, not only decorate it.
