## Mathematics and prior work

For paired endpoints h+ and h-, define e=(h++h-)/2, d=(h+-h-)/2.
The source origin m is the mean of source e; it is never reestimated on application.
Let Ue, Uo be full numerical orthonormal bases of source e-m and d.

Two fixed candidates, both retained rather than selected on held-out results:

1. Euclidean: P=Uo Uo^T, R=I-2P.
2. Oblique: C=[Ue,Uo], P=Uo [0,I] C^+, R=I-2P.

When C has independent columns, P^2=P, P Ue=0, P Uo=Uo. Define
T(h)=m+R(h-m), so T^2=I. The unconstrained complement is fixed by the
minimum-norm extension; its action is a modelling choice, not identified truth.
If source spaces intersect numerically, record construction failure and do not
silently reduce rank or regularize. Fixed SVD tolerance is max(shape)*eps*smax.

For an application pair, with a=e-m, the exact residual identity is

    mean(||T(h+)-h-||^2, ||T(h-)-h+||^2)
      = 4*(||P a||^2 + ||(I-P)d||^2).

The first term is unwanted even/content action; the second misses ownership.
The identity operator has error 4||d||^2. Algebraic involution alone cannot prove
that T implements the textual edit. Every *application* call takes only one h;
partner vectors and design labels are used solely by the separate evaluator.

This is standard finite-group representation/projection mathematics. In the
function space of complete text orbits, the pullback Kf=f composed with g is an
involution. A finite hidden-coordinate dictionary is not automatically invariant
under K. This closure issue is the question, not a new Maschke or Koopman theorem.

- Etingof et al., [sections 3.1 and 3.6](https://math.mit.edu/~etingof/replect.pdf):
  invariant complements and invariant positive forms. Actual sections read.
- Williams/Kevrekidis/Rowley, [EDMD](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1408.4408), section 2:
  observable dictionaries and finite approximation, not a guarantee of closure.
- Feng/Steinhardt, [sections 4.1 and 5](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.17191v2):
  additive binding directions and task-dependent transport; no universal claim.
- OpenAI/math pinned adc7f...: metric/connection sections/06-matrix.tex 1--120
  re-read. Fixed paired observations and a controlled right inverse motivate the
  design. Its PDE, harmonic-frame and uniform-jet assumptions are NOT established
  for LLMs, and its theorem is NOT imported into this experiment.

## Frozen design and scope

Source: HM68 family0, all 36 focal/companion-or-neutral contexts, four x/y cells,
two g partners. 144 source pairs. Capture stage is block16, float32, eager MPS.
One query-independent body-end operator and six query-specific endpoint operators.
Two candidates for each: fourteen operations. No native logits/answers in fitting.

Existing hold-out evaluation: HM68 family1 and both HM71 forms. This is explicitly
a new operation tested on existing captures, not a new blind replication.
Freeze the source operator files BEFORE loading these application vectors.

Fresh confirmation: six cyclic directed trait pairs (all six targets), eight
cells, forward/reverse presentation: 96 new public synthetic bodies. Six queries
per body: 576 actual early-cut captures, no block17+, output logits, generation,
API, private data, or new neural training. Each behaviour has newly written
high/low descriptions, not recycled source/cold sentences. Topic variation is
part of this confirmation, not declared nuisance to be removed.
Reverse controls have identical lines/events/token bags. g partners change only
the two actor-name tokens. Body prefixes must be identical across future queries.

Bound: 576 attempted calls /600 collector seconds; no uncertain-call retry.
Resume only fully saved calls; unfinished in-flight calls require review.
