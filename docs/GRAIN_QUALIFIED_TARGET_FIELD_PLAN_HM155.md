## Scientific discriminator

HM154 found cross-wording structure in PERSONA-CONDITIONED WRITER predictions.
It did not observe a reader inferring a described person. Do not equate these
observation laws. HM10B2/HM114/HM124-146 already address ownership, wording and
whole-history interchange. This packet does not rediscover binding or tune a
reader. It connects an independently defined reader's common target response
to person-qualified evidence and tests whether two local evidence operations
compose, versus needing nonlocal context carriers.

Competing predictions:

1. Prompt-only agreement: changing action ownership at fixed whole word bag
   has no e*person / n*person effect, even when named queries differ.
2. Locally composable qualified evidence: independently swapping social and
   worry clause K/V transfers their natural effects to the appropriate target
   across the three query descriptions, and their union approximates the
   whole-history effect.
3. Distributed/contextual realization: qualified effects exist but local
   union differs from whole-history interchange or carries substantial
   cross-target/nonlinear effects. Keep this remainder; do not delete it.

Let F0,FE,FN,FEN,FW be actual endpoint fields. Exact accounting:

    FW-F0 = (FE-F0)+(FN-F0)+(FEN-FE-FN+F0)+(FW-FEN).

The third term is composition interaction; the fourth is the nonlocal
carrier remainder. Distinct from independent human trait effects. Do not
interpret any norm as a psychological-information percentage.

with fixed W. One common reference q0=softmax(W*mean(all48 natural endpoints))
is mathematical, not a human prior. Center fields under q0, compute the6x6
