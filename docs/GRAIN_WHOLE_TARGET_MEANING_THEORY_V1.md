## 3. A vector edit induces a measure, not necessarily an ordered shift

For SOURCE-author weights w_after and w_before, ordered target support s_k and
anchor a_iq, define

```text
m_uq,k = sum_i (w_after,ui-w_before,ui) 1{a_iq=s_k}; sum_k m_uq,k=0
d_uq,k = sum_{j>k} m_uq,j
delta mean_q = sum_k (s_{k+1}-s_k) d_uq,k
delta P(a_q>cut_q) = sum_{s_k>cut_q} m_uq,k.
```

The two old readings are an integral of the tail curve and its value at one
declared cut. An increasing valuation f with f(s_first)=0,f(s_last)=1 has

```text
delta E[f(a_q)] = sum_k (f(s_{k+1})-f(s_k)) d_uq,k
attainable range over ALL such f = [min_k d_uq,k, max_k d_uq,k].
```

This is finite summation by parts plus a simplex extremum, not newly invented
mathematics. Extrema describe the whole function class; they are not selected
deployment thresholds. Opposite tail signs distinguish redistribution from
one unambiguous direction under every increasing score definition.

For fixed behavior difference B_ur, the matched event contact is

```text
c_qr,k = mean_u B_ur m_uq,k
J_qr,k = sum_{j>k} c_qr,j
contact(f) = sum_k delta f_k J_qr,k.
```

Both signed areas are retained. Their difference is the continuous contact;
neither area is psychological information or noise to remove. This is an
uncentered signed contact, not Pearson r, AUC or a human causal effect.

It has a genuine probability-law representation: g=mean abs(B); weight every
pair by abs(B), use after/before as plus/minus when B>=0, swap when B<0. Then
c=g(Pplus-Pminus). If all B are zero the comparison is UNOBSERVED, rather than
an estimated neutral personality. These are behavior-oriented SOURCE laws,
