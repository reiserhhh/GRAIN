# Body-derived relational observation and the boundary-role distinction

2026-10-10 JST, HM89. Finite construction on new controlled materials, not a new
general mathematics theorem or a validated personality instrument.

## 1. What became independently executable

One body is encoded through blocks0--16; layer17 Q/K/V are captured before its
attention output or any answer. No application target question is supplied. The
following operations are NumPy/SciPy arithmetic on these frozen carriers:

    W_ij = mean_h softmax_{j<=i}(q_hi dot k_hj)
    T_ij = W_ij 1[j<i] / sum_{j<i} W_ij.

Explicit A/B name tokens are boundary roots, first body token unresolved. Denote
the three-channel boundary values by B and nonroot tokens by I. The directed
harmonic law is

    H_I = (I-T_II)^(-1) T_IB B + unresolved-deficit contribution.

T_II is strictly lower triangular, so the inverse is a finite path polynomial.
This is a candidate *observation construction*, not the actual multilayer neural
path: head averaging, removing self links (mean .139735), and repeated use of a
single-layer graph are explicit choices. Classical harmonic hitting probabilities
justify its arithmetic but do not make the probability a calibrated semantic
posterior. Actual observed dynamics or author response B_u are not inferred here.

The independent target kernel remains the HM87 frozen source seed:

    p_t^0(i|e) = softmax_i(k_i dot q_t^source).
    p_t,A(i|e) = p_t^0(i|e) H_iA / Z_t,A(e).
    phi_t(e,A) = sum_i p_t,A(i|e) <d_t,V_i(e)>.
    Theta_t(P,A) = integral phi_t(e,A) dP(e).

Thus the application query dependency of HM88 is absent in this subpath, but the
encoder, fixed source queries and source-supervised behavior covectors remain.
The generic assessment system instruction is also retained; question-free here
means no application-specific trait question, not an entirely unprompted encoder.
This is not raw-text measurement without learned neural parameters. A is the
named subject under observation, not automatically the narrator or actual author.
P is evidence composition, not a psychological truth supplied by the graph.

## 2. Event probabilities are now constructed, not copied from native queries

For each declared event G, the positive gate gives

    pi_t,A(G|e) = sum_{i in G} p_t^0(i|e) H_iA / Z_t,A(e),
    p_t,A(i|G,e) = p_t^0(i|e) H_iA / sum_{j in G} p_t^0(j|e) H_jA.

These sum and conditional laws exactly reconstruct the readout. In contrast,
unresolved absorption is retained separately; conditioning on named roots cannot
be reported as increased empirical coverage. Empty A mass is absent observation,
not a zero trait. Oracles here retain half of the shared neutral event and are
diagnostic local readings, not qualifications of every event as self behavior.

## 3. The experiment distinguishes vector connectivity and positional supply

384 fresh bodies cross six targets, two reporting frames, two event orders and
the full sixteen (x,y,g,h) cells. Person swaps keep token bags and six-name counts
exactly fixed. The two report/action forms are:

    Person R recorded that Person U performed the behavior.
    Person U performed the behavior, according to Person R.

R and U are opposite people. Global harmonic action-token allocation favors the
actual actor in all four frame/order conditions; alignment is .027616/.032704
when the reporter is first, .102919/.084440 when the actor is first. Pure
position-only transition gives -.021005/-.003319 and .087847/.033724 respectively.
Within this controlled design the vector graph adds a relational component beyond
position, without running an application personality query or fitting a reader.

This does not establish causal mediation of a model's eventual answer: the
network is stopped before that answer and this graph is not the full computation.

## 4. More paths do not automatically preserve more author contrast

Designed evidence laws Pplus/minus=(1 +/- .5*(x*g+y*h)/2)/16 have identical
single-bit marginals and person-name counts. Across the 24 target/frame/order
conditions, positive target contrast is 15/24 for cold, 23/24 for direct named
root mass, 21/24 for global harmonic, 17/24 for event-local harmonic, and 18/24
for positional harmonic. Both designed role oracles give the expected opposite
signs across all24. These are deterministic finite contrast signs, not independent
participants, significance tests, AUC or accuracy against human questionnaires.

Mean contrasts across all24 are cold .009061, direct .105533, harmonic .081500,
event-local .204962, position .051892, actor oracle .412870 and reporter oracle
-.398073, in the source vector units. The event-local large pooled mean masks
negative/reporting-frame effects, so do not select a winner by pooled amplitude.
All arms were declared before capture; source functions remain unchanged.

## 5. An exact opportunity identity explains part of the slicing gain

In the actor-first form, every behavior token occurs before the reporter name.
Inside its event the only causally available named root is the actual actor.
Therefore for event-local absorption, independently of the numerical link weights,

    actor-sign * (H_A-H_B) = 1-H_unresolved.

All384 corresponding action records satisfy this to 3.331e-16. The support
diagnostic is separately saved as opportunity_diagnostic.json, with
4032 behavior-token opportunities audited in each frame and no new model calls.
Greater local alignment is partly guaranteed by graph support, not evidence of superior role
understanding. Reporter-first places both opposite roots before behavior and does
not have this shortcut. Its local synthetic author contrast is negative for five
targets in forward order and two in reverse order. This updates the slicing
decision, not an earlier-version wholesale rejection: local cutting may remove
cross-event contamination, but also imposes a narrative opportunity condition.

## 6. Next mechanism, not a precision rescue

Actor identity and reporter identity are different relation channels. A root
probability defined only by a person's name can mix them. The next bounded
question is whether a role-valued/typed transport law can keep reporting, action
and unresolved channels distinct and preserve their interaction with behavior.
It must be source-constructed and tested on fresh reordered relation materials,
not chosen head/layer/diffusion strength on these application results. Direct
one-step success remains a positive baseline, not something to erase in favor of
a more sophisticated method. Preserve the complete event/context composition.
