## Measurement object and assumptions

Fixed offered opportunities A then B are the condition. There is no freely
chosen topic component in this design, and no claim that such choice is noise.
Z=(Z_A,Z_B), W=(W_A,W_B) are two separate paired-vector proxy bundles;
Y=(Y_A,Y_B) is an independently recorded pair of binary operational actions.
Same unit/role, frame and pairing identity must be retained.

For a simulator regime U require W independent of Z given U, and
E[1{Y=y}|U,Z]=P(Y=y|U). Neither Y_A/Y_B nor the marginal responses are
assumed independent given U. Existence/range and separation by Z are explicit
requirements; a small equation residual does not verify these assumptions.
U is an unnamed simulator regime, not an inferred human personality class.

Learn only from weighted observed records:

    K[z,w] = P_source(W=w|Z=z)
    m[z,y] = P_source(Y=y|Z=z)
    K H = m.

For a new author paired-vector law p_u, read the four behavior outcome cells

    q_u(y) = sum_w p_u(w) H[w,y]
    theta_A = q(10)+q(11), theta_B = q(01)+q(11)
    coupling_u = q(11)-theta_A*theta_B.

With K=B A, m=B C and B separating relevant regime functions, a bridge
solution obeys A H=C; a compatible p_u=pi_u A therefore gives q_u=pi_u C.
This is the finite noncausal proxy argument, not a new identification theorem.
Bridge event weights need not be probabilities. Reject inadmissible aggregate
joint probabilities explicitly, preserving unclipped values; do not rescue them.

## Fixed design and competing mechanisms

Two binary regime coordinates give four unnamed states. W coordinate accuracy
is .8 and Z .75. Behavior means are .3+.4*u_j. Its joint11 probability is
the product of those conditional means plus gamma*(2u_A-1)*(2u_B-1).
Two declared criterion protocols have gamma=.04 and -.08, with all cells
strictly positive. They have IDENTICAL vector channels and marginal behavior
means, but different joint behavior laws. This is a semantic-identification
comparison, not a sign/parameter grid or a human causal intervention.

Two source populations [ .4,.1,.2,.3 ]/[ .1,.4,.3,.2 ] use the same channels.
Three new author laws have uniform marginals and joint association kappa
[-.6,0,.6]. The evaluator knows the latent construction; the compiler/cold
scorer receive no U, latent emissions, kappa, gamma or application outcomes.

Predictions fixed before execution:

1. All authors have theta_A=theta_B=.5, but coupling=(.04+gamma)*kappa.
   Full pair reading therefore distinguishes authors whose marginal scores do
   not differ, in both source populations and both criterion protocols.
2. Product-of-author-means gives coupling0. Averaging products of calibrated
   single-event weights instead gives .04*kappa; these are DIFFERENT
   operations and both fail to recover the general joint behavioral meaning.
3. Replacing paired W by the product of its own marginal laws preserves every
   marginal and loses the association. Its cold coupling should become0.
4. The SAME paired W input should have different, potentially opposite,
   behavioral coupling under the two independently calibrated criterion laws.
   Thus geometry alone does not choose the behavioral interpretation.

Acceptance concerns new nonconstant measuring ability and these mechanism
distinctions, not Pearson/AUC/significance or more accurate event prediction.
All six author/criterion cases and both source banks retained. No method
selection, additional source sampling, inverse/rank/grid rescue or clipping.
