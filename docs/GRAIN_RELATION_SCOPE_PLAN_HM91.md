# HM91: relation scope before target-answer computation

Frozen before collecting new material. This packet changes no source reader,
head, layer, rank or strength. Its question is not classifier accuracy.

## Decision and prior-work difference

M01 evidence identity already distinguished action/intention/preference/etc.,
and HM52 found that vector steering is not evidence qualification. Those are
not rediscovered here. HM90 supplies an adequate paired body-only observer.
We now ask whether relation changes act as a common scalar/negation on its
behavior response, or require a relation-indexed response object. A second
question is whether relation/content pairing survives into author observations
when BOTH relation and complete behavioral/subject marginals are identical.

No mode is assigned a universal personality weight or deleted as noise.
An asserted report is an assertion, not verified real-world action. Negating a
report does not entail negating the described action. Preference, intention and
imagining can carry information in their own right.

## Fixed new material

Six targets, two reporting frames, six modes, all16 corners (x,y,g,h):1152 bodies.
K1/K2 carry high/low behavioral content with independent actors A/B; the opposite
person is the reporter. K3 is neutral. All bodies have the same event order.
All behavioral phrases are new relative to HM90. They are design prototypes,
not externally validated trait items. All six readers are retained on all bodies.

The inner versus outer negation pair has the same complete token bag:

    Person B does report that Person A does not [behavior].
    Person B does not report that Person A does [behavior].

The second frame uses did/claim. Actor swaps also preserve token bags. Those
controls are checked BEFORE collecting. Matching action-token strings across
modes permits an exact, separate behavioral-span account. This does not imply
that surrounding words are irrelevant: their full contribution is retained.
Frames share behavior phrases, so they test report phrasing, not independent
lexical populations. Event/subject boundaries are design-provided literal
anchors; no natural segmentation or real-author recovery is claimed.

   The tilted scopes also share their token bags, so bag-only statistics cannot
   distinguish these laws. These laws are synthetic evidence composition,

## Evidence and acceptance

Primary: frozen input/raw arrays; every frame/mode/corner/readout; coefficient
and scalar-action tables; inner/outer scope contrast; seven-term accounting;
same-marginal author-law table. Failures and opposite signs remain in place.
Focused synthetic tests and an alternate numerical verifier are required.
Numerical identities use1e-10 tolerance, not a scientific effect threshold.
No r/AUC, teacher-fidelity optimization, or statistical p-values for16 designed
corners masquerading as independent participants.

Science decision: scalar-action failure means relation cannot be represented
by one global confidence/negation multiplier in THIS observer. Scope contrast
alone does not demonstrate correct semantic understanding. If observer remains
insensitive, keep that result and do not improve prompts/heads to force success.
