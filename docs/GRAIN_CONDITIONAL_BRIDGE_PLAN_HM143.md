## Fixed design

- Every condition uses the frozen nine HM142 GRAIN15 contrast vectors. The
  generator emits those finite vectors exactly; decoding is not natural text.
- All class prevalences equal 1/3, so frequency matching cannot identify labels.
- Each condition has a fixed row-permuted observation law. Local laws, not
  anonymous labels, differ between conditions. The estimator sees frequencies.
- Local calibration authors, each pairwise same-author bridge cohort and cold
  application authors are disjoint. Same-author identity WITHIN a bridge is
  supplied by the design, never inferred from author fingerprints or used as
  a predictive feature. Pairwise cohorts share a declared population law.
- Three fixed seeds, source120000/condition/seed, bridge60000/edge/world/seed,
  cold6000/world/seed. No new encoder/reader/API/private data/training.
- Simulation parameters/labels enter generation and AFTER cold sealing,
  evaluation. They never enter local-law estimation, bridge inversion, matching
  or scoring. The engineer knows the design; no claim of blind replication.

## Population cases, all retained

1. Shared author class: each condition sees the same anonymous component,
   through its different observation law. A unique relative label frame exists.
2. Independent conditional classes: local laws identical to case1; paired
   coupling is independent. All36 reference-fixed label frames tie. Do not
   manufacture a shared factor just because the three instruments each work.
3. Soft frustrated response: a valid joint distribution on nine configurations.
   Every preferred pairwise bijection matches2/3 of its population, but their
   cycle moves every class. This is valid condition-dependent response, not
   inconsistent data. Keep soft couplings, not only hard matches.
4. Incompatible pair cohorts: AB and BC exact identity, AC exact cyclic shift.
   Each pair is a valid law with the same marginals, but no ONE common joint
   population can have all three laws. Population LP and an analytic inequality
   distinguish this from case3. This is exact only, not a fictional cold triple.
5. Higher-order alias witness: uniform independent triple versus mod3 parity
   triple have identical marginals and all pairs, but different joint laws.
   Thus case2's uninformative PAIR bridges do not prove no author structure.
