## Registered construction and signed account

For owner o and pair i,j, let X0, Xi(x), Xj(y), Xij(x,y) be actual current K/V.
All use the same coordinate and declared read F:

    Xhat = Xi + Xj - X0
    A = F(Xi) + F(Xj) - F(X0)
    B = F(Xhat)
    Y = F(Xij)
    Y - A = (B - A) + (Y - B).

B-A is reading interaction on the constructed state; Y-B is encoding
composition remainder. Xhat is a numerical counterfactual, not a natural text.
RMS components do not add; retain signed terms/cross-products and all outcomes.
Anchors are material-dependent. Do not subtract conditions or choose a winner.

For x,y,o in {-1,+1}, keep all eight Walsh coefficients and exact reconstruction.
The xy and xyo coefficients of an additive qualified-event read are zero.
A nonzero xyo coefficient locates ownership-dependent joint composition, not
a new personality axis or a pure causal ownership effect.

Author profiles use ONLY ownerA four corners. Two strictly positive laws are
(1 +/-.4xy)/4 = integer counts [7,3,3,7]/20 and [3,7,7,3]/20.
Their individual behavior marginals/current/condition/counts match. Their
joint combinations differ. Difference in mean read = .8 times its ownerA xy
coefficient; additive A difference is zero. B-owner records are contextual
controls, never evidence about authorA. Output each pair/target, not fake people.

Two actual cold subprocesses receive only A-qualified numeric observations,
counts and addresses; no raw text, source actual pair effects, truth or LLM.
They return declared event-weighted means for each law/channel. Numeric cold
scoring does not make upstream encoding independent of the LLM.
