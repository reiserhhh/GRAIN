## 1. What is now held fixed

For every bank the whole historical input bag is identical over all16 contexts.
Each occasion contains the same R and S action words at the same addresses. The
variable b_i says which person performed R, not whether R exists in the record.
References have the same20/5 count multiset but different assignments to rows.
Questions are held fixed within queried person and target. Input bag equality
does not imply equality of contextual embeddings, positions of names, or their
causal descendants. Those differences are the intended relational observations.

This addresses the HM145 count alternative: for any operator depending only on
the complete bag at a fixed question, changes of b cannot alter its reading.
A nonzero native b-dependent action excludes that bag-only operator on this
finite support, even when it is weak. It does not exclude sequential shortcuts,
table lookup, or signify human personality. We retain those distinctions.

The recorded `bag_observer` is deliberately conditioned on reference c and
queried person p: it averages b only. It is a conservative assignment-blind
comparison, permitted to know c despite its reference arrangement being invisible
to a STRICT whole-bag operator. It must not be described as the only possible
conditional expectation on the full bag over all b,c. A strict full-bag observer
is additionally constant in c. The primary assignment-RMS question fixes c,p.

## 2. Selection law is part of the evidence, not nuisance to delete

Let t_A,t_B be independent balanced signed categories before observation. Each
person's three-choice law is p(b|t,c)=(1+.6*t*c*chi(b))/8. Retain a pair if
b_B=-b_A. Integrating all unselected b_A,b_B gives

    p(retained | t_A,t_B,c)=(1-.36*t_A*t_B)/8;
    p(retained)=1/8;
    p(t_A,t_B | retained,c)=(1-.36*t_A*t_B)/4.

Both individual priors stay1/2; the selected pair categories have correlation
-.36. This correlation is fully explained by the recording design. It is not a
discovered relation between real people, compatibility, or trait influence.

After the two complete paths are observed, their preselection independent masses
factor, and restriction to the observed retained pair introduces no extra factor:

    p(H_A | b,-b,c)=.5+.3*c*chi(b);
    p(H_B | b,-b,c)=.5-.3*c*chi(b).

The known partner is not an unobserved-prior control. An observer who uses that
old HM145 interpretation would be wrong even if its digit answers were precise.
This is a constructive reason why corpus selection belongs in a measurement law.

## 3. Qualification and valuation are different operations

For a person sign p=+1(A),-1(B), define the ideal supplied-law reading

    y(b,c,p)=.5+.3*p*c*b1*b2*b3.

By contrast the simple first-action fact is f(b,c,p)=.5+.5*p*b1. Both depend on
qualified events at an identical input bag, but they use different interactions.
The mere availability of f, or a strong contextual vector carrying f, cannot
establish the joint diagnostic interaction in y. Fact extraction and valuation
require separate observations and tests.

Every actual readout has a complete32-character expansion in x=(b1,b2,b3,c,p):

    Y(x)=sum_S beta_S chi_S(x);
    beta_S=1/32 sum_x Y(x)chi_S(x).

The designated terms are beta_17 for first-event person binding and beta_31 for
joint/reference/person valuation. All other terms stay, especially beta_8
(common reference shift), beta_16 (fixed person-name preference), and any
reference effects in facts. This is not assuming all remaining terms are noise.

## 4. Preserve common and personal sectors

    C(b,c)=(Y_A+Y_B)/2; D(b,c)=(Y_A-Y_B)/2.

A common context bias can be large while D is zero. A person difference can be
large while it ignores the joint reference law. Even a nonzero b1*b2*b3*c term
in C does not qualify the target judgment to one person. Therefore preserve C
and D separately rather than maximize an aggregate intervention cosine.

Global actor renaming changes b->-b and p->-p, while c is unchanged. The ideal
target/fact maps satisfy Y(b,c,p)=Y(-b,c,-p). Native failure of this equivariance
quantifies name/position asymmetry in this finite design, not a psychological
invariance axiom automatically applicable to all natural texts.

## 5. Relating qualified configurations to author comparison

For a positive author distribution P_u(b), a declared operator induces

    S_q(P_u,c,p)=sum_b P_u(b)Y_q(b,c,p).

This is linear in the evidence distribution, not in individual events. If
P_+=(1+eta*chi(b))/8 and P_-=(1-eta*chi(b))/8, their lower moments agree but

    S_q(P_+,c,p)-S_q(P_-,c,p)
       =2*eta*(beta_7+c*beta_15+p*beta_23+c*p*beta_31).

All four terms are retained: unqualified joint response, reference-conditioned
joint response, person-qualified joint response, and qualified diagnostic action.
The identity is exact on the finite support; these virtual accounts are not new
empirical authors or a demonstration of natural trait validity.

GRAIN's source joint configurations (HM144) need an observation that retains
relations, plus a source-supported target functional. Configurational presence
does not orient an anonymous factor toward a named psychological construct.
Conversely, supplying a named query need not implement the correct likelihood.
The explicit law here separates those two missing connections for examination.

For a vector implementation, let h_(r,j) be the two observed action states in
occasion r, and I_(u,r,j) the registered person/event incidence. A qualified
three-view observation is an outer product of the three PERSON-selected states,
not an outer product of whole-record pooled states:

    T_u = tensor_(r=1..3) [sum_j I_(u,r,j)*phi_r(h_(r,j))].

In the present finite design, scalar signed actions make T_A=chi(b) and
T_B=-chi(b), while pooled action sums are zero on every occasion. This supplies
a concrete qualification-to-configuration interface, not a method for finding
unknown natural actors. The observations I are stipulated text-design metadata
here. If I is uncertain and correlated across occasions, the correct object is
E_I[T_u], not necessarily the tensor product of E_I[each selected state].
Learning an adequate natural incidence law remains part of the observation
problem. A contextual embedding that changes with actor assignment may retain
such a relation, but its presence alone does not supply the diagnostic tensor.

## 6. Actual intervention, not an invented vector inverse

Capture each real history's K/V across all28 frozen blocks. Swap action K, V or
KV from globally exchanged b; reference rows and recipient body stay native.
The natural donor's reference states may also carry b through earlier tokens.
Leaving them unchanged means this action-only swap is a specific direct-read
operation, not a guaranteed complete causal mediation or natural text rewrite.
Reference KV swaps reverse c at fixed b, with action/body still recipient.

Compare effects on full Y and on D separately. High fidelity transporting C is
not evidence of faithful person valuation. Low action-only fidelity can reflect
the untouched causal descendants and must not be called absence of binding.
No layer/head/prompt/dose fit is used to improve either comparison.

## 7. Theory ancestry and what an advance would mean

Finite characters, conditional probabilities, fiber factorization and
equivariance are mature mathematics. Feng/Russell/Steinhardt2024
(https://arxiv.org/html/2406.19501v1, sections3-5) motivates distinguishing
bound propositions from final responses. Feng/Steinhardt2023/2024
(https://arxiv.org/html/2310.17191v2) studies binding vectors/interchanges.
Heimersheim/Nanda2024 (https://arxiv.org/html/2404.15255v1) motivates patch scope.
No Hessian-trained binding subspace is imported or fitted by HM146.

OpenAI/math adc7f124 Universal-Tensor-Squares preliminaries dual-contraction
was reread for component-sensitive tests; its Specht/Schur-Weyl theorem has
different objects and assumptions and is not a personality theorem.

The possible advance is an operational construction requirement: a text-based
measurement should distinguish qualified joint evidence from common context
response and should specify the selection/observation law used for diagnosis.
The numerical experiment will answer which of those operations is present in
this frozen model/design. It will not promote an anonymous target or a tool's
accuracy into complete psychological theory. Executed findings will be appended.
