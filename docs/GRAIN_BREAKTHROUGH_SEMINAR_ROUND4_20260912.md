# Round 4: final goal-first judgment

Read only; at most 500 English words. No experiments or commands. This is the
last short round unless a genuinely blocking contradiction appears.

Codex checked the further novelty question from your round3. See source dossier
M11, P14, P15: selective/adaptive matrix sampling has theory (Wang & Singh 2018),
deep IRT already uses question semantics, and multi-agent construct-specific
item generation exists. Your mask certificate therefore cannot alone carry the
whole project's scientific target. The closest prior work is stronger than just
fixed-item Rasch or pooled regression. Full priority claims remain open.

I propose the central target as follows:

Discover and operationalize a response mechanism that explains how psychological
differences appear in open language under opportunity, choice and state, then
learn the evidence units and observation designs that expose those differences.
The concrete new capability sought is not merely higher Big5 r: distinguish
target-relevant differences from changes in opportunity/measurement channel,
preserve meaningful person-context interactions, and use this distinction to
construct a useful measure in another declared setting. GRAIN itself, slicing,
factors and vector-path use are revisable. LLMs propose and probe evidence;
human criterion/behavior relations decide psychological relevance.

Primary mechanism discovery should precede a large mask-certification program.
Compare pooled evidence, conventional context/choice/interaction models, learned
evidence alone, and learned evidence jointly with design. The useful finding is
a reproducible target-linked response structure with a prospective prediction
about which evidence or opportunity changes reveal it better. A mask can help
choose that test; merely counting non-identifiable pairs is not the endpoint.

Mathematical caution: your round3 'nonignorable selection biases estimation, not
the certificate' holds only for a certificate conditional on a correct,
fixed cell-value model. With unobserved within-cell states affecting selection,
the observed cell expectation may differ from the target expectation. There is
no identification of that target from the mask alone. State this scope.

the surviving mechanism. Linking design and minimal elicitation become central
only if they change that mechanism's attainable measurement. No default full
new experiment matrix; MEPS remains evaluation-only and may not tune anything.
