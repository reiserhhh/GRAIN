# GRAIN conditional multi-construct reading: finite HM46 findings

2026-10-09. This is a controlled frozen-reader result, not a human personality
factor model, a new general theorem, or a substitute for the original scope.

## Object

Let F_q(x;o,a) be a native reader's digit-expected response to query q about
an account x, with ownership o and actuality a. For each controlled content c:

    D_z[c,q] = F_q(x_c^+;z) - F_q(x_c^-;z),  z=(o,a).

The complete 6x6 signed matrix is retained for each example family and wording.
Polarity/ownership/actuality form a balanced experimental Z2^3 index set. Its
character expansion is exact on that finite design, not a linear group action
on natural-language representations. No interaction is defined away as noise.

## Shared response and conditional remainder

For a reference A and conditional B, the exact Frobenius projection is:

    gamma_z = <A,B>_F / ||A||_F^2
    R_z = B - gamma_z A
    <A,R_z>_F = 0.

This is standard projection algebra. Its scientific use is to distinguish
common response-strength changes from retained target-by-content changes.
Form-mean source residual shares are1.39-1.96%; application2.35-3.96%.
Most response energy is shared; this is not a claim of complete reorganization.
Separate wording application residuals reach8.26%, so averaging is not a
substitute for preserving the individual response tables.

If B=diag(r) A diag(t), all cross-product quartics vanish:

    B_ij B_kl A_il A_kj = B_il B_kj A_ij A_kl.

With every A entry nonzero, these restrictions characterize a rank-at-most-one
ratio matrix; with zero support they can become vacuous. Division-free norms
avoid unstable elementwise ratios. They are descriptive algebraic restrictions,
not probability independence tests or psychological factor-analysis tetrads.
See GRAIN_CONDITIONAL_ROUTING_INVARIANTS_V1.md for proof and source attribution.

of squared response energy (source/application); singular-entropy effective

All six matched content/query contrasts are positive in both example families.
Neither a diagonal-only law nor one shared evaluative direction is sufficient.

The writer query still responds strongly to colleague accounts. Application
diagonal means self/actual.53812 and other/actual.52128 show that semantic
relatedness and author-evidence qualification are not interchangeable. Do not
promote this reader's scores to human truth. Imagined content can be informative
about expressed choices, but differs from observations of actual actions.

At block16, property-only and body-endpoint-only swaps carry small parts of the
native ownership/actuality change. Actuality property effects exceed random
norm controls in RMS but not directional agreement; endpoint effects have more
directional agreement but small amplitude. No unique property/endpoint storage
claim follows. Do not turn a patch norm or slope into an information percentage.

## Connection to prior work and GRAIN

Big Five cross-trait steering effects already have precedent:
https://arxiv.org/html/2602.15847v1. That generation-side study does not establish
an inverse reading pathway. Our current object is conditional evidence reading,
with ownership and actuality crossed and local carrier interventions retained.
Overlap/coupling alone is not a novelty claim. Direction orthogonality and
output independence are different objects; forcing an orthogonal coordinate
system is not a remedy for conditional reading.

Counterfactual concept geometry: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/park24c.html.
Algebraic rank/minor characterization: https://math.berkeley.edu/~bernd/owl.pdf.
QK feature interaction: https://transformer-circuits.pub/2025/attention-qk/index.html.
Those mathematical assumptions are not automatically assumptions of this reader.

GRAIN's event/unit/author/condition ledger remains upstream of aggregation.
The observation channel should be written F_q(x;subject,actuality,condition),
not an unqualified content score times a universal eligibility weight. Shared
response modes and conditional remainders can both be retained. Anonymous
GRAIN factors require actual target connections rather than naming them from
the largest response matrix loading. No private data, API, training or publication.
