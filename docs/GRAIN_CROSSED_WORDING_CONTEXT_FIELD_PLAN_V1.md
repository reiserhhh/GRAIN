and the local derivative as an evaluation reference. All roles/queries/arms and
every cell remain visible, including reversed signs and tiny native amplitudes.
Do not interpret negligible numeric predictions' signs as useful directionality.

Within each fully crossed split use the standard double-centered decomposition

    lambda[c,p,q]=mean[q]+context[c,q]+wording[p,q]+interaction[c,p,q],

with zero-sum context/wording constraints. Decompose both covectors and actual
action scalars; keep their different units. Same-field covariance sharing across
queries is checked on paired cell IDs, not duplicated reports or author labels.
The decomposition is a descriptive finite design, not an estimated psychological
random-effects model. Do not use held context means to manufacture cold predictions.
