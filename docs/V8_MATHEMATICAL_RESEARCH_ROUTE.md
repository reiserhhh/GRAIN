## 1. Scientific target

GRAIN is a general methodology foundation for constructing and testing
natural-language measurements. It is not itself a personality scale, symptom
scale, diagnostic system, or clinical instrument.

The V8 target is to identify and estimate reproducible author-relative
structure in text while preserving four separations:

1. observed text events versus latent author process;
2. author process versus measurement error;
3. technical author structure versus psychological interpretation;
4. deterministic measurement versus LLM explanation.

The numerical channel remains deterministic. A frozen LLM may convert
registered evidence into constrained human-readable explanations, but it may
not invent scores, constructs, diagnoses, validity, or certainty.

## 3. Why M3 belongs inside V8

M3 is not a separate product line. It is V8's micro-to-meso existence and
identification layer.

Earlier V8 work asked whether author-relative positions, response operators,
incidence structures, routing kernels, and residual channels can be measured
under declared designs. M3 asks the complementary question:

> Can different microscopic text-path laws generate stable author structure
> that ordinary means, low-order moments, or short-lag covariance cannot
> identify, while a fixed generator-blind measurement battery recovers it and
> refuses exact aliases?

M3-V4 operationalizes three anonymous object families:

- distribution geometry beyond degree-four moments;
- nonlinear response operators beyond a joint polynomial nuisance space;
- path dynamics beyond low-order covariance, including dwell hazard,
  probability current, and nonlinear variance dynamics.

These are candidate mechanisms for the middle of the V8 map. They are not
named factors and are not evidence that any recovered coordinate is a
personality trait.

\[

\[
\mathfrak O_{ur}^{K}
