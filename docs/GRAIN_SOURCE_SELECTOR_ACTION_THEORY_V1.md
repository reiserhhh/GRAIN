## Inherited theory and the empirical contribution

Evidence removal follows [ERASER](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.408/).
Model attribution versus human meaning follows
[Jacovi and Goldberg](https://aclanthology.org/2021.tacl-1.18/).
Projection and crossed designs use mature finite algebra/statistics.
OpenAI/math signed-isotypic support inspires retaining distinct sectors;
its Specht modules and density/moment bounds do not transfer here.

Two recent neighboring studies narrow the novelty boundary:

- [Qin et al.2026](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/qin26h.html) traces MODEL
  persona expression using latent persona vectors. Its abstract distinguishes
  single-token attribution from multi-token personality expression. Our
  digit endpoint is a judgment probe, not a holistic behavior measure. Only
  its abstract was checked here, not a full-paper/circuit audit.
- [Yang and Li2026](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1930314/full)
  organizes cue provenance and trait evidence for human prediction. Its
  supervised calibration differs from direct scoring, so improved performance
  is not attributed solely to cue organization. The abstract and Methods2.4-2.5
  were checked, not the full paper or their implementation.

Our question: can a criterion-linked SOURCE instrument prospectively choose
real text operations that differentiate reader judgments on the SAME events?
This depends on the actual matrix, not ownership of persona vectors, cue
integration, causal tracing, deletion or advanced mathematics.
