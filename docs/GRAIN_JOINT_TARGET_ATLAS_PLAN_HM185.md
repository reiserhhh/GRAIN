## Scientific decisions

- Reproducible joint-order evidence above the primary exchange reference:
  retain a multi-target conditional observation atlas rather than only five
  independent global directions; investigate its evidence/opportunity origins.
- Recurrence within authors but weak cross-author joint-order structure:
  do not convert stable author expression into a transferable personality atlas.
- Broad failure: retain numerical cold capability, but change the observation
  family; no search for favorable folds,orders,reference weights or N accuracy.
