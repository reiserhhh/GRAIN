## The user's enduring vision, not a replacement goal

The ONE endpoint is a foundational theory/method paper for **constructing
context-specific psychological measures from natural language**. A future
researcher should be able to build a measure of personality, behavioral tendency
or occasion-specific state for their setting using this methodology. A counseling
session or support-group discussion is a motivating eventual setting, not an
instruction to build or clinically validate an instrument today.

GRAIN must remain psychologically meaningful and explainable, not merely author
identification, text categorization, or an imitation of an LLM's answers. People
should be scoreable relative to a declared population/condition reference.
Anonymous factors and distributed or dynamic configurations are welcome; a
particular number of factors, unique rotation, or Big5 taxonomy is not required.
Independent psychological/behavioral links can establish meaning after discovery.

The user rejected plans that completed a program pipeline, listed endless caveats,
or polished local neural precision while the domain-level theory remained absent.
They want discovery -> development -> focused verification, not perfect defenses
before finding an effect. Useful associations are real progress at their level.
Preserve correct numbers, author linkage, privacy and fitting/evaluation separation.

Corpus-independent construction principles should guide local construction and
independent evaluation. Do not write universal theory backwards from PANDORA's
accidental correlations. Conversely, local parameter estimation and evidence-based
theory revision are legitimate. One universal recipe is not one universal set of
loadings, nor a guarantee that every arbitrary text reveals every attribute.

## Inherited ideas to integrate rather than erase

- MCD: retain condition selection and conditional responses; context is not
  automatically noise. Free choice and fixed-condition evidence are distinct.
- GRAIN/4W: where/choice, what/expression, who/group-relative configuration,
  when/dynamics. These are dependent organizing views, not four independent factors.
- Slice and no-slice operators are alternatives; a token is not automatically an
  item, cluster not automatically a construct, residual not automatically noise.
- Stable, dynamic and shared objects may be functions/operators rather than named
  scalar axes. A single occasion can be a valid state target; people need not
  repeat an identical topic daily or stay psychologically constant.
- LLM mechanism research: query-conditioned vectors can help construct evidence
  functions. Numeric scoring and explanatory rendering are distinct. Extracting
  an LLM computation is an implementation contribution, not proof about its author.
- The method must tell future builders what to fix, what to estimate locally,
  what to keep as meaningful variation, and how to interpret the result.

## Real data opportunities, including positive evidence

PANDORA provides free multi-topic texts and Big5/MBTI labels. Essays supplies a
fixed writing setting from different people, not same-person cross-context data.
X supplies domain-constrained natural language/time patterns, but matched
psychological or actual trading-behavior criteria have not been verified.

MEPS has46 participants, fixed MEPS answers and free AI conversation, with
processed relational/session criteria and4 phases of adapted mood composites.
Current permission is local external evaluation only, NOT training/calibration.
Assistant replies are context, not the author's own expression; fictional MEPS
characters are not the participant's state. Study1/raw survey/coding folders are
not silently in scope. Read public aggregate reports only.
