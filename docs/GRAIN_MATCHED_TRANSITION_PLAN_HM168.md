## Mathematical ancestry and why this changes the mainline

Pearl/Bareinboim's transportability framework distinguishes a shared intervention
from invariance of its causal mechanism across domains; matching is necessary
to our declared operation, not sufficient for transporting arbitrary outcomes.
https://ftp.cs.ucla.edu/pub/stat_ser/r404-reprint.pdf

Geiger et al.2025v4 distinguish state maps from maps of interventions. Here the
operation is a history-message replacement, not an identified internal circuit.
https://arxiv.org/html/2301.04709v4

Song et al.2009 already supplies conditional observation operators. We use only
finite cross-moments, not its inverse/range-dependent conditional expectation.
https://alex.smola.org/papers/2009/SonHuaSmoetal09.pdf

OpenAI/math pinned adc7f124 Thorp section05 fixes an array before conditional
exposure and separately addresses repeat labels. Inspiration: retain SAME
evidence units while changing the observation law. Independent permutation,
entropy and spectral hypotheses are not transferred to language.

The candidate contribution is an executable GRAIN source-to-action interface
and an informative transport test. It is not a new algebra theorem, a discovery
that prompts affect models, or a completed human personality measurement law.
If it fails, the next decision must change the construction object, not add
