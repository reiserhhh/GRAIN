## 2. Executable source evidence function

For source events e=1,...,N, vectors X_e, measured action profiles A_e, and source
means mu and abar, define a fixed Euclidean cross-moment witness

    C = (1/N) sum_e (A_e-abar) tensor (X_e-mu),
    T(v) = C(v-mu).

This mature statistic is NOT an identified conditional mean operator. For an
application pair displacement d, its source evidence expansion is exact:

    lambda_e(d) = <X_e-mu,d>/N,
    sum_e lambda_e(d) = 0,
    T(X_high)-T(X_low) = sum_e lambda_e(d)(A_e-abar).

Thus a numeric-only program can expose which source event/condition contributions
produce its output. The weights are signed and can cancel; they are not attention,
posterior probabilities, psychological information fractions or causal mediation.
Using A_e-abar fixes the response-origin gauge of EACH source-group account.
Using raw A_e preserves only the TOTAL, not the meaning of every group contribution.

For production group g, with source means Xbar_g/Abar_g:

    L_g = sum_{e in g} lambda_e(d)(A_e-abar)
    W_g = sum_{e in g}<X_e-Xbar_g,d>(A_e-Abar_g)/N
    B_g = (n_g/N)<Xbar_g-mu,d>(Abar_g-abar)
    L_g = W_g+B_g; T(d)=sum_g L_g.

Same denominatorN and all signed terms are essential. These are observation
accounts, not permissions to remove B_g: condition selection may be author signal.

## 3. Dependence cannot supply orientation or transport by itself

Euclidean distance covariance uses double-centered pair distances. Its population
zero characterization requires the conditions in Szekely/Rizzo/Bakirov2007; the
small finite sample V statistic is biased. Production-stratified V statistics
and within-block Monte Carlo tail ranks are only finite, conditional diagnostics.

There is a fundamental orientation ambiguity:

    dCov(X,Y)=dCov(X,-Y), but Cov(X,-Y)=-Cov(X,Y).

More generally orthogonal changes of the response profile preserve its distances.
Therefore unsigned dependence does not locate high/low personality polarity or
identify a source-to-application signed law. A positive dependence witness plus
failed source covariance transport can arise from nonlinear dependence, changed
conditional law, a deficient source family, or their combination. It does NOT
uniquely establish a nonlinear psychological mechanism.

The finite x in {-2,-1,0,1,2}, y=x^2 example has covariance0 and positive distance
covariance. A full Cartesian independent x/y law has distance covariance0.
These are known mathematical controls, not new discoveries about personality.
