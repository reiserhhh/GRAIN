## Decision and prior inventory

HM68 already established content/ownership-dependent interaction. HM89 examined
event-local harmonic graph support, not an actual multilayer cross-entry cut.
HM101 cut the entire history's direct/current and relay routes. HM110 showed that
single readouts and summed single K/V states miss qualified joint observations.
This packet instead distinguishes *within-history event contextualization* from
*later aggregation*, keeping current-token access to all evidence open in all arms.
Do not report another demonstration of generic nonlinearity as the novelty.

For six historical entries E1..E6, shared prefix P, common owner declaration and
bridge R, and fixed current carrier C, cross two permissions at blocks0--16:

* c=1: an entry can read previous entries; c=0: each entry reads only P and itself.
* r=1: R can read the entries; r=0: R reads P and itself, not entry tokens.
* C always reads all causally available, nonpadding tokens, including E and R.

An entry is a designed observation unit, not an automatically parsed natural event.
R includes the owner declaration/header, not just neutral words. Ownership remains
visible to C even when its declaration cannot read E. The masks intervene on support
and softmax normalization, not uniquely on a semantic or native causal path.

## Positive construction and scientific alternatives

With c=r=0, causal support guarantees at every layer:

    E_j^{l+1} = T_l(E_j^l; P^l, E_j^l)
    R^{l+1}   = T_l(R^l; P^l, R^l)
    C^{l+1}   = T_l(C^l; P^l, E_1^l,...,E_6^l,R^l,C^l).

This is an independently encoded event bank with a recursive joint collector,
not addition of final event vectors. Fixed model parameters still implement T_l;
it is not raw-text prediction without a neural encoder or a distilled operator.

If joint author contrast survives 00, cross-entry writing is not necessary under
this intervention; retain a nonlinear recursive collector in the construction.
If it vanishes, contextual event/relay operations are needed. If signed routes
cancel or differ between banks, retain their separate effects instead of a single
causal percentage or a universal coefficient. No post-result threshold search.
