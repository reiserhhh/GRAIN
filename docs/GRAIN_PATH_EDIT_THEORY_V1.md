## 3. Noncommuting observation/edit operations

Let O(X) be the unnormalized identity-addressed triple measure of a history.
Let D be an event deletion and Restrict_D retain only old triples with no
deleted event. Then

    O(D X) = Restrict_D O(X) + B_D(X),

where B_D contains splice-created triples. Equality O(DX)=Restrict_DO(X)
requires B_D empty. These are ordinary finite measure/set identities, not a
new noncommutative algebra theorem. Calling both operations "removal" hides
the distinction. Which is appropriate depends on the requested measurement.

For m=n-2 old and m'=n-4-2 edited triples, let R,D,B be retained, destroyed,
and newly spliced identity triples. In SOURCE excess-field units,

    S_blind(DX)-S(X) = -sum_D v_11/m

    S_gap(DX)-S_blind(DX) = sum_B (v_d-v_11)/m'.

Thus removal, new adjacency, normalization, and law substitution are separate
signed accounts. Their cross terms must be retained in any norm/variance
calculation. Their RMS values do not add and are not psychological fractions.

No contribution is automatically noise or deleted. An observation-preserving
restriction with holes is an alternative ledger, not an actual full text whose
