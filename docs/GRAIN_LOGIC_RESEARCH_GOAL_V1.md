## Pending observation requirements, not reasons to halt local discovery

- Separating text expression, questionnaire reporting, and independent real
  action requires an additional declared action channel; text+self-report alone
  do not identify it. Existing MEPS participation is real conversational behavior,
  not automatically an everyday-action scale.
- Stable person/state psychological naming across opportunities requires a
  declared construct, clock and appropriate external evidence. Current technical
  factor and query functions may still be developed without completing this.
- A human mechanism and an LLM's mechanism may share useful formal descriptions;
  matching model responses does not establish that the human used that algorithm.
- Complete identification across all languages, model families and populations
  is not a finite goal. Maintain precise local transfer examples instead.
