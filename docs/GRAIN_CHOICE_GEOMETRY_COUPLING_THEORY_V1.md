## 3. Group difference and complete signed contraction

Within each declared group, let

    q = mean(s)
    A = E[a|s=1]
    G = E[M|s=1]
    L = E[(a-A)(M-G)|s=1]
    f = A G + L
    E[D] = q f.

A group without any supported geometry fails construction instead of receiving
an invented reference. For high-minus-low target differences, midpoints are
denoted by bars. Ordinary finite product identities give

    F = Delta(qf)
      = Delta q * bar f
        + bar q * Delta A * bar G
        + bar q * bar A * Delta G
        + bar q * Delta L.

These four fields are coverage, choice, geometry and coupling. They are
signed matrix accounts, not nonnegative personality-information shares.

The product-marginal comparison is

    P = Delta(q A G)
    K = F-P = Delta(q L).

K is NOT just the fourth field: coverage also contains the coupling through f.
For two author folds, averaging all four view pairings gives

    J(F) = <F_0,F_1>
    J(F)-J(P) = J(K) + <P_0,K_1> + <K_0,P_1>.

Here each bracket includes the declared view/target averaging/sum. All16
oriented cross terms of the four fields are retained. In this sample,
J(K)=1.9348549e-8 and the two product/coupling cross terms together are
+3.0825666e-9, giving the registered +2.2431115e-8 gap. Higher total contraction
is not equivalent to higher normalized coherence: full=.10303, product=.14677.
These are geometric statistics, not Pearson r, AUC or practical scale accuracy.
