## 1. The theoretical advance is a separation, not a higher score

Encode one body using the fixed neural prefix, without an application personality
query. Apply six source-frozen numerical query kernels to the same body KV. This
is now an operational observation, not a teacher-score approximation. The encoder
still uses local Qwen blocks0-16; neither a neural-free raw-text measurement nor
the historical complete token-slice GRAIN factor bank is claimed.

On 192 fresh controlled bodies in two sentence orders, all six matched target
queries retain positive behaviour/actor yg composition contrasts under the frozen
kernel. In contrast, matched target selectivity is positive for four of six in
each order, with different signs for N/A across order. Actual native contextual
queries on 96 forward bodies yield positive selectivity for all six.

Thus actor-sensitive evidence composition can survive without a perfectly
target-selective kernel. Semantic response, relational evidence and target
selectivity must be retained as different observations. A positive author-law
contrast is not proof of human personality or uniquely qualified self evidence.

## 2. Constructible cold observation

Let H(e) be the once-encoded body carrier; q0_t is a frozen neutral raw query.
R_p places it at the declared virtual query position by standard RoPE.

    a_ti(e) = softmax_i(<K_i(e), R_p(e) q0_t>/sqrt(d))
    r0_t(e) = sum_i a_ti(e) V_i(e)
    phi0_st(e) = <d_s, r0_t(e)>
    Theta0_st(P_u) = integral phi0_st(e) dP_u(e).

Indices i include only body tokens. Dividing the full attention contribution by
body mass cancels non-body keys from its denominator, but does not undo prior
system/context influence already encoded in H(e). Whole-prefix body allocation
remains a separate observation from HM86, not silently fixed to one here.

The cold process reads only a sealed numeric bundle and body carriers. No raw
text, actor/polarity truth, native queries, weights, torch or transformers are
available there. All targets, directions and twelve heads are computed. The
source directions use designed behaviour contrasts; no unsupervised factors or
new personality labels have been fitted.

## 3. Contextual query is a separate computational function

For the native raw query q_t(H), write q_t(H)=q0_t+Deltaq_t(H). In recipient RoPE
coordinates the native observation is:

    phi_st(e) = <d_s, r(K(e),V(e),R_p(e)q_t(H(e)))>
    delta_st(e) = phi_st(e)-phi0_st(e)
    Theta_st(P_u) = Theta0_st(P_u) + integral delta_st(e) dP_u(e).

The last identity does not identify Deltaq from body vectors or eliminate its
neural computation. It defines the remaining mechanism explicitly. Native query
movement can add or oppose selectivity; it is not universally beneficial noise
reduction. Nor is it automatically a psychological state of the author.

For each finite behaviour edit, cross raw high/low queries with high/low body
carriers, moving each query to the recipient endpoint. Define F_ab accordingly.

    D_body = (F_hh-F_lh+F_hl-F_ll)/2
    D_query = (F_hh-F_hl+F_lh-F_ll)/2
    D_native = D_body+D_query.

This symmetric product/polarization convention preserves interaction. It is not
a unique causal-path attribution or evidence of final-answer mediation.

## 4. Relation to author measurement and order

Two synthetic laws P+/-=(1+/-epsilon*yg)/16, epsilon=.5, have equal separate
x/y/g/h marginals but different behaviour/actor associations. Under the cold
matched kernel their contrasts are positive for all six targets in both orders.
They remain positive on h=-g equal-name-count subsets; this restriction changes
two actor assignments jointly, not a pure ownership intervention.

Reversing sentence order changes the causal contextualization of H(e). It is not
an orthogonal coordinate transformation. Both orders are retained, not selected
to maximize scores. Relation contrast direction survives in this experiment;
amplitudes and target selectivity do not become order-invariant. The correct
measurement object retains order/condition with the observation law and P_u.

No natural authors' P_u, personal response B_u, psychological validity or dynamic
trait has been estimated here. A is the queried target person, not a verified
account author. These boundaries do not erase the existence of the local
relational observation now made independently executable.
