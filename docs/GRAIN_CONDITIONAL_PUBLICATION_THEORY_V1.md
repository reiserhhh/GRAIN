## 7. Next information object,not another precision campaign

J records only conditional FIRST moments of three outputs. Equal means do
not determine dispersion,tail or joint-response dependence. Park/Muandet's
conditional mean embedding becomes distribution-sensitive only when the
output kernel is sufficiently informative;the current linear output map is
not characteristic. A next construction should ask about distribution-valued
publication fields,with a fixed counterworld whose means match but laws differ.

bandwidth,metric or coordinate to make a failed shape correlation positive.
