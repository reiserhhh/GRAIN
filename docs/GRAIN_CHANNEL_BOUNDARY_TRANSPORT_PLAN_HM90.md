## Finite mathematics and its ancestry

For each head h, let T_h be its strict-causal normalized QK graph. H_h gives
A/B/unresolved boundary absorption. This is a direct-sum directed harmonic
problem: edge blocks diag(T_1ij,...,T_12ij), not a smooth unitary connection,
not a symmetric sheaf Laplacian, and not actual multilayer neural attribution.

Let barT=mean_h T_h, barH=mean_h H_h, H0=harmonic(barT). On the interior,

    (I-barT_II)(barH_I-H0_I)
      = mean_h[(T_h-barT)(H_h-barH)]_I.

Identical boundary/deficit conditions are required. It is a standard finite
linear identity, not a new general theorem. Separately retain the difference
between normalize(mean raw head weights) and mean(normalized head weights).

For source kernel p_thi, body value projection c_thi and subject gate H_hiA:

    m_th = sum_i p_thi H_hiA; n_th = sum_i p_thi H_hiA c_thi.
    phi_t = sum_h n_th/m_th.

Replacing H_hiA by barH_iA preserves only the marginal law. For paired versus
averaged-law numerator/mass, the exact conditional difference is

    phi_pair-phi_mean = sum_h[(n_pair-n_mean)/m_pair
                         -(m_pair-m_mean)*phi_mean,h/m_pair].

Cycle the gate head index through ALL12 offsets, without moving the frozen
kernel/value/direction. The average unnormalized numerator and mass equals
the average-law arm exactly. Averaging conditional scores does not have that
identity; report its normalization remainder, not a free coupling advantage.
Synchronous permutation of gate/kernel/value is a coordinate relabeling and
must preserve the observation. A gate-only permutation changes pairing.
