o=+1 means A acted, o=-1 means B acted. Conditions always occur for both people,
in opposite assignments; c is A's condition. All margins and signs are retained.

behavior. Supplied unknown ownership/condition is unavailable, not a zero score.

On the A-owned binary behavior support, define actual control quantities

    m_c=(K(c,+,A)+K(c,-,A))/2,
    g_c=(K(c,+,A)-K(c,-,A))/2.

For numeric evidence profiles P_u(b|c)=(1+b(a_u+B_u*c))/2, the observation is

    y_uc=m_c+g_c*(a_u+B_u*c).

fixed targets into a design D with rows [g_c,c*g_c]. A source-frozen Moore-Penrose
