## 1. The measured function includes its observation procedure

For one author's frozen evidence blocks S and T, a target name n and an
operational definition c, let Y(r,d,n,c,x) be the fixed digit log odds under
route availability r,d. The body encoder is held causally native; r controls
body access by postbody/prefinal rows, and d controls final direct body access.

    delta(r,d,n,c) = Y(r,d,n,c,S)-Y(r,d,n,c,T)
    M(r,d,n) = delta(r,d,n,cooperation)-delta(r,d,n,exploration)

This M is a definition-selective evidence ACTION, not an author's absolute
trait score. Its values do not have Pearson/AUC units. Its criterion excludes
the inference that a score increase itself improves measurement.

HM218 qualified evidence before native observation; HM221 separated names
from operational definitions. HM222 asks which computation a reader of that
qualified evidence needs. None of the three stages alone supplies a human
trait interpretation or a universally correct score.

## 2. The finite operator accounts

On the two-route Boolean support, the exact incidence-algebra expansion is

    M(r,d,n) = M00(n) + r*a(n) + d*b(n) + r*d*j(n),
    a = M10-M00, b = M01-M00,
    j = M11-M10-M01+M00.

Consequently native-minus-closed equals a+b+j. The increments a,b are effects
when the other route is unavailable. The effect of adding relay when direct
is available is a+j, not a; adding direct when relay is available is b+j.
Keep all signed terms. Neither abs-values, percentages nor equal splitting
of j establishes a psychological information allocation.

The registered primary is the author mean of j under anonymous Scale K.
Because M already contrasts BOTH evidence blocks and definitions, j tests a
definition-selective evidence/route interaction, not merely a generic
two-route output interaction. Full endpoint and contract accounts are retained
so that generic changes are not mistaken for criterion-specific changes.

This is standard finite Moebius/factorial algebra. It is not a new theorem.
Nonzero j distinguishes strict additivity on this graph support; it does not
identify a unique neural operation, multiplication unit, or semantic cause.

## 3. Vector-valued accounts and their output reading

Apply the SAME signed accounts to the actual final normalized vector h. Let
w be the fixed mean high-digit head minus mean low-digit head. Then

    Y = w.h + curvature,
    j_Y = w.j_h + j_curvature.

The curvature is the difference of high/low log-mean-exp residuals. Retaining
it prevents a final linear head from being mistaken for a globally linear
inference procedure. Normalization, attention renormalization, residual
recursion and MLPs are part of each intervened h. There is no assumed globally
linear transport and no percentage allocation of personality signal.

## 4. Content-blind references retain opportunity

Closing B->R and B->final makes all postbody states independent of BODY TOKEN
IDENTITIES at fixed length, prefix, contract and positions. The proof is
causal-layer induction: row-local residual/MLP updates cannot restore a missing
body edge, and every remaining predecessor is body-blind. It does not say
that differing body lengths have no effect. M00 is retained, not reset to zero.

The two closed token-shift controls and tiny network test are checks of this
architectural statement. They are not natural author observations or evidence
that discarded text is psychological noise. Graph cutting itself changes
softmax denominators and recursive states; it is not a naturally occurring
text edit or an established latent-independent degradation kernel.

## 5. What this can add to the mainline

A direct-only criterion action would permit a reader interface with a
body-blind relay bank indexed by contract, length and common prefix. A relay-only
action would permit a criterion-conditioned evidence carrier without final
direct access. A material interaction would require a joint construction;
adding two separately measured readers would not recover the native account.
These statements concern this finite interface, not universally minimal
circuits or absence of backup mechanisms outside it.

The next construction decision should follow those accounts. It must not
choose the largest score or tune cuts to recover a higher correlation. Body
encoding is still neural, and a relay bank alone is not an LLM-free scorer.

Prior art: [Heimersheim/Nanda](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.15255v1) already
distinguishes mediation, calibration and AND/OR routes;
[Wang et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16591) separates QK routing and value
updates in ICL. Our criterion graph is not their QK/V decomposition or a novel
general context mechanism. [Mohammadi et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.02452v3)
already studies name/definition conflicts. System-specific construction and
its empirical consequences must be separated from these established methods.

OpenAI/math pinned adc7f124 three-state foundations provides an observation-law
question: what law connects an observation to its target? Its branch product
requires conditional independence, and conditional Jensen requires an
input-independent degradation kernel. Neither hypothesis was established for
these neural routes. Its reconstruction threshold is NOT transferred here.

the sum of separately scored routes fails. This does NOT exclude a different
