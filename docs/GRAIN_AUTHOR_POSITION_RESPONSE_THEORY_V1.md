## 1. A three-part measurement record

For a frozen SOURCE population R and target q, preserve the existing score
program S_Rq(X) and a SOURCE-only origin

    m_Rq = mean_(SOURCE author,window) S_Rq(z_iv).
    P_uq = S_Rq(X_u) - m_Rq.
    D_uq(g1,g0) = S_Rq(g1 X_u) - S_Rq(g0 X_u).

The score is HM218's finite Gaussian-mixture observation law, not a discovered
natural language likelihood. X_u is the same complete twelve-comment mean;
g0/g1 are the old matched removals. The origin is an in-bank instrument
reference, not an unbiased population norm or a clinical standardization.
It is fixed when other application authors are added or removed.

A useful record keeps [P,D,Q], where Q is the event identities, observation
contract, SOURCE origin/law, admissible edits, encoder version and conditions.
Neither P nor D is the person's psychological response operator B_u. D is
the observer's response to changing the available evidence about the person.
Choice, context, state and joint evidence remain; none is declared noise.

For SOURCE full/association/departure accounts, P_F=P_B+P_D and the same holds
for the edit responses. All three survive the cold interface. The new CLI
receives numeric SOURCE prototypes/profiles and addressed event vectors only;

## 2. Why response matching does not settle author measurement

An author-specific constant added to both edited observations leaves their
response unchanged while changing their level. This elementary distinction
was already established in HM77/HM109 and the earlier author panel; HM225 does
not repeat it as a new theorem. Likewise, model agreement and a human anchor
are distinct edges, not a transitive identification.

For three standardized random variables X,M,Y, positive semidefiniteness of
their correlation matrix gives the ordinary constraint

    |r_XY - r_XM*r_MY| <= sqrt((1-r_XM^2)*(1-r_MY^2)).

Agreement with M does not uniquely fix contact with Y. Here X can be the
SOURCE evidence response, M the native response, and Y an existing Big5
outcome. Do not apply the equation to different levels/responses as if they
were the same variable, or call correlation a causal bridge.

The missing recent interface was empirical, not this algebra: the newer
SOURCE-selected evidence/navigation and native-reader constructions had no
application outcome join. HM225 freezes their rules and supplies that join,
keeping author level and response separate.

## 4. Scientific decision and ancestry

We now have a reusable numerical record for source-referenced position AND
observer response, and an actual link to the SAME author's existing outcome.
The recent response/navigation mechanism is useful, but its successful local
model contact cannot be used as the criterion for a completed author measure.
Stop improving capsule/head fidelity to solve the different meaning problem.

Next inventory existing criterion-, condition- and reference-indexed author
observation laws and public outcome provenance. The next construction must
explain how the target of a model response becomes a source-supported author
observable, rather than fitting a better predictor on these24 people or
renaming a cooperative-reading criterion as the whole Agreeableness trait.
Keep both position and response; preserve earlier GRAIN anonymous/behavioral
measurement objects rather than treating a weak Big5 link as their failure.

Jacovi and Goldberg2021 distinguish model causal attribution and the social
interpretation attached to it: https://aclanthology.org/2021.tacl-1.18/ .
Borsboom et al2004, pages1061-1062, motivates a substantive measurement process
in addition to association: https://dennyborsboom.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/01/borsboomvalidity2004.pdf .
That is a particular causal-validity theory, not a unanimous definition or
an excuse to discard exploratory evidence. HM171/old external links already
used public Big5 anchors. HM225 is not the first personality correlation.

OpenAI/math adc7f124 three-state foundations161-245 was reread. Its specified
input/observation law precedes combining likelihoods. No posterior calibration,
tree reconstruction threshold or conditional independence theorem is claimed
for Qwen digits or natural authors. Fixed origins, covariance, correlations
and bootstrap are established methods; the new result is this SAME-event
