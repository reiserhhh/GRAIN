- **FU4-a (R26/A07): rival-information adaptive design (P03 p.19).**
  - 500 persons, each generated by one rival among M1-M5, 100 per rival.
  - P(y=1 | cell) = logistic(-1 + 1.5 x_j(cell)), where x_j is rival j's regressor over
    the 32 coded factor cells (belief B, stakes e, relationship r, cost k, register g).
    The intercept and effect are known to the analyst; the rival identity is unknown.
  - Exact posterior over the 5 rivals, uniform prior.
  - Policies:
    - FIXED: cells in a fixed balanced cycle;
    - SCALAR-CAT: choose the cell with the most Fisher information for the person's
      overall propensity, pooling the rivals;
    - RIVAL-INFO: choose the cell that maximizes the expected reduction of posterior
      entropy over rivals.
  - Budget 60 trials.
  - Metrics: trials until the maximum posterior reaches .90 (length-to-confidence);
    misclassification at stopping and at the budget; share never reaching .90.
- **FU4-b (R15): speaker-side politeness RSA** (after Yoon, Tessler, Goodman and Frank).
  - States 0-3; utterances {terrible, bad, good, amazing} and their negations.
  - Literal semantics as declared soft truth values.
  - Speaker utility = w_inf log L0(s|u) + w_soc E_L0[value(s)] + w_pres log P_L1(goal),
    with softmax alpha 3.
  - Grid of goal weights over (w_inf, w_soc, w_pres), 3 levels each.
  - A pragmatic listener L2 infers state and goal weights from utterances generated at
    (a) one true state (1) and (b) all four states.
  - Report whether the social (politeness) and presentational (impression-management)
    weights are identified, as the posterior correlation of the two weights and recovery
    of the true grid point, under (a) vs (b).
