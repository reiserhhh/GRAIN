## 1. Construction, not accuracy optimization

An effective evidence operation can move multiple reader targets. A single
SOURCE coordinate does not determine the meaning of all those movements.
Improving numerical accuracy or fitting a scalar calibration does not repair
that interface. Construct a contrast which has an explicit observation law
and mathematically excludes a specified class of simpler explanations.

For one author u, let X_k be real, complete comment remainders and D_r(X_k)
a frozen SOURCE target functional. Let Z(X_k) describe properties to hold
fixed. The construction outputs positive probability laws P_plus/P_minus:

    nu = P_plus - P_minus;
    nu(1)=0; nu(Z)=0; nu(D_r)>0.

The target-indexed reader action is

    Gamma_(r->q)(u;Z) = integral R_q(X) dnu(X).

This separates three objects: SOURCE evidence meaning D_r, presentation law
nu, and reader interpretation R_q. Their numerical units need not coincide.
No application reader answer is used to construct nu.

## 2. Existence on finite support

Put A=[1,Z] with one row per valid input. For fixed g=D_r, a positive-arm
contrast with nu(Z)=0 and nu(g)>0 exists IF AND ONLY IF g is not in the
column span of A, on this finite support.

Proof: if g=A beta, every such contrast has nu(g)=0. Otherwise there is a
finite signed vector d in null(A^T) with d^T g!=0. Orient it positively.
Because d^T1=0, its positive and negative parts have equal nonzero mass m.
Dividing them by m gives two probability laws supported on REAL X_k and
positive g contrast. This does not require invented interpolation inputs.

The finite LP maximizes g contrast under those constraints. Compactness
guarantees an optimum; the dual provides a bound and numerical certificate.
Zero optimum is a source-identification limit, not a reason to add precision
grids. Sparse finite quadrature reduces required observations without claiming
that unspecified nonlinear reader functions are preserved.

## 3. Event-at-slot balance excludes separable explanations

In HM250 Z contains every indicator I_k(event,rendered_slot) and the F/B/D
SOURCE scores for the four non-C targets. Each valid X_k has eight comments
in chronological subset order; all use the identical prompt renderer.

Every fixed event-at-slot function

    R_sep(X_k)=b+sum_(event,slot) beta_(event,slot) I_k(event,slot)

has Gamma=0 exactly in ideal arithmetic. This holds for arbitrary functions
of each individual comment and its slot, not only linear lexical counts.
It also implies equal expected length, per-comment topic/format composition
and additive embedding sums. The model never receives a mixed embedding.

Consequently any nonzero observed action belongs to the nonseparable part
of the reader FUNCTION on this support. Position interactions, joint meaning,
coherence, contextual evaluation, and unmeasured cues can all be nonseparable;
nonzero action alone does not uniquely identify their psychological meaning.

The other SOURCE scores are equal in expectation, not fully distribution
matched. Their nonlinear reader transformations are not forced to cancel.
SOURCE scores themselves are nonlinear functions of an averaged embedding;
their curvature can survive equal expected additive embeddings. Equality of
means is not equality of all input distributions or inference functions.

## 4. What is measured

For each support input obtain its native digit log odds Y_q(X_k), then
average those ENDPOINTS under the two probability laws. This is the expected
response to randomized complete presentations. Do not average hidden states,
logits or probabilities and then call the nonlinear result the same estimand.

For C as the source target, positive Gamma_(C->C) is a constructive bridge:
joint evidence identified without application answers is used in a matching
reader judgment. Gamma_(C->A) records shared use under the declared controls.
Neither is human personality accuracy. Across authors these operators can
later form a conditional source-to-reader atlas, but HM250 observes one row
and two endpoints, not that full atlas.

Where/choice, what/evidence, who/author-relative SOURCE relationship, and
when/attested input order remain in the design. Conditioning and balancing
are experimental comparison tools, not permission to discard context as noise.

## 6. Adequate observation precision

If each observed endpoint has numerical reconstruction error at most e,
then the contrast error is at most e*sum_k|nu_k|<=2e. If each candidate digit
logit has reconstruction error at most h, high/low log odds error is at most
2h (log-sum-exp is sup-norm1-Lipschitz), so the contrast bound is at most4h.
These are conditional numerical bounds, not model uncertainty, sampling
intervals or human measurement errors. They justify stopping precision work
once numerical error is below a mechanism difference; they do not improve
personality accuracy or infer that a weak response is absent.
