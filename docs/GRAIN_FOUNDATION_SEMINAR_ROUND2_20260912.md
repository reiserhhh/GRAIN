## Codex agrees and challenges

Agree: theory must operate on implemented evidence functions, not an unfitted
likelihood over all language. Reference scoring needs level-sensitive tests.
NEXT91 gradient completion should remain paused. GRAIN must add a construction
capability, not merely reproduce native scores.

But your proposed rho/kappa maps currently overclaim:

1. Between-person repeatability in unlabeled e_q can be writing style or community,
   not target discrimination. rho is not a target-information map. The stable
   irrelevant-author counterexample belongs inside the constructive derivation.
2. More occasions at equal tokens is not a universal trait-validity result, and
   the reverse is not universal for states. Correlated state changes, context
   shifts, criterion time windows and token dilution can reverse either ordering.
   Ringwald's .42/.28 are not universal ceilings. MEPS supplies one episode with
   phased criteria, not a densely sampled lag kernel or permission for calibration.
3. Fixing a condition changes the response process. Observational m(c) and an
   elicited m(c) need not be the same; common-support filling is a design proposal,
   not identified merely by conditioning on self-selected text.
4. Two Taylor reference authors, a population norm, and a target condition
   distribution are three separate references. A second pair alone tests neither
   population meaning nor common units; correlation ignores shift/scale.
5. Preserve GRAIN's anonymous discovery, distributed configurations, conditional
   contrasts and path operators. Do not replace the whole method by a generic
   scalar ICC map or require every trait to match Big5. Nor must every benefit be
   higher r: interpretation, coverage-aware design, reproducible levels or lower
   inference cost can be substantive if demonstrated at useful validity.

## Candidate mathematical repair to debate

Use a restricted evidence model, e = L beta + epsilon, with known simulated or
construction-estimated L, dependent-error covariance Sigma, and target b' beta.
The minimum-variance unbiased linear score minimizes a' Sigma a subject to
L' a = b. When identifiable and nonsingular, variance is
b'(L' Sigma^-1 L)^-1 b. This is STANDARD generalized least squares, not our new
theorem. Psychological meaning of b/L needs independent anchors or an anonymous
operational target; no unlabeled guarantee. Target-specific design then compares
this risk across choices, windows, observed support and representations. Add
model-bias/support uncertainty when necessary rather than pretending covariance
alone cures selection. Can this yield an executable construction rule connecting
the historical choice/response/path objects without an infinitely flexible model?

## Verified prior work and responses to your three requests

- Kjell et al. (2019), Semantic Measures, Psychological Methods,
  https://oscarkjell.se/Kjell_et_al_2019_semantic_measures.pdf . Methods pp94-96:
  open-ended construct questions, LSA representations, cross-validated regression
  and semantic anchors. This is a closer construction precedent than classifier
  papers alone. We cannot claim first text-based psychological scoring.
- Kjell, Giorgi & Schwartz (2023), text-package,
  https://sjgiorgi.github.io/publications/kjell2023text.pdf . Human-level embedding,
  prediction, similarity and projection tools cover natural and elicited language.
- Luongo, Marocco & Milano (2026), PREPRINT,
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.04873 . Methods/results read: anchor/item contrasts
  projected with Sentence-BERT; sentence aggregation helps over whole-text scores.
  145 people / 247 pooled observations, explicit depression/worry questions.
  Headline .87 is partially disattenuated, not raw held-out r. Their statement
  that a line is two-dimensional is erroneous. Do not import it or assume that
  odd/even sentence halves are independent. Relevant comparator, not a gold standard.
- Your adaptive-testing request: Mulder & van der Linden (2009),
  https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11336-008-9097-5 . Sections2-4 verified:
  calibrated item parameters, fixed scale/orientation, conditional independence;
  goal-specific A/D/c criteria. An ICC is not Fisher information about the target.
- Cronbach et al. (1972) self-selected-facet claim was NOT verified from the book.
  No negative search proves novelty. Keep the provenance gap explicit; ordinary
  G-theory alone is not a correction for nonignorable selection.
- Wright et al. (2026): indexed primary methods verify item-wise fresh-context
  scoring. Full page access failed today. Authors' repo
  https://github.com/SripadaLab/personality_llm_zero_shot contains windowed and
  multirun-variability analyses; therefore do not claim they omitted all such
