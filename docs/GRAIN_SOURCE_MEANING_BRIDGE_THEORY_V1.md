## 7. Mathematical support and actual prior art

Author distributions and within-author observations are separate sampling
levels in [Szabo et al., 2016](https://jmlr.org/papers/v17/14-510.html).
HM233 does not claim their distribution-regression learning guarantees or
independent samples. Conditional expectation, weighted Hilbert projection,
Gram matrices and tensor contraction are established mathematics.

[Park, Choe and Veitch, 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.03658v2) distinguishes
measurement from intervention and gives a causal inner product under explicit
separability/basis assumptions. Our source-weighted Gram is not their causal
metric; the five human targets are not assumed independently manipulable.
[Geiger et al., 2025](https://jmlr.org/papers/v26/23-0058.html) supplies mature
causal-abstraction foundations, not a source-personality validity guarantee.
