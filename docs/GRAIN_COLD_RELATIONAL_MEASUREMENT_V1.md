## The object before the coordinates

GRAIN's observation law and the author distribution remain distinct:

    E ~ P_u, F_b(E)=T_{q,b}(E), Theta_{u,b}=E_{P_u} F_b(E).

Here b indexes material presentation and the fixed observer, not a new author
property. Two semantically matched accounts can produce different F_b. A source
score equality therefore defines an equivalence class only for that observation
law, not equality of people or equality of every behavior distribution.

For the controlled experiment E=(x,y,g) is a declared behavioral configuration.
Its three occasion identities and actor binding are held separately. x/y are
design polarities, g assigns the companion act to A or B. These are not learned
psychological axes, causal writer parameters, or independently verified events.
In natural text, this identity layer must be established before transporting
author profiles; an embedding-neighbor match alone does not certify identity.

## A relational reading, not a better mean predictor

HM70 calibrates common event functions Z on two complete declared tables.
Its five score contrasts are not by themselves a semantic discovery: on eight
cells, two affine spaces of rank7 have intersection dimension at least6; after
removing the shared constant, at least5 contrasts remain by the Grassmann
dimension formula. All30 source score pairs attain this lower bound. Even
unrelated full-rank tables can therefore yield five formal common contrasts.
The scientific evidence must come from their consequences on new observations
and correctly matched units, not merely existence or dimension of the intersection.

HM71 freezes the family0 map C0 and asks what happens on entirely new wording:

    Z_b(E) = [1,F_b(E)] C0
    M_b(P_u) = (E_u Z_b, E_u[Z_b Z_b^T]).

No new fit is allowed. In general neither exact Z_b=Z nor exact numerical score
invariance follows from the intention to describe the same act. Distinguish:

- comparable event identity;
- existence of a nonzero author-profile contrast under the new observation;
- source/cold direction alignment;
- preservation of the numerical units and source inverse.

The first is design metadata; the other three require actual readings. A cold
mean contrast can become nonzero even for source-mean-equivalent profiles. That
is observation-law dependence, not evidence that a cold second moment alone
found hidden personality. Do not convert numerical differences to trait names.

## Coordinate-invariant distribution comparison

For d=P-Q, set m=E_P Z-E_Q Z and S=E_P ZZ^T-E_Q ZZ^T. Then

    D2 = ||m||^2 + ||S||_F^2 = d^T K d,
    K(e,e') = <Z(e),Z(e')> + <Z(e),Z(e')>^2.

An orthogonal coordinate change Z'=ZU rotates m and conjugates S; D2 is
unchanged. Use the full symmetric tensor, or a correctly sqrt(2)-weighted
upper triangle, not an unweighted upper triangle. The kernel is standard
degree-two polynomial geometry, not a newly invented or globally characteristic
kernel. Different distributions can have the same first two moments. Its exact
eight-cell separation in HM70 was an empirical finite-rank fact.

The independent-event control has E[Z]E[Z]^T. Within-event products and products
of already pooled means are different measurements. This is where the extra
information enters; it is not reconstructed from a summary that discarded it.

A changed origin z->z+b leaves mean differences unchanged, but changes raw
second-moment differences by b Delta m^T + Delta m b^T. Hence a large raw second
moment is not automatically a new dependence effect. The existing independent
pairing control separates the within-author covariance difference
Delta Cov=Delta S-Delta(mm^T). Its individual covariances are invariant under a
common translation. This is an interpretation identity, not permission to
recenter cold data, replace the frozen primary metric or choose the best score.
Retain the means alongside covariances; do not delete author location/context.

## What would justify transport

If ||Z_b(e)-Z(e)|| <= epsilon on the declared support and ||Z(e)|| <= R, then
by the triangle inequality and the outer-product identity:

    ||m_b-m|| <= ||d||_1 epsilon,
    ||S_b-S||_F <= ||d||_1 epsilon(2R+epsilon).

Proof: write z_b z_b^T-zz^T = (z_b-z)z^T + z(z_b-z)^T
+ (z_b-z)(z_b-z)^T and sum with signed masses d(e). Combining the two bounds
gives a lower bound on cold distance: max(0,D_source-B). This is a conservative
finite-support stability certificate, not a sample CI or a psychological effect
size. Its failure does not prove no relation; its success covers only the
declared units. Large observation-law changes cannot be fixed philosophically
by claiming nuisance removal or universal coefficients.

## How this connects to the mainline

The proposed measurement interface is

    author text -> event/actor/occasion units -> fixed query-vector observation
    -> material-indexed relational functions -> author distributional profile.

It retains choice composition, conditional expression and state/history in
P_u rather than deleting them. It also retains the observer T separately from
personal response B_u. A source-calibrated operator that only interpolates the
old support is not the final natural-text mechanism. HM71 asks whether this
interface survives fresh expressions, before any attempt to make it more precise.

Reading: Gretton et al.2012 Section2/Lemma4/6 gives the kernel mean distance;
Sriperumbudur et al.2011 explains distribution embedding injectivity. The pinned
OpenAI metric/connection introduction inspires observed forms versus coordinate
gauges, but no PDE uniqueness, ellipticity or smooth-manifold result is imported.
