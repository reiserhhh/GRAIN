## Do not redefine success

Neither an additional significant context effect nor a large number of passing
tests closes the whole project. Exact information decomposition at the final
readout is standard mathematical instrumentation; empirical value lies in what
the controlled inputs change and what transfers to independent permitted data.
The plan can yield a well-supported limit rather than superior personality
accuracy, but that limit must come from the relevant test, not from omitting it.
The original objective remains active while the above required work is pending.
