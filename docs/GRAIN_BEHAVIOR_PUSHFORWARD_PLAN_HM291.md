## Construction

Let w_i(x) be the unchanged source-mode posterior from the early18 states.
Let b_i be the seven-vector computed from source author's later18 metadata.
These are mean/sd log1p inherited span lengths,mean/sd log1p adjacent cached-record
gaps,exact-venue entropy,switch fraction and distinct-venue fraction.
Span units are inherited end-start address units,not newly certified token or
character lengths. Recorded gaps are sampling-regime quantities,NOT reply latency or emotional
recovery. Venue switch/entropy/distinct counts overlap,not three replications.

    m(x)=sum_i w_i(x)b_i
    m_G(x)=sum_g W_g(x) mean_(source i:G_i=g)b_i
    m(x)-m_G(x)=sum_i w_i(x)(b_i-mean_(G_i)b).

This is a specified SOURCE working joint law with deterministic b_i attached
to mode i; it does not prove natural B independent of X given I. Neither
posterior is calibrated as a human-trait probability. Actual versus OLD
permuted profiles compare two equally sized grouping schemes while the full
mode predictor remains exactly unchanged. Keep both full and profile terms.
No refit of kernels,profile counts,prior,signs or source likelihood is allowed.

The cold numeric constructor requires only a source bundle and an identified
early episode. It receives no later metadata or current person's labels.
Source b values are licensed numeric derivatives,not current person's truth.
