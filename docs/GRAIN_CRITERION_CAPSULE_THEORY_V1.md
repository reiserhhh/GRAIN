## 1. Object and reason for construction

An author observation is not merely a bag of token vectors. Its use depends on
the observation contract q and the reader context c. HM222 found a usable relay
and non-additive direct/relay action. A standalone body projection is therefore
not automatically interchangeable with that particular full neural reader.
We now ask whether an observed criterion-conditioned component can act after
the original excerpts have been removed from the recipient computation.

The new interface exports numeric source evidence, then reads it in a fresh
process with a common excerpt-free template. Neural source encoding and neural
recipient computation remain. Export/import ability is not human validity.

## 2. Retained baseline and paired evidence increment

On fixed layer/head/identical-token suffix addresses, write

\[
V_q(x)=B_q(x)+I_q(x),\qquad
I_q(x)=V_q^{\rm native}(x)-V_q^{\rm closed}(x).
\]

Closed is a specific graph intervention that retains length, positions, tokens,
query and baseline context. B is not defined as psychological noise. Differences
between S and T in their closed baselines are retained in the source record.
The exported object is the pair-operation capsule

\[
\Delta I_{u,q}=I_q(S_u)-I_q(T_u).
\]

It represents this evidence operation, not an absolute latent personality value.
The same events re-encoded under six contracts are six views, not six independent
observations. Capsule provenance binds author, selected events, source graph,
criterion, suffix coordinates and observer version. No native output answer
enters the capsule calculation.

## 3. Cold observation action rather than frozen probability replay

Let c0(q) be a fixed recipient with the existing empty-evidence contract. At each
layer inject a*DeltaI into suffix V ONLY for the final-row contraction. All
pre-final states and their K/V, masks and causal prefix remain native. No K is
directly patched. Final-row Q and self K/V are recomputed from the intervened
previous final state: their values need not equal the empty baseline. Recompute
final QK weights and subsequent final-row states under the new recipient. The
sealed plan's "keep recipient K" means no direct K edit, not a frozen final K.

\[
F_q(a\Delta I;c_0)=
\log\frac{\sum_{j=5}^9\exp z_{q,j}(a\Delta I;c_0)}
{\sum_{j=0}^4\exp z_{q,j}(a\Delta I;c_0)}.
\]

The fixed symmetric action is Zq=Fq(+DeltaI/2)-Fq(-DeltaI/2). This dose is not
optimized. Positive and negative injections are numeric counterfactuals, not
generated natural texts or two reachable psychological states. We do not keep
source p fixed and do not fit a new classifier to recover a desired answer.
This creates a new observer, not an exact reconstruction of the native observer.

## 4. Involution separates shared values from criterion-dependent content

J exchanges cooperation/exploration within each scale name. J^2=Id. Define

\[
P_+=(Id+J)/2,\quad P_-=(Id-J)/2,\quad
\Delta I=I_{\rm common}+I_{\rm specific}.
\]

These are ordinary finite orthogonal projections in fixed V units. We transport
both components; no claim that the common component is noise. The three modes
are DeltaI, JDeltaI and P+DeltaI. If P-DeltaI=0 then aligned and swapped actions
are exactly identical under the same reader. However common content CAN still
produce a criterion contrast because the reader itself depends on q.

Thus an aligned-minus-swapped contrast discriminates criterion-dependent
transported content from solely criterion-blind source content. It does not
isolate pure personality or rule out all lexical/task/position interpretations.
Nonlinear reader coupling of common and specific content remains in the result.
Neither geometric energy nor this contrast is a psychological information fraction.

## 5. Mathematical and methodological ancestry

Increment/action/projection algebra is standard. Function Vectors already
transports task abstractions across contexts; path patching already distinguishes
mediation, direct action and calibration. Our candidate contribution is an
author-evidence observer construction, NOT generic vector transfer or new group
theory. OpenAI/math three-state observation experiments motivate specifying the
law before merging evidence. Their conditional independence and input-independent
degradation assumptions do not apply to this deterministic criterion-dependent
capsule; neither Jensen information-loss claims nor reconstruction thresholds
are transferred. Source/cold association is about this model's reading, not an
external personality scale relationship.
