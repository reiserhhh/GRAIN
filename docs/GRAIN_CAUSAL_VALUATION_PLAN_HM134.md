## Theory and sources

Attribution of a disposition from constrained behavior is established psychology:
Jones/Harris1967 https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-1031(67)90034-0
and Bayesian inverse planning https://www.tomerullman.org/papers/intelligenceAttribution.pdf
(read abstract, computational framework and inference equations). Different
strategies/outcomes can explain identical action records; our text mechanism is
not a replication of their human experiment or an assumption of rational LLMs.
Actual interchange scope follows Geiger et al2025:
https://jmlr.org/papers/v26/23-0058.html

For a latent theta, action a and context z, the observation law is

    p(theta|a,z) proportional to p(a|theta,z) p(z|theta) p(theta).

If the act is fully imposed independently of theta, its likelihood ratio may
equal1. That does not erase information in the author's choice of z. With unknown
constraint strength, discounting need not be complete. Unit tests demonstrate
these alternatives, rather than asserting the equation identifies human traits.
This is ordinary Bayes, not a novel denoising formula or automatic personality key.
