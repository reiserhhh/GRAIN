# HM46: new-material multi-construct evidence routing

Prepared 2026-10-09. HM45 remains planned and unexecuted. This packet replaces
neither the original 18/100/44 scope nor existing GRAIN psychometric work.

## Scientific decision

Do six queries read six independent content tendencies, mostly a shared
evaluative direction, or a mixture? Does changing author ownership or event
actuality change that structure? Can those changes be transmitted through the
same-word property states, or primarily through an end-of-account carrier?
The point is a conditional evidence law, not improving a teacher approximation.

surface forms, and a complete polarity x ownership x actuality cube. All 192

Keep actual/imagined and self/other accounts; an imagined choice may itself

## Mathematical object and literature

For each content c/query q/form w/example f, decompose the native response on
the balanced (p,o,a) design using its eight Z2^3 characters:

    beta_S = (1/8) sum_b (-1)^(S dot b) score(b)

Retain all eight components and exact reconstruction/Parseval. This group acts
on experimental indices, not automatically on token vectors. Build the six by
six polarity-contrast matrix D[c,q | o,a,f,w]. Analyze its singular spectrum,
source/application agreement and source-leading-mode overlap. Rank and overlap
describe reader responses, not discovered psychological factors. Compare the
explicit evaluative sign vector (+,-,+,+,+,0), leaving anthropomorphism unassigned.
Common-mode and target-specific remainders are retained, not called noise.

The new question is motivated by Park/Choe/Veitch ICML2024 counterfactual concept
geometry (https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/park24c.html) and Anthropic2025 QK
feature interactions (https://transformer-circuits.pub/2025/attention-qk/index.html).
We do not assume Park's causal-separability hypotheses or replace the causal
metric with unqualified Euclidean cosine. SVD here is a declared finite output
matrix description, not their causal-inner-product theorem.
