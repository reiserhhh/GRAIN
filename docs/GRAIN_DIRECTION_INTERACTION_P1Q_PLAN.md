# P1Q: cache-only functional interaction of projected and remaining states

2026-09-07. Declared while P1P runs, before reading its response summaries. No
new neural forward. Decision: does apparently successful or unsuccessful direction
transfer arise from the direction alone, its complement, or cancellation between
the complement and their joint readout interaction?

Use all80 P1P recipients and both B/R donors (160 cases), all four patch conditions,
without rank/layer/setting/criterion selection. Projected and complementary
displacements sum to the natural displacement before FP32 rounding. Save actual
rounding error. For baseline f0, natural fn, projected fp and complementary fc:

    D=fp-f0, C=fc-f0, I=fn-fp-fc+f0
    fn-f0 = D+C+I
    (fn-fp)^2 = C^2+I^2+2*C*I.

Aggregate squared terms with their signed cross term separately per query and
evidence axis, then by setting; never call them nonnegative information fractions.
Report vector energy retained by projection alongside functional changes, not as
equivalent quantities. Report matched-random changes and baseline errors from
P1P, without letting either select the direction retrospectively.

This is an exact, reference-dependent finite difference, not a new theorem,
causal mediation proportion, gradient or stable personality factor. The patches
are artificial states. Independently recompute from raw candidate logits and
saved FP32 arrays. CPU cap60seconds, immutable output/source snapshot, tests on
additive and multiplicative synthetic readouts. No new training or text/label use.

If complement and interaction cancel, do not claim that a low projected error
identifies a sufficient isolated mechanism. If they are individually small, the
current rank-one approximation gains a narrower local justification. Keep failed
criteria and scenes. Next design should address the observed mismatch, not add
dimensions blindly; old queues remain paused.
