## 1. The mechanism question that changed

HM251's public behavioral directions do not automatically share meaning with
native model judgments. Numerical precision does not supply that missing link.
Input-vector/measuring-covector duality was already present in HM36/HM125.
The missing interface here is a statistical calibration to an independently
observed operational criterion, not another semantic direction or metric.

The data requirement is a qualified paired observation (C,Z,W,Y): C is the
opportunity/condition, W is the text/vector channel to be used for application,
Z is an auxiliary observation from a distinct occasion, and Y is a distinct
operational behavior measurement. They concern the same underlying response
regime. Y is not an LLM answer, target-word embedding or a latent class name.
Neither common authorship nor distinct event IDs proves independence of errors.

## 2. Noncausal measurement bridge

For each condition c and criterion q define

    K_c[z,w] = P_source(W=w | Z=z,C=c),
    m_cq[z]  = E_source[Y_q | Z=z,C=c],
    K_c h_cq = m_cq.

These are observable source moments; the constructor receives no latent U,
emission channels, application Y or LLM judgments. Suppose the generating law
has W independent of Z conditional on (U,c), and the conditional mean of Y
does not depend on Z after (U,c) is given. Write

    A_c[u,w] = P(W=w | U=u,c),
    B_c[z,u] = P_source(U=u | Z=z,c),
    b_cq[u]  = E[Y_q | U=u,c].

Then K_c=B_c A_c and m_cq=B_c b_cq. If B_c separates the relevant functions
of U, K_c h_cq=m_cq entails A_c h_cq=b_cq. Existence of a solution is a
separate requirement. A useful proxy is not automatically a sufficiently rich
proxy for every behavioral target. No intervention or do-effect is claimed.

For a new author's conditional vector law p_uc and observed condition choice
pi_u(c), the measurement is

    theta_uq = sum_c pi_u(c) p_uc^T h_cq.

If the application W and behavioral-mean channels remain the same and p_uc
is generated by those channels, theta_uq equals the corresponding behavioral
mean. Choice and condition response remain in the measurement. It is not a
context-centered residual or a per-event probability classification.

## 3. Why ordinary prediction can drift while the bridge does not

The source conditional regression is

    r_pi(w,c) = sum_u pi(u|c) A_c[u,w] b_cq[u]
                / sum_u pi(u|c) A_c[u,w].

It is a legitimate source-population predictor under a squared-error objective.
Its dependence on pi is not a software bug. In general A_c r_pi != b_cq, so
averaging its predictions for a different author/regime population need not
preserve the behavioral meaning. The bridge instead solves the measurement
equation A_c h=b_cq without estimating A_c, B_c or a full latent model.

This explains a concrete difference between predicting a source answer and
measuring a transported criterion mean. It does NOT establish superior
single-event prediction risk. Bridge weights can lie outside [0,1] and have
higher sampling variance; clipping changes the measurement equation.

## 4. Identified functionals need not require unique coordinates

When h is nonunique, all solutions are h0+n, n in ker(K). A new author law
p identifies its reading iff p is orthogonal to ker(K). Equivalently in the
finite Euclidean-coordinate account there is a q solving

    K^T q = p,     p^T h = q^T m.

The minimum-norm representative is computational convenience, not the uniquely
true psychological function. An out-of-row-space p returns feasible readings
that disagree, not a fabricated point score. Those two readings are witnesses,
not a bounded identified interval. This uses standard linear-functional
identification, not a newly discovered duality theorem.

In continuous spaces closure of the adjoint range and membership in the actual
range differ. Injectivity/completeness alone does not make a compact operator
surjective, and finite SVD success does not settle continuous identification
or sample estimation. This packet does not implement an infinite inverse.

ancestry; no claim of a published-paper error or novel proxy method is made.

98--116/166--210 motivates observation laws and qualified independence before
composition. Its symmetric tree reconstruction theorem is not transplanted.

Project delta: a source-paired operational calibration that can read an author
vector distribution without recovering every latent factor or imitating an
LLM answer. This is integration of established theory into GRAIN, not evidence
that our own mathematics supersedes prior art. It adds positive construction
in a declared world; it does not identify an actual human personality construct.
