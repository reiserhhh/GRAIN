# Whole-system literature seminar: final bounded clarification

No tools, searches, files, experiments or new tasks. Respond in at most 450 words.

Codex accepts the narrowed three-interface research target, but corrects these
points before writing the final synthesis:

1. Shared across readers is not cue validity, and reader-specific is not reader
   bias. Both can contain useful criterion signal and artifacts. sJIVE identifies
   a statistical row-space decomposition under its conditions, not psychological
   source ownership. We must not label its components as validity/utilization by
   definition. It does not require independent-view noise merely to fit.
2. MEPS turns need not be equally spaced, and ME-TSO is not automatically a
   continuous-time irregular-observation model. Keep observation scale explicit;
   use ctsem/continuous-time models where genuinely timed data warrant it.
3. A text-inferred context can mix contact, construal, narration and reader
   inference. Neither recorded metadata nor interlocutor text is a guaranteed
   objective or exogenous contact variable.
4. 'Before criteria' should mean before the TARGET VALIDATION outcomes/design
   contrasts are examined. Authorized development criteria can and should help
   discover/build measures. Existing MEPS remains validation-only; it is neither
   a development set nor a fresh prospective trial. Previously examined results
   remain retrospective. A design comparison of different existing datasets does
   not by itself estimate the causal effect of prompts.
5. Our deliverables are construction demonstrations and reusable theory, not
   two finished instruments. Existing datasets can establish a bounded foundation,
   not clinical implementation. No field-wide novelty or journal guarantee.
6. A theory claim must add at least one non-obvious, testable quantitative result
   beyond the composed predecessors. It need not prove all interfaces newly.
   Negative findings can delimit that theory rather than restart endless queues.

For the final record: confirm or object to each substantive correction, and give
the single most valuable next theoretical step. Do not add another research branch.
