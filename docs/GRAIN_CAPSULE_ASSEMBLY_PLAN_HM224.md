# HM224: source-supported assembly of multiple author evidence blocks

2026-10-12. Fixed before preparation and every new model forward. This is a
finite mechanism/construction experiment, not an accuracy or precision search.
Original18/100/44 remain OPEN; all older artifacts and failures are retained.

## Decision and competing mechanisms

HM223 exported one paired S/T evidence operation. It did not show that separately
encoded event blocks can be added to obtain the same author observation. HM102
already distinguishes normalized local readings from attention-mass merges;
HM142 already excludes repeated-event independence; HM205 already handles
lossless regrouping of fixed embeddings under a declared Gaussian law. None
of those experiments re-encoded the current real excerpts into disjoint blocks
and then transported their conditional actions to HM223's fresh reader.

The unknown is whether the compiler of a multi-event author operation is modular
on this support, and whether a non-modular compiler difference matters after
reading. Competing explanations for assembly failure are source compilation,
nonlinear recipient reading, both, or exact cancellation. A vector norm alone
does not decide the target observation. No desired sign is a success requirement.

    I_j = (Vnative(S_j)-Vclosed(S_j))-(Vnative(T_j)-Vclosed(T_j)).

## Prior art and reading scope

Finite inclusion-exclusion/Moebius interactions and nonlinear composition are
standard; no new theorem is claimed. Shapley-Taylor primary abstract was read
(https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.05622); this packet is not its attribution algorithm.
Wang et al2026 Sec2.3-3 actual methods were read
(https://arxiv.org/html/2605.16591v2): they use context/edge-controlled sub-FVs,
globally fitted weights and injection layer/scale sweeps. We do not fit those
weights, do not equate their task FVs to our evidence observations, and do not
claim that failure of fixed unit-weight addition disproves weighted FV models.

OpenAI/math pinned adc7f124 algebra.tex1-160 was reread: compositional meaning
requires compatible episode updates. Its finite monoid/F2/star-height theorem
does not apply to this real-valued nonlinear compiler. This is design inspiration,
not transfer of a recent mathematical proof. Standard observation-law assumptions
remain explicit; two comment groups need not be statistically independent.

## Verification and stop

Before sealing: synthetic linear null, nonlinear-reader-only world, signed
cancellation, duplicate identity rejection and exact prompt reconstruction.
After capture: old model/digit head unchanged, source native/closed body hashes
equal, closed forbidden edges zero, all expected scopes present. After cold:
prefix hashes equal native, zero injection exact, whole sum and H replay,
alternate scalar odds and decomposition/bootstrap, CPU64 head checks. These
verify observation implementation, not human personality validity.

Stop after the finite stages even if results are negative. No head/layer/scale/
partition/seed/accuracy rescue. Decide whether the author recipe may sum separately
compiled blocks or must preserve a source-supported joint assembly contract.
Do not infer universal invariance, or abandon older choice/state/dynamics.
No API/MEPS/Git/Claude/automation/new training/lockbox/old queue.
