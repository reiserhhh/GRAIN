## Competing constructions, fixed before outcomes

C_t=(G,X_t,X_{t+1},X_{t+2}), t=0..15. G is the FULL measured five-bit SOURCE
profile. Each source author contributes equal mass, and each of their two
18-record chronological windows contributes half. Source cliques are measured
at their actual positions, not pooled over t. Adjacent clique separator margins
agree because they come from the same complete source windows.

1. Separator-corrected chain Q: first profile/pair joint times conditional
   transition kernels. It preserves every SOURCE clique and profile prior.
2. Overlap-product: prior times all conditional clique likelihoods, without
   separator divisions. Its raw empty-evidence reading may alter the prior.
   ALSO retain its per-profile-normalized version as a proper alternative law;
   normalization restores the prior but does not guarantee clique preservation.

The second candidate is a declared comparison, not a claim that an earlier
packet used this product. Neither is assumed to be the true human path law.
The chain imposes order-two conditional independence beyond measured cliques.
Its maximum-entropy property does not identify psychological mechanisms.

Predictions: Q preserves cliques exactly; product need not. On real held
histories support may fail entirely: retain all missing cases and stop rather
than smoothing/refitting/reducing profile counts. All supported five-target
readings and removal actions remain, including negatives and old permutation.
No criterion of increased r/AUC/native contact or bigger action is used.
