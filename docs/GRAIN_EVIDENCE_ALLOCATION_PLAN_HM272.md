It did NOT identify how the later reader assembles these events. Compare:

1. Query-dependent relative allocation between evidence banks is necessary for
   the operational source-linkage contact on these same observations.

   that contact under a declared equal-bank observation composition.

grid, or rediscovery of generic softmax nonlinearity. No outcome fitting.

## Exact local operation

For every suffix/candidate query, head and layer, let native current-query
weights be A. Evidence banks X and Z EXCLUDE header, inter-event seam, suffix,
candidate and pad keys. Their original masses are mX and mZ, m=mX+mZ. Define

    A'[X] = (m/2) A[X]/mX
    A'[Z] = (m/2) A[Z]/mZ
    A'[outside] = A[outside].

With conditional bank value means fX and fZ, the LOCAL output difference is

    O - O' = (mX-m/2)(fX-fZ).

This identity is standard finite probability. Recursive downstream effects
are not the sum of these local terms or a unique psychological mediation.
The outside weights are preserved at each ACTUAL modified query, not frozen
to the original unmodified network's queries. Total evidence mass and token
selection inside each bank remain query-dependent. Sinks are not deleted.
Zero bank mass fails the operation; no epsilon, imputation or precision rescue.
