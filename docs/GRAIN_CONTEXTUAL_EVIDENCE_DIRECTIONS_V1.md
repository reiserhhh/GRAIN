## Exact Compatibility Restricts Scalar Weighting

F(x) is a vector of observations on a declared alternative cube. Define

    d_j(x_-j) = F(x_j=+1,x_-j)-F(x_j=-1,x_-j).
    K_jk(c)   = d_j(x_k=+1,c)-d_j(x_k=-1,c)
              = d_k(x_j=+1,c)-d_k(x_j=-1,c).

The equality follows directly from the same four vertex values. It is the
mixed-difference compatibility of a state function. It must not be described
as nonzero circulation, noncommutativity, time-order sensitivity, curvature
or a newly discovered connection.

If d_j=a_j(c)b_j and d_k=a_k(c)b_k with fixed different lines, then K_jk
would have to belong to two lines whose intersection is {0}. Thus K_jk=0.
Independent scalar gates are not arbitrary: their changes must be mutually
compatible if they are to define one common observation of the same account.
Nonzero interaction plus distinct evidence lines requires at least one
direction to change. Parallel-line nonlinear worlds are the explicit exception.

For unit lines u_j,u_k and P_j=I-u_j u_j^T:

    SSE_four_edges >= (||P_j K||^2+||P_k K||^2)/2
                   >= (1-|u_j^T u_k|)||K||^2/2.

The first bound is the parallelogram inequality. The second is the minimum
eigenvalue of P_j+P_k. Summing all square bounds counts each edge n-1 times,
so dividing by n-1=5 gives a valid global edge-normal lower bound here.

The protocol uses mean edge directions, not optimized principal directions.
Its normal fractions are not the best approximation errors over all possible
axes. Exact fixed-line impossibility nevertheless follows from noncollinearity
when mean lines are nonzero; no direction/rank/threshold search is necessary.
Orthogonal changes of observation coordinates preserve these bounds. Arbitrary
rescaling or nonlinear recoding need not, so observation units remain explicit.

## Consequence for a Text-Based Measurement Interface

Retain a dominant evidence profile plus a contextual vector action:

    d_j(c) = a_j(c)b_j + r_j(c),
    P_j r_j(c)=r_j(c),
    Delta_k[a_j b_j+r_j] = Delta_j[a_k b_k+r_k].

The last equality makes the upgrade more than independent tunable weights.
The r terms keep changes in target assignment, and the compatibility constraint
ensures their local actions can belong to one consistent account-level score.
A finite potential F can always enforce this constraint by construction; it
does not yet supply the transferable law for unseen texts.

At author level, integrate qualified observations with choice, condition and
history retained. Do not center away the common part or call reader modulation
the author's personal B_u. Psychological naming requires its own link; this
result changes the architecture of the measurement method without assigning
new personality names to anonymous factors.
