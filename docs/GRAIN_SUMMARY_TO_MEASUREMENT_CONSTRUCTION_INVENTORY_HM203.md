## 2. Keep the construction levels separate

For an evidence set X_u,retain owners,event identities,occasion,condition and
observation regime. Qualified event extraction Q and a frozen encoder F give
an observed event family E_u=F(Q(X_u)),not a catalog of interchangeable vectors.

HM203 currently adds a target-conditional observation

    V_q(x)=B_q(x)+I_q(x),
    D_q(x;W)=R_q(x,p_native(x),W).

R retains body paths,neural final recurrence and target-conditioned p. Even if
one common increment works well,that is not proof that Ibar alone predicts all
targets:the reader still sees x through other inputs. The operation is useful
without pretending these inputs disappeared.

The measurement level instead requires a declared source law and reference,
for example the existing observable-anchor contract

    psi_q(H)=E_source[Y_q | H,regime,opportunity],
    b_q=E_source[psi_q(H)],
    s_q(H)=psi_q(H)-b_q.

Here H is the supplied qualified evidence;Y_q must be separately specified.
For a natural questionnaire anchor,Y_q is the documented score/axis and its
author join. For a behavior anchor,it is the recorded behavior and opportunity.
For a native-model anchor,it is a model response,not human truth. Free selection,
conditional response and state remain available parts of H rather than being
deleted to obtain a tidier score.

The positive development target is a compiler from a frozen source law to a
numeric reader on NEW evidence. It may use a neural text encoder,but must not
need an LLM to reason again over every target. Any approximation must be named;
a covariance witness is not renamed a conditional mean merely because it is
correlated with one.

## 4. Mathematical guidance and honest novelty

[Geiger et al.,2025,Sections2.2-2.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2301.04709v4)
distinguish maps between states from maps between mechanism transformations.
This guides matching an evidence operation to a high-level rule,rather than
judging only unmanipulated answer agreement. HM203 does not establish a complete
causal abstraction of the native network or of human psychology.

[OpenAI/math posterior foundations,lines49-116 and166-232](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/The-exact-reconstruction-threshold-for-the-three-state-symmetric-channel-September-25-2026/build/sections/02-foundations.tex)
make the observation law and posterior coordinates explicit. Sufficiency there
comes from that law;it is not a consequence of pooling arbitrary vectors.
Conditional Jensen compares observations under an input-independent degradation.
The native p in HM203 remains body-dependent and the averaging operation is not
that established posterior experiment. No tree reconstruction theorem applies
automatically to our target contacts.

The useful research question is therefore constructive:which source-observed
target function and evidence operations can the new reader support? Its answer
can be locally useful without universal sufficiency,perfect fidelity or a new
mathematical theorem. Originality must reside in an added measurement construction
or a discriminating empirical mechanism,not in relabeling known projection,
conditional probability,causal abstraction or task-vector ideas.
