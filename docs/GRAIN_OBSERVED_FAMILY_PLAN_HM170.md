   scores relate to the SAME author's later observations. This supplies an

   result; do not rescue it by choosing new axes/partitions or adding sourceN.

Every role/window/endpoint is retained. Report author score dispersion, empirical
early/late correspondence, early score/later observed endpoint correspondence,
per-event Brier against both comparators and complete source support. Accuracy is
a diagnostic, not a promotion objective. Source fit is construction, not validation.

permission to delete choice. Report conditional two-sided Monte Carlo tail ranks;
they assume stratum exchangeability and are exploratory, not new psychometric
significance or independent event counts. Keep all negatives and both components.
