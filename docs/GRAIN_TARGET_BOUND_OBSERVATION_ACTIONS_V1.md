## Object and observation stages

Let z=(x,y,g) be a complete designed evidence cube in {-1,+1}^3. The focal
behavior x always belongs to A. The companion behavior has polarity y; g
assigns it to A or B while balancing the neutral anchor owner. Thus g edits
ownership/context, not a pure external instrument. Six fixed queries q ask
about Big Five targets and anthropomorphism. Named behavior-target matches
are material design identities, not human construct validity.

Keep separate observation laws:

    F_body(z), F_query,q(z), L_q(z), S_q(z), O_q(z).

They denote the complete-body endpoint, post-query endpoint, centered answer
logits, bounded candidate-answer score, and high/low log odds. The body prefix
is identical across its six future queries. Query-independent body values
follow the causal-mask design; they are not an empirical personality finding.
All endpoints see the full body, but presentation reversal still changes the
neural observation. Equal information availability does not imply equal states.

## Lossless account, not denoising

For each stage and pair, use the established Boolean character basis:

    c_q,J = (1/8) sum_z chi_J(z) F_q(z)
    cbar_J = (1/6) sum_q c_q,J
    d_q,J = c_q,J - cbar_J
    F_q(z) = sum_J chi_J(z) (cbar_J + d_q,J).

All eight characters, the reference level, shared actions and query-varying
actions are retained. The six-query weighting is this experiment's reference,
not a unique psychological decomposition. Shared signals are not labeled noise.

The exact polarity-by-ownership contrast at fixed focal polarity x is:

    A_q(x) = F_q(x,+,+) - F_q(x,-,+)
             - F_q(x,+,-) + F_q(x,-,-)
           = 4 (c_q,yg + x*c_q,xyg).

Its equally weighted x-marginal is 4*c_q,yg. It is a controlled textual contrast,
not a causal effect of human personality or a single-token binding intervention.
The third-order term retains how one behavioral context changes another's
ownership action. Do not replace it with independent trait weights.

## Finite findings that change the next decision

All6336 frozen HM68/HM71 captures,132 complete cubes and1056 unique body-prefix
documents were used; no new model calls or fitted map. Four material banks show
positive aggregate matched-minus-other score actions:

    source0 .091654; source1 .065595;
    new forward .076729; new reverse .120510.

This distinguishes a content-matched target component from a solely generic
query-invariant evaluation. Off-target effects persist; the matched target is
the strongest column action in only2-3 of6 cases. Anthropomorphism has negative
matched actions in3 of4 banks. The interface must represent mixed/shared,
target-conditioned and context-dependent actions rather than six pure gates.

Order changes raw geometry more strongly than it changes the observed target
action. New-forward versus new-reverse full yg cosines are .269514 at body,
.587437 after query, .890272 at centered logits and .847563 at score. For xyg
they are .006498, .051139, .383811 and .254483. These are different observables,
not an accuracy ladder or common-unit amplification factor. They motivate
testing functionally relevant directions before interpreting Euclidean drift
as loss of psychological meaning. The underlying causal computation is not
localized by these endpoint comparisons.

Neutral-source actions are much smaller but nonzero. Controls are diagnostic
references, not quantities to silently subtract. No cold neutral bank exists.
Finite contextual sign reversals are saved rather than averaged away.

## Bridge to an author measurement law

For an author evidence distribution P_u on this designed support, the exact
finite expectation identity is:

    Theta_u,q = E_Pu[F_q(z)]
              = sum_J m_u,J (cbar_J + d_q,J),
    m_u,J = E_Pu[chi_J(z)].

It separates a common observation law from individual evidence composition.
Preserving joint moments matters when ownership/context interactions exist.
This identity is standard conditional expectation, not identification of a
human trait. P_u contains observed exposure, choice, reported events and their
relations; it is not automatically an opportunity-corrected preference or a
personal response operator B_u. Scores remain query/material-dependent until
an empirical observation transport is established.

## Next mechanism, not a precision repair

The next bounded design should ask whether the content-matched ownership
action can be carried by a separately executable, event-qualified vector
operation and retain consequences for author evidence composition. Competing
accounts are an entity-binding operation, broad query/evaluative routing and
presentation-dependent context. Distinguish them using a frozen observer and
predeclared interventions/consequences, not a prompt/head/rank accuracy search.
This is a scientific question only; HM73 does not launch the next acquisition.
