## 1. Four different links

    assigned persona P,style S,task C,randomness U -> generated article X
    X -> fixed representation V_R
    V_R -> fixed target functional f_k
    qualified repeated human evidence -> psychological interpretation

The first three are computationally observable here; the fourth is not
supplied by generator prompts. A stable or reversible neural carrier need not
have a selectively diagnostic interpretation (HM145/146). A large source
witness norm does not establish cold meaning (HM147). A finite control span
does not define universal removable noise (HM149).

The actual frozen GRAIN4 package uses word TF-IDF/SVD followed by a factor
posterior and four lexical slices. This is separate from text-only Qwen
input embeddings and contextual block16 states. The observer must always
be part of a definition of a measurement, rather than silently interchanged.

## 2. A conditioned response, not an invariant axis by assumption

For fixed observation Psi_R and generator law, define

    d_(k,c,s) = E[Psi_R(X)|P=k+,C=c,S=s]
                - E[Psi_R(X)|P=k-,C=c,S=s].

The universal additive-realization hypothesis is d_(k,c,s)=d_k for every
task and style. A weaker hypothesis permits d_(k,c,s)=d_(k,c), with a
task-conditioned but style-additive response. The new actual corners identify

    I_(k,c,s)=d_(k,c,s)-d_(k,c,neutral).

This makes three previously confused outcomes distinct: location changes but
the response remains; a context-specific response changes; an arbitrary
nonlinear observation manufactures an interaction from additive vectors.
There is no basis for deleting the conditioned component on grounds that
the instruction was about style rather than personality.

Three fixed seed realizations approximate each d/I. They do not identify a
population law with certainty. Cross-seed inner products retain signed
directional support; squared energy alone also counts random realization.
The unit remains specific to the frozen representation. No hidden-unit norm
is a psychological information percentage.

## 3. Nonlinear measuring tools can create the interaction they report

Take actual vectors h_s,h_0,l_s,l_0, let e=h_s-h_0-l_s+l_0 and
h_s*=h_0+l_s-l_0. For an arbitrary fixed nonlinear observation F,

    F(h_s)-F(h_0)-F(l_s)+F(l_0)
      = [F(h_s*)-F(h_0)-F(l_s)+F(l_0)]
        +[F(h_s)-F(h_s*)].

The first bracket can be nonzero even when e=0. The second vanishes when e=0,
but can itself vanish for nonzero e if F is insensitive to that direction.
The opposite-anchor identity is also retained; no unique causal allocation
is implied by choosing one completion. These completions are numeric
diagnostics, NOT new naturally generated texts.

For the current four-slice tensor Phi and linear covector w, F=w.Phi is
frozen by HM147. Measuring e in the16 base coordinates and the two brackets
in all three old scoring arms prevents a scalar success from being mistaken
for a discovered generative interaction. This is standard finite-difference
and function-composition algebra, not a new theorem.

## 4. Consequences for constructing text-based measurements

If a conditional response repeats, the reusable object may be a family of
target observation functions indexed by conditions, not one invariant
direction that survives every text environment. Transport must retain the
conditions and demonstrated response support. It cannot be established by
flipping cold-task polarity after inspection.

This does not discard condition selection: a person's choice of C/S can
carry information alongside responses conditional on C/S. Nor does it
identify a stable human trait or imply state is noise. Choice, expression,
temporal state, evidence qualification and reference population remain
distinct parts of GRAIN's measurement-construction spine.

The next scientific decision should follow the actual outcomes: base-vector
modulation suggests a conditional realization mechanism; score-only
interaction suggests an observation-law issue; seed-incoherent effects
suggest stochastic realization rather than a reproducible direction. None
calls for an automatic classifier, prompt sweep or precision rescue.

## 6. A field is not yet a recovered individual

Hu,Niu and Varma2026, sections2--5 and7, studies context-dependent concept
displacements and their relational geometry:
https://arxiv.org/html/2607.04525v1 . This is relevant prior work, not evidence
that our conditional-field proposal is unprecedented. Their transport test
uses the test concept's displacement in a source model to construct weights.
It is cross-model transport of an observed relation, not recovery of an
unknown author's psychological parameter from a neutral text alone.

HM125 already separated source origins, coordinate mixing and action outside
a reference span; HM126/127 tested transport and response observability.
The present joint materials supply a missing observation, not rediscovery of
those identities. The key comparison after HM150 is between three mechanisms:

    additive offset: h_s(theta)=h_0(theta)+b_s;
    transported expression: h_s(theta)=A_s h_0(theta)+b_s;
    condition-specific realization: h_s(theta)=T_s(h_0(theta),theta,c).

The second already has a nonzero mixed persona/style contrast when A_s is not
the identity. Thus Gamma!=0 alone does NOT select the third or show a change
in latent personality. For an invertible known A_s, a linear target covector
transports as w_s=A_s^(-T)w_0, preserving its values. For a singular A_s, only
covectors in range(A_s^T) are accessible. These are established duality and
identifiability results, not a new theorem. An unknown A_s cannot be repaired
by application-label fitting and then called source-supported transport.

Even a text-only hidden vector is an observation Psi_R(X), not intrinsic
personality. Conditional contrasts at that observation can differ from those
at lexical GRAIN, and token averaging adds a further nonlinear normalization.
Convergent effects across observations help localize a computational effect;
they do not remove the need for author eligibility and construct interpretation.

No operator/metric/rank fitting was added to HM150. Its complete actual
corners provide conditional-response observations, not a uniquely identified
transport law. Avoid automatic accuracy rescue or an assumption that every
observed interaction requires a new nonlinear theory.

## 7. Signed support is a mechanism diagnostic, not an information fraction

For a fixed observation, the three-seed cross product U estimates directional
agreement; its sample value may be negative. This must remain distinct from
nonnegative squared norms and from the number of positive experimental cells.
The article's use of unbiased HSIC also does not give a finite-sample guarantee
that normalized cross-alignment lies in[0,1]: an unbiased cross-HSIC can be
negative even for positive semidefinite kernels. This is a mathematical caveat,
not evidence against the article's actual numerical results. GRAIN retains
signed statistics instead of relabeling a negative contribution as zero noise.
