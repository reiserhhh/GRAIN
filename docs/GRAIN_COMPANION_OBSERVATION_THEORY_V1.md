## 2. A mixed action is an observation of conditional valuation

Write F_q(A,B) for the frozen native reading of a text union with A before B.
Define A0 as control-deleted and A1 as selected-deleted; B0 is the fixed cyclic
foreign companion and B1 the actual same-author companion. Then

    I_q = F_q(A1,B1)-F_q(A0,B1)-F_q(A1,B0)+F_q(A0,B0).

Pure A effects and pure B effects cancel exactly for an additive function.
This contrast therefore observes whether the effect of focal evidence depends
on the supplied companion. It does not determine why it depends: coherence,
position, conditions, writing habits and target evidence are possible causes.
Foreign B is an audit reassignment, not a naturally observed author's history.
Both original view directions are retained, with the edited view always first.

The frozen source witness phi_q(A,B)=a'T_q b has mixed action

    I_math,q = (a1-a0)'T_q(b1-b0).

Equivalently, the companion changes the focal covector from T_q b0 to T_q b1.
Thus the bridge tests a **change of evidence valuation**, not whether a
larger embedding norm or a more accurate fitted regressor predicts a label.
The source vectors have two fixed orientations; v=1 uses the transposed
focal action, not a new operator fitted to that direction.

HM207 joint/profile/pair and HM242 total/within/between covariance accounts
remain distinct instruments with different reference weights. All signed
cross-target contacts are retained. Neither an operator fit nor a native
answer is used to assign source target meaning.

## 3. Nonlinear readout and representation can both contribute

For final normalized decoder states h00,h10,h01,h11, form

    h_add=h10+h01-h00,
    c=h11-h_add.

For the actual fixed digit head f, the exact anchored account is

    I_reader=f(h_add)-f(h10)-f(h01)+f(h00),
    I_representation=f(h11)-f(h_add),
    I_head=I_reader+I_representation.

The reader is high-digit versus low-digit log odds, not the logit transform
of an ordinal expected score. A nonlinear reader may interact even when c=0;
a nonzero c may be invisible to that reader. Keep their signed cross product.
Native logit output and CPU head evaluation are separately accounted for,
without shrinking the old numerical tolerance. Common digit-logit shifts
cancel in the reader; centered mixed candidate logits observe representation
interaction before that scalar normalization.

The partition is anchor- and interface-dependent, not two uniquely identified
psychological mechanisms. h_add may not be a natural encoder state. No new
decoder patch is run to make it one. HM156 already established this account;
the present empirical question concerns aligned natural-text endpoint inputs.
