## Scientific distinction

HM93 separates a directed evidence law from the instrument reading it. Directed
current can still arise from a shared response to an ordered environment, without
an author-specific dynamic rule. Preserve BOTH condition choice and the remaining
conditional response, rather than deleting the former as noise.

Let c index a declared observation opportunity. On a source-frozen state space,
let Q_u(c) be its occurrence share, R_u(i,j|c) its conditional pair law, and R_0
an independent source reference. Then

    J_u = sum_c Q_u(c) R_0(c) + sum_c Q_u(c)[R_u(c)-R_0(c)]
        = J_u^opportunity + J_u^response.

The skew/Hodge cycle projection is linear. Therefore BOTH terms contribute to
author circulation. They need not be orthogonal; save the cross term. Response
is a signed reference difference, not a probability law or automatically a trait.

## Toy mechanism

Three condition-dependent reset kernels are individually reversible. A forward
condition schedule, its reverse, and an unordered schedule have the same condition
occupancy but different observable cycle laws. Conditional emissions have NO
personal memory. Exact complete-condition accounting leaves zero response;
coarsening away the conditions fabricates a response current. A separate planted
conditional cycle, at identical opportunity law, must survive the accounting.

This is a finite probabilistic counterexample, NOT a reproduction of continuous
time stochastic-pump rates or a violation/application of the no-pumping theorem.
