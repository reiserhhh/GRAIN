## Competing mechanisms and scientific decision

Can body-only vector connectivity deliver an actor-conditioned observation law,
or does a scalar propagation law mainly preserve narrative position/reporting?
Each behavior is reported by the opposite named person. Two grammatical frames
put reporter first or actual actor first. Reverse event order independently.
Every actor swap preserves both person-name counts and the token bag. Thus raw
content availability and naming frequency cannot by themselves settle the role.

Six targets x two frames x two orders x 16 full (x,y,g,h) corners = 384 fresh
public, hand-constructed bodies. x/y are the two behavior polarities; g/h specify
their action subjects. They are analysis truth only, never inputs to graph arms.
Root labels A/B come from literal name-token locations, not role labels. Three
declared event groups are design-supplied boundaries, not discovered syntax.

## Fixed mathematical construction

Capture blocks0--16 once per body and full layer17 Q/K/V projections; terminate
before its attention output, MLP, later blocks, norm and answer head. No suffix
question in input. Existing HM87 neutral query seeds and HM86 source-calibrated
behavior covectors remain fixed. No new fit, model generation, API or private data.

From mean-of-all12-head body QK attention, exclude self loops and normalize on
strictly earlier body tokens. First body token is unresolved boundary; all literal
A/B name tokens are respective absorbing boundaries. A directed harmonic law is

    H_B = boundary one-hots; H_I = T_II H_I + T_IB H_B.

Because T_II is strictly lower triangular, its finite path expansion and forward
recursion agree exactly. H has A/B/unresolved channels; do not silently call it
an exact neural attribution. It is an artificial within-layer observation graph,
not multilayer attention rollout or a fitted psychological posterior.

Compare seven prespecified arms: cold kernel, one-step named-root allocation,
global harmonic allocation, event-local harmonic allocation, position-only
harmonic control, designed actor-event oracle, designed reporter-event oracle.
The last two are diagnostic ceilings/alternatives, not deployable algorithms.
Position control uses uniform strict causal links. No thresholds or best arm
chosen from application outputs. Each positive A mass gates the fixed target
kernel p0; normalization supplies both event mass and within-event token law.
Keep unresolved absorption and removed self mass as separate observed quantities.

action-span A-minus-B absorption against g/h: this is role-sensitive transport,
not human personality accuracy. Compute readout high-low effects conditional on
