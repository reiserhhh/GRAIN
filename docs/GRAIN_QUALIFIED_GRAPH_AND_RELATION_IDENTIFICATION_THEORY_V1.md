# Qualified evidence graphs and relational identification

HM113 companion, 2026-10-10. An executable observation-law construction, not a
new general mathematical theorem or a completed personality measure.

## 1. Three objects that must not be conflated

Reports R deliver atoms and supplied relations. G(R) is the qualified evidence
graph. K_q is the shared vector observation kernel inherited from HM112.
An author's conditional observation is

    Theta_uq(c) = (1/n_uc) sum_{e in E_uc} K_q(x_e,c).
    Theta_uq = sum_c pi_u(c) Theta_uq(c).

The graph determines which evidence belongs to the same episode; the instrument
determines how a supported joint configuration is read. pi retains empirical
condition choice. K's cross-material coefficients are not assumed universal.
No individual psychological parameter is defined to equal K's nonlinear effect.

The implementation supports only supplied complete six-slot units on HM112's
fixed binary triple support. Unknown actor/episode/condition/state are explicit
unavailable observations. Partial units remain in an unresolved ledger rather
than become six fabricated zeros. A future partial-unit instrument requires its
own support/observation rule, not default deletion of natural partial evidence.

## 2. Packaging equivalence is not behavioral equivalence

If two deliveries preserve the exact qualified atoms and supplied episode
relations, graph-channel scoring should be identical. Reversing delivery,
changing network packets or forwarding an exact duplicate is not a new event.
That property follows from the join/deduplication construction; zero differences
alone are not an empirical psychological discovery.

Actual utterance order, reformulation, style, timing and deliberate repetition
may carry behavioral information. Do NOT erase them as packaging. The current
packet changes numeric report envelopes, not natural wording, event-time edges
or author expression choices. GRAIN's expression/choice/state channels and HM111
signed contextual routes remain separate retained observations. This invariant
evidence channel does not replace them or reconstruct the full11 model.

## 3. A measured relational counterworld

Within each bank/triple, P+/minus=(1+/- .4 chi)/8, chi=x1*x2*x3. The240 atom IDs
and values match exactly across the two40-episode designs; episode assignment
differs. All unary and value-pair distributions agree, but actual individual
atom-pair edge identities need not. The supplied graph is not learned from data.

Using the real frozen numeric kernel, these laws have different author readouts.
This is a conditional evidence-composition effect in a vector instrument, not
an identified human trait. An encounter-order zipper manufactures a different
joint law when reports are independently reordered, even without new evidence.
The relation-aware scorer avoids that artifact while retaining the nonzero
effect of genuinely changed supplied relations.

## 4. Missing relation information has a mathematical signature

Known constant/unary/pair character moments give A p=b with rank7 on8 cells.
Its positive completion set is exactly

    p_t(x)=(1+t chi(x))/8, -1<=t<=1.

For each frozen q, define b0_q=mean(K_q) and b3_q=mean(chi*K_q). Then

    Theta_q(t)=b0_q+t*b3_q.
    I_q=[b0_q-|b3_q|, b0_q+|b3_q|].

The uncertainty is the product of an instrument response b3 and an unknown
author relation t. Better unary/pair precision cannot determine t, even in the
infinite-data limit, because every p_t has identical supplied lower moments.
If b3=0, this target does not require that third relation; the third-removed
kernel negative control realizes that case. Thus missing links are not always
equally important for every measurement target.

Erased episode links alone permit only atomic/unary summaries. Pair constraints
used in the stronger bound are an ADDITIONAL retained relation summary from the
controlled graph, not something recoverable freely from the unordered atoms.
Adding the aggregate third moment identifies the readout only within this fixed
eight-cell family. It does not establish a universal finite sufficient statistic.

## 5. Shared relational ambiguity across queries

For a fixed bank/triple, all six query ambiguities share the SAME t:

    boldTheta(t)=boldb0+t*boldb3.

Their scalar interval endpoints cannot all be chosen independently; feasible
joint readouts lie on a segment, not the entire Cartesian product of intervals.
This follows from the common author relation law, not from psychological-factor
correlation. The instrument chooses the direction boldb3; the author relation
chooses location t. Different supports/conditions can have other dimensions.
The present independent per-target LPs give correct marginal bounds, not a claim
that every combination of their extrema is jointly attainable.

## 6. What the experiment changes

HM112 made K executable from independent event carriers. HM113 makes its author
input an explicit relation structure rather than accidental report adjacency.
The missing information is now actionable: preserve episode linkage or collect
the needed joint relation summary; do not keep increasing score precision.
The next development should determine which supplied relation types have
behavioral meaning and how their conditional distributions change. Natural
episode extraction, observer calibration and individual response/state remain
open mechanisms, not silently assigned to humans or counted as solved.

## 7. Mathematical ancestry

Provenance-preserving relational algebra predates this system:
[Green et al.2007](https://www.cs.ucdavis.edu/~green/papers/pods07.pdf),pp1--3.
Marginal consistency/extension is a classical question:
[Vorob'ev1962](https://www.mathnet.ru/php/archive.phtml?jrnid=tvp&option_lang=eng&paperid=4710&wshow=paper).
HM62/HM78 already implement sharp finite moment bounds in this project. Standard
LP duality, character orthogonality and evidence-graph joins are not new math.
OpenAI/math pinned06-matrix complementary observation families give a design
analogy; its PDE/harmonic-jet theorem is not a theorem about this kernel.

Candidate contribution is the linked, executable GRAIN construction: qualified
evidence relations, common vector instrument, author composition and explicit
information-preservation consequences. New psychological conclusions require
the corresponding measured evidence, not a new name for these existing tools.
