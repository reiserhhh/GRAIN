## Fixed bookkeeping construction

For source control j at the same task/seed as its neutral observation, define

    b_j = Phi(z_control,j) - Phi(z_neutral,j).
    B = stack_j b_j; C = row_span(B).

One SVD with relative singular tolerance 1e-10 defines an orthonormal row basis
Q. The tolerance is arithmetic, not a parameter search or discovered factor
count. For each unchanged frozen covector w:

    w_overlap = (w Q^T) Q;
    w_complement = w - w_overlap;
    f(x) = <w_overlap,Phi(x)> + <w_complement,Phi(x)>.

No new normalization. Both parts survive in every score/report. On source
controls, complement contraction is zero BY CONSTRUCTION, not an empirical
success. New-task controls need not have that property. The overlap is a finite
empirical response span, not a group orbit, universal nuisance tangent space,
low-dimensional psychological factor or uniquely isolated causal component.
The span depends on the declared kernel and source units.

For targets E and N, retain signed Gram components:

    <w_E,w_N> = <P_C w_E,P_C w_N> + <(I-P_C)w_E,(I-P_C)w_N>.

Cross terms vanish. Normalized cosines cannot be added. Source coefficient
energy fractions are NOT percentages of personality or available information.
Orthogonality to these controls does not certify personality specificity.

## Mathematical ancestry and novelty limit

[Scharf and Friedlander, 1994, Section III](https://www.mssc.mu.edu/~daniel/mscs6960/spring2010/ScharfFriedlanderTSP94.pdf)
provides established signal/interference subspace projection geometry. Their
known subspaces, Gaussian error and detector optimality assumptions do NOT
hold here; no GLRT or UMP claim is imported. We preserve overlap instead of
declaring it interference and deleting it.
[Gretton et al., 2012](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v13/gretton12a.html) provides
kernel means/witness functions, not automatic psychological interpretation.
OpenAI/math fixed adc7f124 Universal-Tensor-Squares preliminaries21-100 was
reread for dual tests sensitive to components. Its Specht/Schur-Weyl theorem
does not apply to GRAIN psychology. Only that design perspective is used.

The potential contribution is an empirical explanation and a construction rule:
target anchoring must be distinguished from sensitivity to other controlled
text transformations. Projection algebra itself is mature mathematics.
