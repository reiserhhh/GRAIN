## 2. Two nested conditioning families

For a complete path X, let T(X) be all distinct state words with the same
directed transition counts, first edge and last edge. Endpoints, length and
state occupancy then also agree. Let O(X) be unchanged HM265's return-block
orbit, fixing prefix/suffix plus boundary excursions and permuting interior
excursions. O(X) is contained in T(X); the O-orbits partition T(X).

For a local triple measure M and frozen SOURCE field psi, define:

    S(X)=<M_X,psi>
    A(X)=<E_uniform_O(X) M,psi>
    C(X)=<E_uniform_T(X) M,psi>
    Q(X)=A(X)-C(X)
    D(X)=S(X)-A(X)
    S=C+Q+D; A=C+Q.

C retains observed choices/counts/boundary conditions. Q captures the finer
excursion configuration relative to this reference, NOT all possible
nonlinear interactions. D captures the remaining restricted-orbit order.
All parts remain usable and reconstruct the raw score. State and choice are
not automatically removed or redefined as noise.

For any unknown homogeneous Markov kernel P, the likelihood of each member
of T(X) is initial(x0)*product_ij P_ij^Nij. Hence this declared conditional
reference is uniform without estimating P. Mixtures retain the equality.
Changing states, informative selection or higher history can violate this
competitor. We did not establish that natural human text obeys this law.

The conditional tower E[A|T]=C and E[D|O]=0 are exact under the declared
uniform family law. They do not guarantee orthogonality in the actual
population. For two natural windows, use all signed terms:

    Cov(S0,S1)=sum_{U,V in {C,Q,D}} Cov(U0,V1).

These terms are algebraic covariance accounts, not psychological variance
fractions. Negative/mixed terms are retained. A field f(a,b)+g(b,c) has
Q=D=0 because both cropped pair margins are fixed by the family.

## 3. Exact complete-family calculation

Take the interior graph N=count(X[1:-1]), endpoints s=X1,t=X[-2]. Denote
distinct Euler word count by W(N;s,t), using the existing finite BEST/count
implementation. For each marked interior triple abc, replace its two edges
by one marked ac edge in N'=N-Eab-Ebc+Eac. This is a bijection between:

    original word + marked abc occurrence
    contracted word + marked ac edge occurrence.

Therefore the summed abc count across the family is W(N';s,t)*N'_ac.
Sequential deletions enforce availability when edge types repeat. Removing
a first or last interior edge gives the summed boundary triples. The exact
three-event special case contributes its one observed triple. Total triple
mass equals (len(X)-2)*W(N;s,t). Parallel edges are not new observations.

The instrument supports three-to-eighteen events and rejects larger inputs
explicitly, without truncating. This is the registered finite instrument
scope, not a theoretical claim that longer text cannot be measured.

A designed indicator for triple(1,0,2) on two ten-event words gives:

    (S,A,C,Q,D)=(1/8,1/16,1/24,1/48,+1/16)
    (S,A,C,Q,D)=(0,1/16,1/24,1/48,-1/16).

The full family has6 words and the fine orbit2. Configuration and order can
be separate nonzero observations even though all counts/edges match. The
example's target is designed, not discovered human psychology.

is transferred to this human-text sample.
