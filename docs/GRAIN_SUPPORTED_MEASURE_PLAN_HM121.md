equal token count: genuine available evidence mass is retained.

The attention numerator and denominator are linear in history mass at fixed
current Q. Its mass derivative is exp(logit_i)*(v_i-attended)/denominator;
current Q and the later recurrence also respond. Full autograd includes these
recursions; it is not a bag-of-words approximation.

An endpoint-invisible representative change leaves nu_l,t unchanged for all t.

the recurrence, including positions. This is established measure arithmetic,
not a new theorem or assertion of natural-text/on-manifold interpolation.
