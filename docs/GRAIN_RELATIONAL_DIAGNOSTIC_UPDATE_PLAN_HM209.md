```text
J_g(a,b)=mean_(i:g) K(a,z_iA) K(b,z_iB)
I_g(a,b)=mean_(i:g) K(a,z_iA) * mean_(j:g) K(b,z_jB)

P_J(g|a,b)=P_I(g|a,b)*exp(d_g)/sum_h P_I(h|a,b)*exp(d_h)
```
