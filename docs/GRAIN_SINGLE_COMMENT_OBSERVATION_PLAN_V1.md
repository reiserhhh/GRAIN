## Exact fibers and finite empirical projection

For each role (whole cohort, source, held), partition exact float64 g rows by
numeric equality; +0/-0 are equal, nonfinite inputs fail. Keep every class,
including singleton classes. Report exact h duplicates separately.

Use equal-author mass, with equal comments within an author, and report a
comment-uniform companion. Let E_k be an exact-g class, with mass p_k:

    m_k = sum_{i in E_k} p_i psi_i / p_k
    r_i = psi_i - m_k
    inf_f sum_i p_i ||psi_i - f(g_i)||^2 = sum_i p_i ||r_i||^2.

Verify the Pythagorean identity for an arbitrary fixed class-constant function,
not a fitted accuracy contestant. The fraction versus total centered variance
is a finite proxy-information diagnostic, NOT a personality variance fraction,
population noise floor, confidence interval or universal Bayes risk.

Per class/query retain extrema addresses and raw gaps. Numerical certification
uses only a fixed float64 dot-product roundoff envelope
8*gamma_d*(abs(h)@abs(W)); exact-g grouping never uses that envelope.
Record raw nonconstancy separately from gaps greater than the envelope.
For a two-point class, any deterministic g-only output has worst absolute
error at least half the gap. Two distributions (.75,.25) and (.25,.75) have
identical distributions of g (all its moments), but target means differ by
half the gap. These are reweighting witnesses, not newly observed people.
