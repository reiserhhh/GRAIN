## Local Reconstruction

The raw post-block states H have observed moments m=OH. Given one reference's
native-score gradient g=dF/dH, with all other states c held fixed, the unique
four-moment covector is

    lambda(H,c,q)=O^-T g(H,c,q),
    dF_(H,c,q)[Delta H]=<lambda(H,c,q),O Delta H>.

H alone is not presumed to determine the action when unpatched context states
c differ. The expression retains that explicit conditioning variable.

## Two Different Ways Averaging Can Mislead

E[g] can be small by signed cancellation even when E[g g^T] is nonzero.
Conversely, large derivative energy can prioritize rapid low-amplitude changes
over a slowly varying high-amplitude function. These are different questions.

For independent standard Gaussian X1,X2, take the classical illustrative
f(X)=5X1+sin(k X2), k=10*pi. Direct analytic Gaussian moments give

    C11=25,
    C12=5*k*exp(-k^2/2),
    C22=(k^2/2)*(1+exp(-2*k^2)).

Keeping only X1 has mean-square remainder .5*(1-exp(-2*k^2)); keeping only X2
has remainder25. Large derivative energy in X2 does not make it the best
one-coordinate approximation of function values. This uses analytic moments,
not a transplanted numerical quadrature value or a claim that text is Gaussian.
See Constantine et al., https://arxiv.org/html/1606.01929, Sections2-4.1.

Therefore retain signed actions, finite-scale changes, and conditional fields;
do not call mean-zero directions noise or maximal-gradient directions personality.
Neither an eight-source empirical spectrum nor a rank4 positional map identifies
four psychological factors.
