## Mathematics

In L2(R), P is the source observation projection, E is conditional expectation
given binary c, and M is multiplication by c. These are DIFFERENT operators.
For mean-zero/unit-norm c and v=||Pc||^2:

    EPc = v*c; ||(I-E)Pc||^2 = v*(1-v).

Including c makes range(E) a subspace of range(P); E and P then commute.
It does NOT in general make P commute with M. The conditional-product space is
closed under M and has that stronger property. Report both commutators, source
target visibility, and choice/within off-diagonal blocks. This is standard
projection theory applied to an actual GRAIN observation law, not a new theorem.
