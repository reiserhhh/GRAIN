## Frozen function and competing predictions

The common request is a possible first-person self-description by the writer
of the sample. All candidates start after the same assistant `I` prefix; each
public statement is mechanically lowercased at its first character and given
a leading space, with no other semantic rewrite. Complete10-item keys for all
five factors are copied from HM251; Emotional Stability is oriented to N and
Intellect/Imagination is the O anchor, not equivalence to every inventory.

For candidate j, let

    ell_j(X)=sum_t [W_(b_jt).h_jt(X)+bias_(b_jt)-A(h_jt(X))],
    lambda_uj=ell_j(own_u)-ell_j(foreign_u).

For each public key, H/L are its independently given positive/negative poles:

    B_q=(mean_H lambda - mean_L lambda)/2,
    U_q=(mean_H lambda + mean_L lambda)/2,
    Q_q=mean_j k_j lambda = B_q+a_q U_q,
    a_q=(n_H-n_L)/10.

All three remain. U is not noise; an imbalanced Q alone can reflect common
support rather than the balanced behavioral direction. Raw sequence sums are
PRIMARY. Fixed per-candidate token-normalized accounts are explanatory and
never selected instead. Candidate length/EOS affects the observed law; paired
contrasts do not eliminate all stylistic/discourse differences.

PRIMARY: all5 pooled FULL-source contacts M_p[B_q], with the complete5x5 table
and whole-author999 marginal bootstrap, seed26120261013. Keep both directions,
SOURCE choice/within and GRAIN15, all item/pole/U/Q readings and negatives.
These are covariance-like correspondence units, not Pearson r/AUC/clinical
accuracy. Do not normalize probabilities over the50 candidates: they are a
finite non-exhaustive set, not a questionnaire answer alphabet. Their complete
event probability mass is a separate descriptive account.

H_contract: native trait-name or binary-answer contracts can obscure a SOURCE
relationship present in these independently keyed predictive observations.
H_source: changing to this forward operational kernel does not recover that
relationship, directing work toward SOURCE meaning/opportunity construction
rather than the reverse reader alone. Mixed target/direction outcomes remain
valid; no required number of positive fields, sign flipping or tuned threshold.
Different prompts are different observation laws, not a clean isolated causal
intervention on wording. A positive contact is not human validity.
