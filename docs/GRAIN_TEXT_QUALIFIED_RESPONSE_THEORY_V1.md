# Qualified text response: marginal changes and mixed carrier innovations

HM104, 2026-10-10. This note defines the measurement object before its empirical
results. It does not identify a natural continuous language manifold or name a
new human personality factor.

## 1. A premise can modify the evidence operator

Let E encode a history followed by a fixed current expression; F_q is the fixed
source target observation of the current K/V carrier. For two content levels
c+/- and explicit owner clauses o=A/B, the four actual encoded texts supply

    I_q = F_q(E(c+,A)) - F_q(E(c-,A))
          - F_q(E(c+,B)) + F_q(E(c-,B)).

I_q is a finite observed interaction, not a differential or a personality score.
If nonzero, this instrument cannot represent the premise effect as an additive
constant independent of the described content on this support. It says what
the fixed observer does; a correct or incorrect attribution is a separate matter.
The subjects/names/identity clause vary together, so it is not a unique pure
psychological ownership intervention.

The carrier interaction W has the same four-corner formula with E in place of F.
Let S7 span six content high-minus-low secants at unknown owner and the A-minus-B
owner secant at mid contents. Write

    W = P_S7 W + (I-P_S7)W.

The second term is an observed mixed innovation relative to the declared marginal
support. It is not noise or an error to eliminate. Retain it as qualified content.
It need not be orthogonal to trait-relevant response directions, and it is not
necessarily a derivative: these are finite discrete texts.

## 2. Encoder interaction and observer nonlinearity are distinct

An affine numerical chart y(x)=x0+P_S7(x-x0) supplies the exact bookkeeping

    delta2 F(x) = delta2 F(y(x)) + delta2 [F(x)-F(y(x))].

The first term allows a nonlinear observer on marginal directions; the second
is the effect of what that chart omitted. Do not equate output interaction with
the existence of a unique interaction vector, or identify a nonzero W with a
psychologically meaningful signal. Add all six observed W_i to form S13 as a
second declared support; no direction is selected by output performance. S13
contains W_i, but does not guarantee all four endpoints or their nonlinear
readout are recovered. A new support is not an automatically better predictor.

This finite first/mixed-order comparison borrows the design logic of a spanning
jet family from OpenAI/math's connection manuscript; its smooth harmonic jets
and PDE hypotheses do not hold for these discrete text secants. No theorem is
transferred. Standard finite differences and orthogonal decompositions suffice.

## 3. Response geometry only on the declared support

For an orthonormal frame B, define F_B(z)=F(x0+Bz). Then

    J_B=J_x B, dJ_B[a]=dJ_x[B a] B.

These are exact chain rules for the numerical observer, not derivatives of the
text encoder. The minimum-norm controller and its Lie bracket inherit the
HM103 definitions with J_B. They now use directions supplied by actual text
contrasts rather than all K/V coordinates. Yet combinations of those directions
may still be off the actual text manifold. A nonzero bracket characterizes
this response construction, not an automatically localized natural circuit.
Square full-rank S6 has no vertical fiber; K-fixed F is linear in V. These known
controls prevent a response construction from being sold as a new theorem.

Makelov et al. (arXiv:2311.17030v2) demonstrate why effective subspace control
need not localize the model's naturally used variable. Starting with actual
encoded endpoints narrows the question; it does not solve the full localization
problem. This is a mechanism distinction, not a new accuracy target.

## 4. Author composition can preserve the qualified relation

In corner order [high/A, low/A, high/B, low/B], fix D=[1,-1,-1,1] and

    P+ = (1 + 0.5 D)/4, P- = (1 - 0.5 D)/4.

Both have the same high/low and A/B marginals. Using the explicit HM102 author
expectation, their score contrast is

    E_P+ F_q - E_P- F_q = 0.25 I_q.

This is a known probability identity. Its purpose is an actual construction
bridge: the same counts can hide different qualified evidence assignments,
and joint observations can retain the difference. These profiles are designed
evidence compositions, not observed human author pairs. Target scores alone do
not establish psychological validity. The companion executable freezes this
law before reading the primary results and checks the scores by two alternate
actual-corner calculations after primary verification.

Next mechanism should be decided by the signed actual interactions and mixed
innovations, not by tuning a teacher approximation, head/layer/rank search or
reading deterministic numerical accuracy as human prediction success.
