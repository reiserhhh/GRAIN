## Implementation decisions fixed before S0

- Five outer/three inner author folds, deterministic hash assignment, both source
  packets treated as development. All views, donors and doses stay with authors.
- Select minimum inner-CV MSE; ranks2/4/8, ridge1/10/100. Responses preserve the
  origin (zero displacement predicts zero); fit no intercept.
- SVD and uncentered PLS directions are orthonormalized, then training RMS-scaled
  before Ridge. Full Ridge uses training coordinate RMS scales. Report the
  comparison as complete estimators, not a pure coordinate-only causal contrast.
- S1 membership and edits are selected without reading response outcomes or
  labels; preparation freezes text/vector provenance before collection.
- If attention/input gradients are expensive, keep the block16 gradient primary.
  No full Fisher, old128-shard queue, new persona generation, or extra label audit.
