    Y_q(x) = logsumexp(logits_5..9) - logsumexp(logits_0..4)
    d_alone = Y(S) - Y(T)

general-personality or social-desirability factor. Neither account supplies

The evidence receipt is therefore indexed by actual events, background,

context-free scalar intrinsic to a text block. No background is deleted from

## A mixed contrast is not automatically nonlinear semantic reasoning

R removal changes length, numbering, position and semantic background
jointly. Even a linear observation can yield this mixed contrast if its
aggregation convention changes. For a sum-of-event reader I=0; for a linear
mean-vector reader with equally sized R and S/T,

    x_(R+S)=(x_R+x_S)/2
    d_with_R=d_alone/2
    I=-d_alone/2

SOURCE A departure +0.025541 alone versus +0.012714 with R is close to this
dilution pattern. This is an interpretation of the frozen SOURCE predictions,
not a newly promoted primary or a fit to native answers. Background effects
must not all be labelled semantic interaction, psychological noise or new
attention mechanisms. SOURCE and native nats belong to different laws.
