## Design and Units

Each of 16 lexical/context families supplies a full five-factor design:
anxiety polarity A, general AI-trust polarity T, anxiety owner U, trust owner V,
and sentence order S. Polarity 1 is the higher/positive expression; owner 0 is
self and 1 is the colleague. Each ownership change swaps the target predicate
with a neutral predicate, so the words do not change. Sentence reversal also
preserves the words. All 32 full cells must be distinct. U and V are separate,
avoiding the alias from globally swapping both polarity assignments.

Additional anxiety-only, trust-only, and neither-content controls preserve
unobserved targets. Single-sentence order duplicates are deliberate exact-text
nulls, not new independent evidence. Totals: 800 records, 656 different texts,
1,968 actual forwards for three queries. Source/validation/application families
8/4/4 are frozen before observations. Contexts, words, functions and queries
are not optimized on outcomes. The direct NA and trust evidence covers a subset
of the query, not an administered composite scale. Dejection is an adjacent
query without direct dejection material in this packet.

## Finite Group Construction

The designed address group is (Z/2Z)^5. For a fixed pair of polarities, the
same-word ownership/order subgroup is generated by U,V,S. This is a group on
the designed materials, not an assumed symmetry of real authors or the LLM.
Lift each actual observer response to its orbit f(g). Characters
chi_J(g)=(-1)^popcount(g&J) give

    c_J=mean_g chi_J(g) f(g),  f(g)=sum_J chi_J(g)c_J.

Retain all 32 components. Group averaging over U destroys every U-odd component,
including A:U binding. In this convention the native binding contrast

    [high-low at self] - [high-low at other] = -4*c_(A:U),

with the other three coordinates averaged. T:V is analogous. Apply the
decomposition separately to centered logits, native scores and the frozen
affine program. Also retain all conditional four-corner link/representation
splits, since nonlinear scoring does not commute with averaging/projection.

This uses standard finite character theory. [Cohen and Welling (2016), Section
4.4](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v48/cohenc16.pdf) motivates lifting responses
to functions on a group. [Finzi et al. (2021), Sections 3-4.2](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/finzi21a/finzi21a.pdf)
clarifies the need to specify input/output actions before demanding equivariance.
We do not train their networks, impose LLM equivariance, or identify a learned
personality representation with a mathematical group representation.

OpenAI/math's pinned hypergraph fourier.tex was reread completely; its product
characters and Parseval accounting inspire keeping, rather than deleting,
components. Its Boolean influence/hypercontractive bounds and natural-language
independence are not assumed. The permutation-transfer foundations.tex was
reread: no S_n mixing, trimming, or zero-psychological-error claim is imported.

## Source-Supported Action

Fit only source-family mean character coefficients. A new family contributes
only its actual cell-zero anchor to a cold completion:

    theta_pred(g)=theta_cold(0)+sum_J c_source(J)[chi_J(g)-chi_J(0)].

Six fixed arms: constant anchor; natural-coordinate main effects; main plus
A:U/T:V binding; all nonconstant components; deliberately reversed binding
signs; scalar main-plus-binding. Main terms are {1,2,4,8,16}, binding adds {5,10}.
No clipping of scalar completions. All arms remain, regardless of outcomes.
The primary binding-versus-main MSE gain independently bootstraps source and
application families 2,000 times. This uses an actual cold anchor, not a
single-text predictor or estimated personal B_u. No validation model selection.
