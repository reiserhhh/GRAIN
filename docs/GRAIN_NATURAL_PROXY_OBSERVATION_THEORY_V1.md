## 3. Three mathematical questions, not one accuracy number

1. Author reproducibility: do separate event collections retain a person's
   language distribution? The observed agreements provide usable evidence.
2. Target association: which reproducible directions link to a criterion?
   Openness has a candidate; general five-target reading is weak here.
3. Measurement transport: do the same observation/error law and target units
   support a common function across contexts/populations? The source moment
   fit alone does not supply this law.

An author may have a stable convention that has nothing to do with the queried
criterion. Conversely, a small target-linked difference can matter while most
reproducible variation reflects other individual choices. These objects should
be kept as separate operators, not forced into one low-error fit objective.

The familiar nonunique-function/identified-functional result remains intact:
the problem in HM253 is not numerical nullity. Source sample invertibility
does not certify population inverse estimation or its target interpretation.
A POST-OUTCOME source-only descriptor whitened both centered view covariances
and computed sample canonical correlations. Minima across six operators are
.00240--.08343, maxima .96324--.97274. Thus strong shared directions coexist
with very weak ones, independently of a simple coordinate-unit rescaling.
This was not used to change scoring, choose ranks or assert population weakness.

## 4. A new observation-law issue to investigate, not a retrospective cause

Disjoint text is not the same as independently generated proxy noise. There
are two different conditioning objects:

    U: an author's event-generating distribution at declared conditions/time;
    F: one finite bank of that author's already collected comments.

Under independent draws GIVEN U, independent-view noise is possible. Given a
fixed finite bank F and a random partition without replacement, the view errors
are negatively coupled. These statements are compatible because conditioning
on F and on U are different. Corrections for the finite-F estimand must not be
silently applied to a U estimand and called denoising.

For an EXPLICIT uniform random partition of N events into k equal views,
let q_e be any fixed event feature vector, mu_F its mean, and

    S_F = sum_e (q_e-mu_F)(q_e-mu_F)^T / (N-1), n=N/k.

Standard finite sampling gives

    Var(phi_v | F) = (1/n-1/N) S_F,
    Cov(phi_v,phi_w | F) = -S_F/N, v!=w,
    sum_v(phi_v-mu_F)=0.

Proof: uniform without-replacement sampling gives the first identity. Equal
view exchangeability and the exact last identity imply the second by taking
Cov(phi_v,sum_w phi_w)=0. Therefore an auxiliary view made from the same bank
can have correlated sampling errors even if every event ID is distinct. This
is mature sampling algebra, not a new personality theorem.

HM253 uses DETERMINISTIC chronological interleaving, not the above random
partition. We have NOT established that this algebra accounts for its target
results. It provides a competing observation mechanism and a next experimental
design: explicitly randomize the partition orbit of the SAME event bank,
separately test the finite-bank and event-generating-distribution estimands,
and retain condition choice/state. No estimated correlation is a proof that
the author's full personality is constant over the approximately900-day bank.

Before a new packet, inventory earlier split/partition/alias studies and
declare what new target-bearing observation capability the proposed design
adds. A sampling correction by itself is not an external-validity bridge. Do
not replace this question with inverse-matrix, dimensionality or Ridge grids.

## 5. Prior art and contribution boundary

[Fu and Green2026, section6.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.21909v3) connects
proxy observations to reference measurement; [Zhang et al2023/v4, prop2.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2303.10134v4)
separates function uniqueness from functional identification. Their assumptions
are not certified by an empirical full-rank matrix. [Hansen1982](https://larspeterhansen.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/Hansen-econometrica-GMM.pdf)
provides moment-estimation ancestry; we do not claim a valid asymptotic J-test.
[OpenAI posterior observation foundations](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/The-exact-reconstruction-threshold-for-the-three-state-symmetric-channel-September-25-2026/build/sections/02-foundations.tex)
requires qualified observation laws before independent evidence composition;
its symmetric tree reconstruction theorem is not transplanted.

GRAIN's present contribution candidate is construction of qualified text
measurement procedures, maintaining choice/conditional response and separating
author geometry from criterion meaning. HM253 adds a natural-data test of a
source-only paired moment compiler plus an unused third view; it does not claim
to invent IV, GMM, author fingerprints or finite-population covariance.
