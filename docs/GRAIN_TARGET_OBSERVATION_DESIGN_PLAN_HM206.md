This is a measurement-construction experiment, not a prediction tuning grid.

## Question, prior inventory and new capability

HM191 already proves that current target scores do not close future evidence
updates. HM175/177/180/185 already cover profile dependence, pair/triple
relations and their source limits. HM192/193 separate conditional relations
from routing. HM204/205 supply a positive common reader and grouping interface.
Repeating those comparisons would not discover a new mechanism.

Here the unfilled capability is choosing WHICH observation to obtain, based
on a SOURCE target link, before seeing that observation's content. Test:

1. A condition that distinguishes empirical source author identities may
   differ from one that distinguishes a specified measured target.
2. Target-specific choices may differ from each other on the same prefix.
3. A positive SOURCE expected value does not guarantee useful new-author
   evidence. Cross-author empirical evaluation must keep signed failures.

The choice is over two existing archive conditions, not new prompts, human
interventions or semantic attention heads. Adaptive testing and Bayesian
experimental design are established. The project addition is a source-bound
numeric acquisition interface and an actual author-disjoint archive check,
not a new general information/variance theorem.

## Exact material and sampling experiment

Use HM192's sealed numeric cells/units/codes/folds:386 SOURCE authors, inherited
203/183 author folds, all five binary labels/full32 profiles, two disjoint
17-event windows. No HM204/205 12-event joins. No raw text, application labels,
private material, model/API/training/Git/Claude/automation/lockbox/old queue.
The historical GRAIN15 map and global cell thresholds were developed before
this test; this is NOT untouched full-pipeline CV or new human data.

For author u, p_u(a) is uniform early-window sampling, a in 0..7. The late
window supplies condition c=b//4 and expression state b%4. Observe only its
availability mask M before the early event, not late fine states/counts.
Then reveal early A, choose one AVAILABLE condition C, and read a uniformly
drawn late event B within C. Availability itself can carry target meaning
and stays in the before score; it is not declared irrelevant or deleted.

This is a retrospective controlled archive-access experiment. Availability
is known from recorded metadata BEFORE fine-content selection. It is NOT a
forecast before the archive exists, a controlled change of a person's topic,
or a natural conditional-iid human-text claim. The product of empirical
window frequencies defines the explicitly randomized archive sampling.

Within calibration fold, U is uniform. At m,a:

    w_u proportional to 1[M_u=m] p_u(a)
    mu_q=sum_u w_u Y_uq
    r_uc(b)=Pr(late fine cell=b | author u, condition c)
    p_c(b)=sum_u w_u r_uc(b)
    mu_qc(b)=sum_u w_u r_uc(b)Y_uq / p_c(b)

The target-risk value is

    V_q(c|m,a)=sum_b p_c(b)[mu_qc(b)-mu_q]^2.

It is the exact reduction in Bayes squared-error risk under THIS source law.
Separate information objectives use I(Y_q;B|m,a,c) and I(U;B|m,a,c).
The target-MI versus identity-MI comparison holds the entropy criterion fixed,
so an objective difference is not confused with using two different losses.
Identity is empirical source address, not a discovered personality class.
No source identity catalogue is needed or exported by the deployed selector.

Freeze six policies: target-risk max, target-MI max, identity-MI max, condition0
if available else1, condition1 if available else0, and uniform available.
Ties choose0; no search, sign rescue, label-threshold/condition refinement.
All five targets are present, no selected winning trait or policy promotion.
Missing source support is unavailable, not a prior-filled neutral response.

## Cross-author evaluation and control

Compile fold0, evaluate fold1, and vice versa. Evaluate by exact enumeration
of the held author's actual early/late frequencies, not repeated synthetic
people. All policies use the SAME SOURCE before/after scores. Held Y is used
only for this internal SOURCE-bank evaluation, not policy choice or fitting.

Primary common support requires valid SOURCE before and after scores for every
held-positive outcome of every available condition. Report lost mass/authors,
do not pretend complete mass if it is not. All six policies share this support.
Store per-author risk/gain contributions. Gain can be negative on held authors.
Source forecast is a construction identity, not evidence that transport works.

One fixed whole-profile permutation within each fold supplies a target-link
control. Build its policies on shuffled calibration profiles and evaluate
against original opposite-fold Y. This is descriptive, not a permutation p
value; no additional permutations or seeds are selected after observing it.

## Capability and verification

The cold selector takes only availability, an addressed early cell and a target
index. It recommends condition probabilities and explicit support. It cannot
generate a prompt or authenticate authorship. No target label/native answer is
allowed in its input. Scores and aggregate joint-profile laws stay available.

Tests: a toy fingerprint-only condition beats a target-only condition for
identity MI but not target MI/risk; a null target has zero value; source-law
evaluation equals predicted risk reduction; inverted held labels produce a
negative gain; absent menu/source states stay missing; source-event IDs must
be distinct across windows. One alternate literal per-author/event audit
suffices. No arithmetic polishing after the decision is resolvable.

Stages prepare/build/evaluate/verify once, exclusive new directory, CPU only.
Freeze build BEFORE cross-author evaluation. Preserve all targets, policies,
author folds, negative gains, availability, and unsupported inputs.
