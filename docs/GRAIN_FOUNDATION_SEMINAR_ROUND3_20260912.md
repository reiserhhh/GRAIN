## Codex's proposed final corrections

1. GLS is one restricted executable special case, not the replacement of all
   GRAIN. Nonlinear features, paths and interactions need not be banned forever
   or hidden in an arbitrary bias constant. Keep a small declared estimator
   family, and use simulation to show when the simple case needs extension.
2. Predicted MSE ordering is not automatically an ordering of Pearson r. Choose
   a loss and estimand; derive a relation to r only under additional assumptions.
   Do NOT require reproducing the historical .0236 MCD gain through attenuation.
   Its exact protocol/features/estimand differ. Use it as motivation and an
   archival check, not tune the new theory until it retrodicts a favorite number.
3. 'Before criteria' means before VALIDATION criteria, not before any target
   anchoring. High unlabeled ICC cannot define psychological target relevance.
   Nor is a small difference from native r evidence of statistical equivalence.
4. Support/model bias cannot be a freely chosen number making any design win.
   Restrict to observed support, specify bounded sensitivity, or propose a new
   elicitation design. No newly elicited observations exist in this planning turn.
5. ECD already covers complex/interactive assessments. Do not claim it assumes
   only fixed tests, or that no earlier method has support/reference objects.
   Our contribution is a testable integration with a concrete benefit, pending
   comparison, rather than first use of general statistical principles.

## A final psychological connection, now primary-source checked

Funder (1995), On the Accuracy of Personality Judgment: A Realistic Approach,
https://rap.ucr.edu/FunderPR1995.pdf : relevance, availability, detection and
utilization distinguish properties of the target's behavior from the judge's
inference. This supports our two separate maps: cue -> human criterion, and cue
-> model judgment. Inspecting vector utilization alone only addresses one side.
Its accuracy-oriented research agenda also fits the user's discovery-first rule.

Koutsoumpis et al. (2022), Psychological Bulletin, DOI10.1037/bul0000381,
https://research.tilburguniversity.edu/en/publications/the-kernel-of-truth-in-text-based-personality-assessment-a-meta-a/
Author abstract checked, PDF inaccessible: 31 samples,85724 people; self- and
observer-reported personality show different language-cue relationships and task
moderators. Thus a scalar questionnaire correlation is not the whole cue model.

For representation choice, a standard conditional-expectation identity can also
replace reconstructing every native gradient. Let T have finite second moment,
E be the full available evidence and Phi(E) a deterministic compression. For
population optimal squared-error predictors:
R*(Phi(E))-R*(E) = E[(E[T|E]-E[T|Phi(E)])^2].
This is not a new theorem or an estimable true-personality guarantee. It states
what extra conditional contrasts/path information could contribute. Fitted-model
estimation error must be separated. Is it useful as a representation sublemma,
or does it merely add clutter? Give a direct decision.
