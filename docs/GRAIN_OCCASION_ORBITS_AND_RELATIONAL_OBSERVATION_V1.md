## The new empirical distinction

An account can be represented by action content X, event/actor relationships R,
narrative order/conditions C and concrete occasion symbols U. Keep all four:

    O_q(H) = f_q(X(H), R(H), C(H), U(H)).

For the declared C4 action only U changes. Each action on material and metadata
is exact; no equivariance of neural hidden states is assumed. Define:

    P_G f(H) = (1/4)*sum_g f(g.H)
    delta_g = f(g.H_hi)-f(g.H_lo)
    delta_bar = (1/4)*sum_g delta_g
    V_U = (1/4)*sum_g (delta_g-delta_bar)^2
    (1/4)*sum_g delta_g^2 = delta_bar^2 + V_U.

P_G is the standard finite-group projection on an orbit. Its invariance is
algebraic, not an empirical finding. The measured quantities are the amplitude
of delta_bar and the variation V_U. A nonzero mean need not be a semantic
circuit: equality/repetition patterns are also retained by this action.

In 48 distinct-event versus same-event-mention pairs, native mean-contrast RMS
is .047506 and code-sensitive SD RMS .018936. Invariant contrast energy is
86.29% of this declared orbit's total contrast energy. Thirty-two pairs keep
the same nonzero sign in all four codes. Same-event emphasis has mean-contrast
RMS .147626 and 98.05% invariant energy; other-person additions .135753 and
97.39%. These are fixed-reader observations, not personality variance shares.

## A controlled quotient still loses a relationship

Let w_m be the fixed mention weight and k_m its occasion label. For linear
probability pooling:

    S_mix(g.H) = sum_m w_m p_(t_m,g.k_m)
    P_G S_mix(H) = sum_t W_t * (1/4)*sum_k p_(t,k),
    W_t = sum_(m:t_m=t) w_m.

Each C4 orbit visits all four labels for every fixed record, so the second
identity is a finite change of summation order. It removes individual label
assignments but retains content multiplicities. Same-event repetition and
different events have the same mention-type weights W_t in this design.

The full ten-answer orbit-mean mention mixtures therefore coincide to
1.110e-16 in all48 pairs. Native orbit-mean laws differ in all48. Their scalar
paired-RMS lower bound is .023753. Any deterministic function of that specific
linear orbit-mean summary shares the obstruction, regardless of model capacity.

This is not a proof against all nonlinear summaries. Geometric pooling does
not commute with orbit averaging; its distinct/alias orbit summaries differ
(maximum coordinate difference .001555). Do not turn a small difference into
exact equality or claim that no flexible function could amplify it.
For other-person additions and order changes, both declared bases/pools still
collide, while native observations change.

## Toward a relational measurement interface

Use a mention-to-event incidence object A_(m,e), with actor-scoped event nodes,
instead of treating every mention as an independent behavioral event. Mentions
and their rhetorical emphasis remain available; they are not deleted as noise.

    n_e = sum_m A_(m,e)       # number of reports of this event
    E = qualified current claims on event/actor nodes
    M = report multiplicities and revision history
    C = sequence, other-person context, conditions and choice
    O_q = T_q(E, M, C, U).

The incidence identity and group projection are established mathematics.
The empirical contribution is a more precise observation object and a
discriminating experiment for the unknown T_q, not a new pooling theorem.
The reader may exploit token repetition, event understanding or both. HM59
will keep the action text and all occasion-token counts fixed while moving
explicit same/different-event relations between action pairs.
