# Goal-revision seminar: Round 2

Read-only; return at most 750 English words. No experiments or private data.
Read `docs/GRAIN_BREAKTHROUGH_SOURCE_DOSSIER_20260912.md`. It contains independently

Please use it to revise the TARGET, not to add a list of defensive work packages.

Your T2 conditional response operator, with T1 comparison rules and T3 reader
mechanism, is promising. But Codex challenges five assumptions from Round1:

1. Exact within-cell exchangeability plus reproducible LOW-dimensional operators
   is too narrow a gate. High-dimensional configurations and local functionals
   may be measurable. Strong interactions do not by themselves prove no common
   scale or useful comparison exists.
2. Pre-response venue is not exogenous merely because recorded first. Inferred
   topic is usable descriptively; it is not an independent intervention. Preserve
   the joint choice/response distribution and its identification conditions.
3. Calling a reference mean an invariant needs a specified transformation; the
   mean changes when the reference context distribution changes.
4. T3 is not identified by multiple readers plus one criterion without further
   measurement assumptions. Distinguish statistical lens decomposition from a
   unique recovery of psychological truth.
5. Do not say CAPS/WTT 'never supplied' an identification result unless checked.
   The new conditional operator is close to established functional/distributional
   models, and SEM/IRT already handle interactions and complex responses.

What remains genuinely new? Candidate central target:
Learn comparable psychological evidence from self-selected, non-identical
language, and derive how that evidence must change with the measurement target
and observation design. Rebuild GRAIN if necessary. It should produce an explicit
construction rule with positive capabilities, not only a calibration wrapper.

Please return:
- one proposed final scientific claim, with its closest competitor and the exact

- one decisive ideal-world experiment and one real-text construction test whose
  outcomes would actually choose the architecture;
