\[

\[

\[

It is also (D_e(z)+D_n(g_ez))-(D_n(z)+D_e(g_nz)). Reverse the square orientation

compatibility of the masked mixed field is necessary and sufficient for the two

This is an elementary finite cocycle result, not a new group-theory theorem.
