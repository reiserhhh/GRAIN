## Competing mechanisms and finite decision

Strict criterion-blind context: own versus foreign context has the SAME
log-probability gain under high and low voices. Its target contrast is zero,
even when generic predictability improves. A target-dependent contextual
link produces a nonzero contrast. The neutral voice records generic gain.
The reverse-token control has exactly the own context token bag; a difference
excludes a context-bag-only account, not all grammar/position explanations.

GRAIN's independent SOURCE fields provide a complete signed5x5 contact table.
Positive matching contacts support this declared SOURCE linkage; negative
contacts remain evidence against it. There is no accuracy threshold, best
target/prompt selection, fitted calibration, or automatic sign correction.
Nonzero target action is NOT sufficient to identify human personality.
