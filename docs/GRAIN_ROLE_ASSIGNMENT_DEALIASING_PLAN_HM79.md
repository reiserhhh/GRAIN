## Question and competing mechanisms

HM68 varies focal content x, companion content y and companion actor g.
Its ostensibly unrelated K3 notebook action always has actor h=-g.
Thus the old g edit is a compound actor exchange, not an isolated owner edit.
Its numerical intervention effects remain valid. This packet identifies
which independent assignments explain that compound effect.

1. Companion assignment contributes after holding the notebook actor fixed.
2. Notebook assignment contributes to the same content interaction.
3. Their joint assignment changes the interaction, so an evidence unit needs
   surrounding actor allocation as well as local ownership.

All outcomes are useful, including cancellation or no companion effect.
Do not rescue a desired answer by changing wording, prompts, targets or layer.
Single-actor flips change the count of A mentions. Record this explicitly;
the completed design does not magically isolate ownership from every possible
name-exposure mechanism. The old h=-g contrast keeps total actor counts fixed.

## Exact observation algebra

For each fixed context/query/stage, expand the full finite response as

    F(x,y,g,h) = sum_(a,b,c,d in {0,1}) alpha_abcd x^a y^b g^c h^d.

On the old design h=-g, the three-bit coefficients are

    beta_abc = alpha_abc0 - alpha_ab(c xor 1)1.

In particular beta_xyg = alpha_xyg - alpha_xyh. The old attribution cannot
separate these terms, regardless of its numerical precision.

Let M(g,h) be the x-by-y four-corner difference at fixed actors. Then

    M(g,h) = 4(alpha_xy + g alpha_xyg + h alpha_xyh + gh alpha_xygh)
    M(+1,h)-M(-1,h) = 8(alpha_xyg+h alpha_xygh)
    M(g,+1)-M(g,-1) = 8(alpha_xyh+g alpha_xygh)
    M(+1,-1)-M(-1,+1) = 8(alpha_xyg-alpha_xyh).

Report every coefficient, not only these highlighted terms. Score, log odds,
centered candidate logits, body endpoint and query endpoint are distinct stages.
Target-matched and other-query effects remain separate; no six pure trait axes.
Squared old effect has a signed account:

    ||beta_xyg||^2 = ||alpha_xyg||^2 + ||alpha_xyh||^2
                    - 2 <alpha_xyg,alpha_xyh>.

These are algebraic identities, not disjoint psychological-information fractions.
No deterministic material interval is a population confidence interval.
