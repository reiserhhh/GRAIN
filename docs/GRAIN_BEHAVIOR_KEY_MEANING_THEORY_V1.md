## 1. The SOURCE functional is not automatically the trait's meaning

Three objects must be distinguished:

    author-group association D_q(X)
    operational behavior-key value g_q(X)
    model-reader judgment R_q(X).

The first can be learned from a labeled author population, the second from
independently specified behavioral criteria, the third from a model's response
contract. Their names can coincide without their observation laws coinciding.
HM250's failure of a common C direction does not imply absent personality
information or bad numerical instruments. It leaves the definition/transport
of meaning unresolved. HM251 changes that definition, not the failed signs.

Independent criteria are not gold human labels either. An IPIP item key says
which stated tendencies belong to a specified inventory; projecting arbitrary
text onto its model vectors measures alignment to those behaviors under this
encoder/metric. It is not yet administration/scoring of that questionnaire.

## 2. Constructing the operational meaning

Let H_j be the query-free model vector of public first-person item j. For
criterion q with fixed high/low keys, define

    d_q = (average_(high q) H_j - average_(low q) H_j)/its Euclidean norm.

No application author, questionnaire score or reader answer is used here.
Unequal pole counts have equal total pole weight, not equal item mass. Do not
orthogonalize d_q to erase shared meanings; their full Gram matrix remains.
Emotional Stability is reverse oriented for N. Intellect/Imagination is the
official operational O anchor, not an asserted identity with all Openness
inventories. Every public ten-item key is retained without filtering.

For an actual multi-comment input X, let h_t(X) be the contextual state of
token t before any personality query. Fix reference length L_u for the full
twelve-comment author window and define

    g_q(X) = <d_q, sum_t h_t(X)/L_u>.

The denominator stays fixed across X for an author. Replacing it with varying
length/norm could itself manufacture a nonlinear comparison. Per-comment
scalar scores summed together would be annihilated by the matched marginal
design, irrespective of the quality of their psychological labels. Complete
contextual states are therefore a different observation, not a renamed
additive feature pipeline. This observation is neural; the frozen contraction
and experimental-law calculation are numerical, not raw-text LLM-free.

## 3. A finite contextual test rather than an accuracy grid

For s in {0,1}^4, X_s selects one event in each of four pairs and four common
events. It contains real complete comments, not vector interpolations. Every
event in a pair always has the same slot. Construct positive probability
arms whose signed law nu satisfies

    nu(1)=0; nu(event-at-slot)=0;
    nu(g_r)=0 for r!=C; nu(binary venue-count indicators)=0;
    maximize nu(g_C).

This does not delete context: both context choice and interaction remain in
every actual input. Matching the full **coarse binary count law** is not
matching all topics/situations or nonlinear transforms of rival scores.
Neither does mean balance prove four actual psychological traits fixed.

Any R_sep(X)=b+sum_(event,slot) beta_(event,slot)I(event,slot) has nu(R_sep)=0.
The exact input-token embedding bag is one observed member of that class and
is saved as a control. A nonzero native action cannot be explained by an
arbitrary independently added per-comment/slot score on this support; many
other joint semantic, discourse and evaluative mechanisms remain possible.

Using characters chi_A(s)=(-1)^(sum_(j in A)s_j),

    R(X_s) = sum_A c_A chi_A(s),
    nu(R) = sum_A c_A nu(chi_A).

Keep signed accounts of all orders0--4. Order0/1 cancels under marginal
balance; higher orders can have either sign. This is an exact finite response
decomposition, not evidence that a degree-k term is a degree-k human trait.

## 4. What a positive bridge would enable

After directions/laws are frozen, observe unchanged A/C native judgments and
Gamma_q=nu(R_q). Positive C mean would establish a usable operational-meaning
bridge for these inputs: an independently keyed, query-free SOURCE evidence
operation prospectively moves the matching reader. C-A separation is a
different, stronger question. Either can exist without improving prediction
accuracy, identifying a unique neural path or validating real author traits.

The positive construction ability is reusable: replace the public criterion
key, supply a qualified encoder/reference and declare the actual observation
family to build a conditional evidence-to-reader map. That is closer to the
project's general measurement-method objective than fitting an observer's
answers. How it transports across keys, natural conditions or people remains
a subsequent empirical question, not a promise built into the formula.

## 6. Input directions and measuring covectors are different types

Added during native collection, after the original design was sealed. It does
not change the registered Euclidean functional, weights or predictions.

Read [Park, Choe and Veitch2024](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.03658), sections2.2
and3, Theorems2.2/3.2/3.4 and Assumption3.3. A difference of context vectors
is an input-space vector. A linear measuring functional belongs to its dual.
An output-token contrast is not automatically the same object as a context
difference, even when their numerical arrays have the same dimension.

For a coordinate change h'=A h, a behavioral context difference transforms
as d'=A d, whereas a measuring covector transforms as w'=A^(-T)w. Therefore

    (d')^T h' = d^T A^T A h

is not generally d^T h. A specified metric, with its corresponding change
of coordinates, is required to turn a contrast direction into a measuring
covector. HM251 deliberately fixes the native Euclidean chart; its g_q is
an operational functional in that chart, not a coordinate-free semantic law.
If a bridge fails, changing numerical precision cannot repair this type issue.

Park's causal-inner-product identification additionally assumes suitable
causally separable concept families spanning the space, plus vocabulary-side
independence. We have neither established those hypotheses for continuous,
correlated personality traits nor built their causal inner product here.
Applying an arbitrary whitening/inverse or declaring five orthogonal traits
would not supply the missing assumptions. This literature connection points
to a **mechanism distinction** (input change versus measurement), not a license
to fit a metric to these twelve reader outcomes. No new metric experiment or
precision/weight search has been started by this note.
