## Fixed functional and meaning

For left/right source pair kernels A_jt, B_jt, retain the original four laws

    F_c = E_author|c E_pair[A_jt B_jt]
    J_c = E_author|c (E_pair A_jt)(E_pair B_jt)
    I_c = (E_author,pair|c A_jt)(E_author,pair|c B_jt)
    R_c = E_author|c E_pair[A_jt B_j,relink(t)].

Same priors and role marginals are retained. Normalize pi_c L_c over all32
profiles to obtain q_L(c|pair), then integrate a declared target bit b_q(c).
These are empirical kernel-law readings, not demonstrated calibrated
probabilities for humans. Same-author dependent query pairs are averaged:

    s^L_uq = (1/22) sum_v sum_c b_q(c) q_L(c|x_uv)
    d_uq = s^F_uq - s^J_uq.

This is not a product posterior treating22 overlapping pairs as independent.
For F/J/R it is equivalently a linear SOURCE-criterion functional

    s^L_uq = sum_j W^L_uj b_jq,
    d_uq = sum_j (W^F_uj-W^J_uj) b_jq,
    W^L_uj = mean_v normalized_source_author_kernel_mass^L_uvj.

All W to an author's own source bank are exactly zero by the cross-fit
contract, not a learned denoiser. The weights are independent of profile
labels; psychological criterion meaning enters through b. The score is
LLM-answer-independent mathematics; its historical upstream E5 encoder is
still neural, and its coordinate calibration is not newly cross-fitted here.

## Why full reading and relational increment can disagree

Post-outcome descriptive accounting, with no new fit:

    Cov(sF,y) = Cov(sJ,y) + Cov(d,y).

For GRAIN15 N, the complete F criterion correlation is -.062148 while d is
+.102146. Their covariances with the binary criterion are respectively
-2.244074e-4 and +7.285052e-6; the J covariance is -2.316925e-4. The score
SD ratio sd(d)/sd(J)=.019788. A small positively criterion-linked relation
can sit inside a much larger, negatively associated background reading.
This is NOT evidence for globally discarding that background, deleting
context, increasing d by fitting a weight, or optimizing norms toward zero.
It motivates distinct operational functionals rather than equating an
entire posterior-like score with all contained evidence.

These are ordinary covariance identities. The new empirical question was
whether such author-criterion association occurs on the existing natural
paired material; it was not guaranteed by a simulator definition.
