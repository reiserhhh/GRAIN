## 2. Fixed linear observation map

For the old independent1024x15 direction bank A, an E5 observation x gives
z=A^T x. The bank was learned historically for anonymous stability/response/PCA,
not optimized for the current N query. Write its reduced QR factorization
A=QR. For a fixed reading d, define

    d_parallel=QQ^T d
    d_perp=(I-QQ^T)d
    beta=R^-1 Q^T d
    d^T x=beta^T z+d_perp^T x.

Thus a SOURCE or query reader can keep the original factor coordinates while
adding an explicit target-qualified companion. This is standard linear algebra.
No component is removed; complementary means unobserved by these fixed LINEAR
coordinates, not psychologically irrelevant or undesirable.

On the unrestricted ambient space the fixed linear reading is determined by
z precisely when d_perp=0. On restricted natural support nonlinear functions
of z can sometimes determine it even with nonzero d_perp. The present experiment
does not adjudicate that, nor the sufficiency of full GRAIN tensors/path features.
No fictional vector perturbation is passed off as a naturally observed text.

The geometry depends on the inherited Euclidean E5 metric. Squared norm ratios
are NOT author-variance fractions, personality information, probabilities or
causal shares. An invertible change of the15 coordinate basis preserves Q Q^T;
changing the ambient metric would be a different registered comparison.

## 5. Signed cancellation is an account, not a selected new model

After the primary output, a read-only covariance account on the saved24 author
means gives, using empirical1/n covariance:

    Cov(theta_full,Y)=Cov(theta_parallel,Y)+Cov(theta_perp,Y).

For natural ordinal deletion the terms are6.1947015223e-5=1.9846207963e-5+
4.2100807260e-5. For whole endpoint patch they are-1.9330048882e-6=
4.8299469682e-6-6.7629518565e-6. Correlations themselves do not add because
their denominators differ. This post-outcome arithmetic was not used for
selection, fitting, or redefining the originally registered full query.

The implication is to keep a TARGET-AND-ACTION-qualified observation tuple,
not demand that every operation with the name N follow one scalar direction.
Natural deletion reencodes changed context; endpoint patch replaces a selected
internal state. More exact reproduction of one need not identify the other.
