For native labels L/R and o=0/1, orient the first-token log odds toward the
lottery option: J=(-1)^o*(l_L-l_R). Keep both order readings, their average
and their signed defect; option wording/ordering need not be a perfect gauge.

    delta_(b,c) = mean_o J(S,b,c,o) - mean_o J(T,b,c,o)
    primary = mean_author mean_c[delta_(other,c)-delta_(self,c)]
    cost_interaction = (delta_other,7-delta_other,3)
                       -(delta_self,7-delta_self,3)
