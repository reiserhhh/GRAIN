    I_lqr(x) = V_lqr(x)-B_lqr(x),
    Ibar_lr(x) = mean_q I_lqr(x),

Call I a body-access increment, not a martingale innovation, denoised trait

At fixed x and B, permutations act by the affine rebasement

    T_pi(V) = B+P_pi(V-B).

This is a conjugated finite permutation action: it composes and preserves the
norm of I, not necessarily the norm of V. The common increment is its Reynolds
projection onto the fixed subspace, implemented by the five-query mean rather
than enumerating120 permutations. This classical group construction justifies
the operation; it does not assert psychological symmetry or reader equivariance.
Equal increment norms need not imply equal diagnostic meaning. No group/math
novelty is claimed from applying this mature construction to the new interface.
