## 1. A recurrent relationship has more than one origin

Preserve author mean positions, within-panel marginal shape and cross-channel
co-location. With source-only, panel-balanced common link L0,

\[
V^g_u=E_u[gg^T]-m^g_u(m^g_u)^T,\quad
L_0=(E_sV^g_u)^{-1}E_sC_u,\quad
C_u=V^g_uL_0+R_u,\quad
R_u=\operatorname{Cov}_u(g,\psi-gL_0).
\]

The shared-link component can differ across people without different L0.
Remainder recurrence excludes this particular shared-linear explanation alone,
not every common nonlinear law, missing variable or selection mechanism.
Both components remain observations; neither is automatically removed as noise.

The reference is fit on other authors, and actual/shifted pairings have separate
L0 calibrations. Subtracting actual L0 from shifted data would inject an artificial
negative Vg L0 author signature into the null. Within-author shape is not total
source covariance: between-author means must not leak into the common slope.

Under g->gA, psi->psiB, L0 transports to inverse(A)L0 B. Source-covariance tensor
norms remain invariant. Signed energy satisfies

\[
\|\Delta C\|^2=\|\Delta(V^gL_0)\|^2+\|\Delta R\|^2
+2\langle\Delta(V^gL_0),\Delta R\rangle.
\]

These are standard linear projection and tensor identities. The cross term can
be negative; component norm ratios are not disjoint personality percentages.

## 3. Constructive counterexample: shared rule, stable remainder

New numeric worlds directly implement y=g^2 for every author, with different
author g distributions. Three panels preserve that author's distribution while
changing record order. All three source/application seeds give residual author
win fraction 1 after subtracting the source-fixed common LINEAR L0.
There is still only one nonlinear rule. Thus

    recurrent residual relationship != identified personal response function.

The linear shared world has R=0 and residual win fraction .5. Author-specific
rotations with identical marginal first/second moments yield residual 1;
independently changing rotations yield .471930/.463596/.539912. These are
noiseless identifiability examples, not realistic prediction performance.

The nonlinear counterexample does not erase author information: its author
distribution is precisely what is different. It redirects the interpretation
from an unproven personal reading rule to a distributional measurement object.

## 4. Reader law is not the author's psychological response law

In this actual packet, psi=h W_f for all authors under a fold. A shared full-
embedding observation law exists exactly by construction. The g-level remainder
therefore cannot, by itself, establish a person-specific reader mechanism.
It describes how each author's joint distribution of lexical behavior and
semantic evidence projects into the common reading system.

More generally keep the distinction

\[
\text{observation: } \psi_q=T_q(h,c,\mathcal H),\qquad
\text{author profile: } M_u(q,c)=\int T_q(h,c,\mathcal H)\,
dP_u(h,\mathcal H\mid c).
\]

Tq is the reader/measurement law. Pu represents author evidence selection,
expression and history. An author's response to a controlled situation is a
different writer-side/behavior-side object; it is not identified by a covariance
between two summaries of the same already-written comments.

Retain choice of conditions and the condition-specific profiles separately;
do not center them away. State/history can enter Tq and Pu, but technical panel
recurrence is not real-time state, emotion or causal author B_u validation.
This notation is a research specification, not an estimated universal law.
