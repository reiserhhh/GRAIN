## Decision and genuinely new ability

HM36/125 already distinguish input vectors from measuring covectors. HM160
already scores an observed conditional behavioral endpoint. HM61/74 use the
phrase measurement bridge for model-response composition, not a proxy moment
equation. HM233 calibrates source label association, not independent paired
proxy observations. None supplies the present conditional measurement bridge.

The new constructor receives observed (condition, Z-vector, W-vector, Y)
records only. Y is an independently observed operational behavior, not a
model judgment or a hidden class label. It learns a function satisfying

    K_c h_cq = m_cq,
    K_c[z,w] = P(W=w | Z=z,c), m_cq[z] = E[Y_q | Z=z,c].

It must read new author W observation laws without Z/Y, a trait query, latent
regimes, emissions or application behavior outcomes. This differs from
ordinary E[Y|W,c]: that conditional regression can change when the source
population changes even if the behavior and vector observation channels do not.

## Assumptions, not numerical rank magic

For a latent regime U, assume W independent of Z given (U,c), and the mean
of Y given (U,Z,c) equals its mean given (U,c). The Z posterior family must
separate the relevant functions of U. A bridge must exist separately; it is
not guaranteed by saying a representation is informative. The W and Y mean
channels must remain invariant in application. These assumptions are declared
in the simulator, not proven by an observed low equation residual.

U is a finite event-generating regime of the simulator, NOT a discovered
human personality class. A source/application profile is a persistent law
over these regimes and conditions, NOT an actual new participant. Conditions,
state mixture and author choice are retained rather than centered away.

## Frozen cases and competing explanations

1. Two source populations with different regime mixtures, same vector/behavior
   channels, two conditions and two behavioral targets. Exact observed census
   weights are compiled, not supplied latent likelihoods. Compare both bridges
   and ordinary conditional regressions on six new author laws (24 readings).
2. Nonunique h: four W categories, only three independent latent channels.
   Score is identified only if the new W law annihilates ker(K). Compare
   h and h+/-null directions; do not declare the minimum-norm representative
   uniquely meaningful. Return an ambiguity witness for an alien law.
3. Copied W as Z: strict event ledger rejects it. Bypassing qualification
   gives K=I and zero equation residual, yet ordinary-regression drift can
   survive. Distinct event IDs alone also do not prove proxy independence.
4. Coarsened W channel: a behavior mean outside its range has no exact bridge.
   Keep the failed equations; no clipped or regularized substitute.
5. Application W channel reversal, Y unchanged: an allowed vector law can
   give a wrong behavior reading although all source equations are satisfied.
   This records a transport limit, not a reason to tune the source scores.
