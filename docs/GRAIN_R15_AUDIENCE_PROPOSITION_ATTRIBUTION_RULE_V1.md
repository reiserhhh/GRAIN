# R15 construction rule: target proposition, attribution and audience as separate components (V1)

2026-09-26 JST. Authority: `NEXT_PACKET_16_CHANNEL_UTILIZATION_AND_NATURAL_OOM.md` Part D;
`SEMINAR_FABLE_14_DECISION.md`. Written without any new neural reading. It governs future R15 reader materials and
the use of their readings.

## The recorded failure (kept visible; not rerun)

- **The bundle.** In `F17/hedge_commitment_reader_r1`, the "disagreeing" frame read "Talking to a friend who is sure of
  the opposite, the speaker says: …". It bundles an audience manipulation with a second proposition, not-P, held by a
  named third party.
- **Results.**
  - Self utterances in that frame reach ≥ .99 on "reporting another person's view".
  - Own commitment for bare self is .969 there, against .029 in the neutral frame.
- **Reading.** For the whole premise, the reader's answer may be correct. This is a target/material scope confound. It
  is neither a clean audience effect nor proof that the reader is broken.
- **Status.** The materials and all readings stay unchanged and visible. The frame's effect remains attribution-
  disabled.

## Rules

1. **Compose a premise from separate components.** A premise is an audience component, plus an attribution
   component, plus an utterance form over P. Each component varies in its own factor, in a crossed design. No
   component may carry the content of another.
2. **The audience component is proposition-free.** It names a setting or listener role only, for example "Speaking to
   a new colleague," or "Speaking at a public meeting,". It contains no proposition, stance, belief or truth value
   about P or not-P, and introduces no second agent who holds a view.
   - Disagreement as a target is a separate listener-stance factor. It is coded explicitly as an additional
     proposition with its holder, and any query about reported views must then name that holder.
3. **The target proposition is fixed and listed.** P is instantiated identically across components. Any additional
   proposition that a component introduces is listed in the material metadata with its holder, before reading.
4. **Attribution is explicit and query-matched.**
   - The attribution component states who asserts P (self, or a named reporter) with fixed reporting verbs and
     marked quotation.
   - Ownership queries name the holder, for example "The speaker is reporting the neighbour's view that P". They do
     not say "another person's view", which any second agent in the premise can satisfy.
5. **The truth query is never stance.** "P is true" is a truth-conflation diagnostic only. It is not a commitment,
   ownership or stance channel, and not a reader target. This is unchanged from packet 15.
6. **Readings enter downstream only through a source-calibrated channel.**
   - The unit is a text with its joint query response. The channel is fitted on source materials for each declared
     knowledge state (owner and frame known or unknown).
   - Query names are not treated as semantics.
   - Report only the functionals that the channel recovers, with source-fixed tolerance sets
     (`F17/hedge_channel_utilization_r1`). For the packet-15 materials, only the merged epistemic + non-committal
     share qualifies.
7. **Declare the contract with the model alphabet before building any bridge.**
   - State the mapping from textual forms to the speaker model's utterance alphabet in advance.
   - Addressee-directed forms such as deference need their own category, or a declared merge with its information
     loss.
   - If the calibrated channel is two-pole, graded commitment levels are not claimed.
8. **No re-read is authorized by this rule.** A re-read with compositional materials would quantify effects; it would
   not change this construction decision. It needs separate local-neural authorization and a new frozen protocol.
