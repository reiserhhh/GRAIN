HM274, 2026-10-14 JST. A finite observation-regime discriminator, not an
accuracy promotion, a new mathematical theorem or a calibrated personality score.

## Mainline finding

Matching the broad evidence-sampling regime does not automatically align a
SOURCE-defined target field with a neural behavioral reading. On actual
same-window next-cached comments, GRAIN15's exchange-even N/A pair fields
recur across the two source banks, while their contacts with joint reading
departures are negative or window-dependent. This separates a usable
population geometry from the rule that interprets the evidence.

HM270-273 already distinguish author linkage, paired-source configuration,
cross-event encoding and reading allocation. HM274 adds a complete behavioral
read on actual adjacent panel evidence, rather than reusing cross-window donor
dyads. It does not rediscover ordinary covariance or neural nonadditivity.

## Operators and their interpretation

Reuse the frozen positive F/J/I/R laws from
[HM273](GRAIN_SOURCE_EVENT_COUPLING_THEORY_V1.md). For each target q,source
components are psiF-psiJ,psiJ-psiI,psiF-psiI and psiF-psiR. Both display orders
are evaluated, retaining each role's original marginals and conditions.

For a source component S and behavioral reading R:

\[
S_e=(S_{xz}+S_{zx})/2,\quad S_o=(S_{xz}-S_{zx})/2,
\]
\[
R_e=(R_{xz}+R_{zx})/2,\quad R_o=(R_{xz}-R_{zx})/2,
\]
\[
R_s=(R_x+R_z)/2,\qquad R_j=R_e-R_s.
\]

Rj is departure from an equal singleton mixture, not factorial synergy or
causal personality effect. Display reversal is not human time reversal.
Uncentered contact products retain all25 source-target/reader-target cells:

\[
C_j=E[S_eR_j^T],\quad C_o=E[S_oR_o^T],\quad C_s=E[S_eR_s^T],
\]
\[
C_{raw}=E[(S_{xz}R_{xz}^T+S_{zx}R_{zx}^T)/2]=C_j+C_o+C_s.
\]

These contacts are not Pearson correlations, probabilities, accuracy,
significance or additive psychological information shares. Finite two-element
group projections and the identity are standard mathematics.

## Mainline decision, not precision rescue

The useful distinction is SOURCE association versus conditional reading
meaning. Numerical fidelity is adequate for these signed accounts. More head,
kernel,width,precision or positive-diagonal selection cannot supply a missing
target-meaning rule. This packet preserves the positive source structure and
the disagreeing reader instead of choosing whichever looks favorable.

Before another finite experiment, consolidate the existing event-addressed
source laws and readout constructions into one explicit candidate measuring
rule. State what independently usable construction it adds,which competing
mechanisms predict differently,and which part is already established prior
art. Do not launch another decomposition merely to obtain a positive sign.
Original18/100/44 remain OPEN,goal ACTIVE,no queued/live packet process.
