## 2. Geometry, channels and boundaries are different objects

For head h, body Q/K define causal probabilities W_hij. Removing self links and
normalizing strictly earlier tokens gives T_hij. Named A/B roots and a first-token
unresolved root define boundary values B. Every H_h solves the same finite problem:

    H_h,I = T_h,II H_h,I + T_h,IB B.

This is a directed acyclic direct-sum operator. An edge has the block
diag(T_1ij,...,T_12ij). It is a transparent special case of vector-valued transport,
not a learned role-valued connection or a symmetric sheaf Laplacian. These blocks
are not unitary or generally invertible. Repeated within-layer traversal is an
observation heuristic, not the actual model's multilayer information flow.

Two compressions must be separated:

1. normalize(mean_h W_h) versus mean_h normalize(W_h).
2. harmonic(mean_h T_h) versus mean_h harmonic(T_h).

The mean RMS discrepancies here are .001838 and .016686 respectively, in different
matrix spaces. Their ratio is not a psychological information-loss percentage.

## 3. The missing term has an exact expression

Write barT=mean_h T_h, barH=mean_h H_h and H0=harmonic(barT). The boundary values
are common. The averaged unresolved source term must be included in H0; interior
row deficits happen to be identical across heads in this experiment. Define

    C = mean_h[(T_h-barT)(H_h-barH)].

Averaging each head's harmonic equation gives

    (I-barT_II)(barH_I-H0_I) = C_I.

Thus the difference is propagated channel covariance, not simply numerical
imprecision. The inverse exists because barT_II is strictly lower triangular;
its Neumann expansion is finite. More generally, differing source terms add
mean(b_h)-b0 to the right-hand side; a matched averaged deficit convention makes
that term zero. No continuum limit, manifold assumption or
PDE uniqueness is needed for this finite algebra.

## 4. An evidence unit includes paired content

For fixed source target t, let p_thi be the numerical source-query kernel on the
new body, c_thi=<d_th,V_hi> its source-calibrated value, and H_hiA the person gate.

    m_th = sum_i p_thi H_hiA
    n_th = sum_i p_thi H_hiA c_thi
    phi_t(e,A) = sum_h n_th/m_th
    Theta_t(P,A) = integral phi_t(e,A) dP(e).

P is the author's evidence-composition law, not a personality truth inferred by
this formula. Current Pplus/minus are controlled synthetic laws, not real humans.
Same-marginal P can give different Theta through behavior/subject relations.
Empty gate mass is absent observation, not a zero trait. Unknown probability
is retained; normalization does not increase empirical coverage.

The paired numerator compared to the mean-law numerator contains

    n_pair,t - n_mean,t
      = sum_hi p_thi (H_hiA-barH_iA) c_thi.

This is the concrete connection/content term. A common gate removes it even when
both marginal channels remain. Its sign is source-reader and text dependent;
retaining it is not a guarantee of prediction improvement.

## 5. Conditioning must not be mistaken for pairing

With paired mass/numerator mp,np and mean-law mass/numerator mm,nm:

    phi_pair,h-phi_mean,h
      = (np-nm)/mp - (mp-mm)*(nm/mm)/mp.

The first term and normalization term are separately observed. They can strongly
cancel, reverse or amplify one another. Integrating this identity over the author
law preserves the exact accounting, checked to1.735e-16.
