## Finding and construction ability

On the SAME source-selected real S/T evidence, the anonymous `Scale K`
cooperation-minus-exploration action is **+1.366014 nats**, 95% whole-author
bootstrap interval **[0.897311,1.892058]**, positive for **23/24 authors**.
Thus these evidence blocks distinguish the two specified behavioral criteria
without a familiar Big5 name. The criterion descriptions are subcriteria,
not operational definitions of the entire Big5 traits.

Familiar names change this discrimination. The definition contrast is
+0.046821 under Agreeableness and +0.398500 under Openness. The named
interaction corner is -0.351680, interval [-0.595608,-0.109529]. A universal
criterion-only reader is not an adequate description of the observed field.
The large positive action common to all six cells is also retained.

The usable increment is a versioned contract renderer taking addressed
evidence, a display name, a criterion and a response alphabet. It exports
their full response field instead of silently equating a personality word
with a behavioral definition. It does not replace the neural encoder.

## 2. Complete accounts

For final normalized state h and ten digit logits z, define

\[
Y=\log\sum_{j=5}^{9}e^{z_j}-\log\sum_{j=0}^{4}e^{z_j},\qquad
\delta_{u,n,d}=Y(S_u,n,d)-Y(T_u,n,d).
\]

The registered primary is the equally author-weighted mean of
`delta_K,cooperation-delta_K,exploration`. On the familiar-name2x2 design,

\[
\delta_{u,n,d}=g_u+s_n l_u+s_d v_u+s_n s_d i_u,
\qquad s=(+1,-1).
\]

This is an exact standard factorial representation, not three discovered
psychological mechanisms. The contrasts are `2v` for named-averaged definition,
`2l` for definition-averaged name and `4i` for the interaction corner.
Finite differences commute; this experiment does not establish noncommutativity.

## 3. Same-vector response account

Apply the identical S/T and name/definition contrasts to stored h. Let
`w=mean(W5..9)-mean(W0..4)`. Then exactly

\[
Y=w^\top h+
\log\operatorname{mean}\exp(z_H-\bar z_H)
-\log\operatorname{mean}\exp(z_L-\bar z_L).
\]

Both linear and curvature accounts remain. The primary mean has linear
part1.136640 and curvature part0.229374; neither is removed or interpreted
as a psychological-information fraction. A shared linear head does not
make the preceding network computation linear.

Post-outcome Euclidean description of the definition-action vectors:

These are final response-state displacements, not localized causal paths,

Positive geometric alignment does not identify one invariant semantic axis;
the output action is demonstrably contract-dependent.

## 4. Mainline implication and stopping rule

HM218/219 provide prospective source-evidence selection and a direct A
judgment effect. HM220 did not detect beneficiary-selective use in its
point menu. HM221 now supplies positive operational-criterion discrimination,
while showing that naming changes that discrimination. These are different
observation destinations, not increasing accuracy on one prediction task.

An evidence-independent contract offset cancels in S/T. Numerical head
error3.434168e-6 is far smaller than the observed effects. No definition,
name, prompt, head, scale or accuracy rescue is justified. The next question
is how a specified criterion qualifies the use of the SAME evidence carrier,
not whether another wording produces a larger scalar. Inventory existing
query/reader/carrier operations before any new finite design.

Keep the neural vector observation distinct from the desired source-only
mathematical observation law. This packet does not construct a raw-text
LLM-free personality scorer. Human meaning, a natural observation law and
the original18/100/44 scope are not supplied by this positive model response.
