# Qualified evidence carriers and two-stage composition

2026-10-11. HM156 explanatory theory; prospective selection rules are in the
separately sealed HM156 plan. This document may receive outcome interpretation.

## 1. A different observation unit

A lexical slice identifies where an event is described, but not necessarily
where its influence on a target judgment is carried. Autoregressive encoding
can carry an actor-event relation into later words whose literal content does
not change. Distinguish three objects:

- The original event span in the text.
- Its context-dependent representation in the encoder.
- Its effect on a role- and target-qualified reader.

An encoder change without a reader effect is not sufficient for target
measurement. A reader effect without the intended actor qualification may be
generic coherence, name preference, or a different inference. This is why HM156
keeps all actor/query interactions rather than maximizing patch fidelity.

An operational observation unit is provisionally a tuple

    (event, actor, condition, history, carrier intervention, target reader).

It is not an additional psychological factor by naming convention. It supplies
an interface for GRAIN's author choice, conditional response, reporting and
state channels. An author can report another actor; those identities remain
distinct. The source reference population is not replaced by the reader model.

## 2. Why independent input choices do not imply independent factors

At fixed wording/opportunity/order, natural ending memory has four components:

    M(e,n) = M0 + e ME + n MN + en MEN,  e,n in {-1,+1}.

All four are uniquely defined by the complete finite support. No low-rank
factorization, orthogonality or psychological independence follows. For a
recipient (e,n), let Me=M(-e,n), Mn=M(e,-n), Mb=M(-e,-n). Then

    Madd = Me+Mn-M(e,n)
    Mb-Madd = 4en MEN.

Even with MEN=0, a nonlinear reader phi may combine the two carrier increments
nonadditively. Conversely, MEN may be nonzero but invisible to a particular
reader. The representation and the observation law must both be examined.

For the same reader and identical recipient memory elsewhere, define

    F0=phi(Mbase), FE=phi(Me), FN=phi(Mn), FB=phi(Mb), FC=phi(Madd).
    I_total = FB-FE-FN+F0
    I_encoding = FB-FC
    I_reader = FC-FE-FN+F0
    I_total = I_encoding+I_reader.

This is an exact finite account, not a newly proved theorem. Encoding and reader
terms can cancel. Their norms are not percentages of psychological information.
Madd may lie outside natural model states; it is an explicit computational
intervention, not a natural behavioral observation. The split depends on its
reference and the chosen carrier interface. Keep K and V units separate.

## 3. Mechanism decisions, not a prediction contest

HM156 intervenes separately on unchanged other behavior clauses and the constant
record ending. Each single-flip donor is naturally encoded. The recipient's
actual text, suffix questions and body states remain unchanged. Effects on
betaEP/betaNP under complete e/n/person contrasts identify whether the operation
changes actor-qualified target reading. Matching and cross-target effects,
wording, opportunity, order, negative and null results are retained.

Full descendant closure is a positive architectural control: naturally changed
history begins no earlier than the first edited actor token. Upstream other
clauses are null controls. Making the positive control exact is not scientific
success by itself. The scientific question concerns the unchanged-text carrier
effects and the two sources of nonadditivity.

If only downstream other-clause carriers act, evidence is not represented solely
at its own named span. If the constant ending acts as well, an uninformative
literal token can become a carrier of qualified context. Neither observation
means the carrier is a pure personality vector. If joint effects are mostly
reader-mediated, an additive evidence bank still needs a conditional nonlinear
observation law. If encoder interaction is substantial, independent event
summaries lose relevant joint structure before the reader is applied. Both can
occur, with opposition. No outcome here warrants universal extrapolation.

## 5. Carrier multiplicity is not evidence multiplicity

One event can affect its own span, later event spans and the record ending.
If all those carriers are scored as independent observations of the author,
the same event may be counted several times. Conversely, one ending may encode
several events jointly. The mapping between event identity and carrier identity
is therefore not necessarily one-to-one in either direction.

GRAIN's evidence-history ledger must track event/occasion identities separately
from carrier addresses. Repeated views of one event should retain a joint
observation/error law, not be treated as new independent behavioral samples.
Different events can also be dependent; an event-level product likelihood is
not automatically justified. This joins the older duplicate/correction ledger
to the vector mechanism without erasing either author selection or context.

For an abstract finite set of eligible events S, a prospective observation law
may need both singleton and interaction contributions, rather than only
sum_{e in S} ell(e). HM156's actor-swap contrasts are not event-presence/absence
experiments, so they do not estimate such an event-set expansion. They can
identify a reason to test it. The next construction must preserve this typed
distinction instead of replacing it with more accurate but unqualified scores.
