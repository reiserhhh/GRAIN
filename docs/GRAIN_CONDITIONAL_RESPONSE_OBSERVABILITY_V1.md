## Defined objects

Let j=(behavior family, condition) index k=12 addressed cells, b in {-1,+1}
their polarity, and F(j,b) the registered own-person response vector. Actor
eligibility is fixed by the public material, not inferred from a high score.

    R_j = [F(j,+1)-F(j,-1)]/2
    mu = (1/k) sum_j [F(j,+1)+F(j,-1)]/2
    p_theta(j,b) = (1+b theta_j)/(2k)
    E_theta[F] = mu + R theta/k

Here |theta_j|<=.4 and sum(theta)=0. Opposite laws preserve family, condition
and global-polarity margins; they differ in their joint composition. They are
constructed evidence-frequency laws, not new participants or psychological
truth. Mu is retained; equal contrasts do not imply zero absolute scores.

For observation L, define author-law equivalence by

    theta ~_L theta' iff L(theta-theta') = 0
    d_L(theta,theta')^2 = (theta-theta')^T L^T L (theta-theta')

This is a pseudometric when L has a kernel. An individual difference can be
real in the evidence law but absent from the chosen observation. Neither
nonlinear decoding nor higher numerical precision can invert an exact collision.
This is established nullspace/identifiability mathematics, not our theorem.

The required observation package is

    L_weighted = R
    L_joint = [U;R]
    L_indexed[:,j] = e_condition(j) tensor R_j
    Phi_u(c,nu,q) = E_{P_u}[r_q(h;nu) | qualified actor=u, condition=c]

U uses uniform token weights; R uses the fixed query-weighted readout. The
indexed column map keeps condition contributions distinct. Known condition
metadata is extra information; it is not autonomously recovered from unknown
natural text in this experiment. The instrument policy nu and author evidence
law P_u remain distinct. Other-actor/focus sectors are retained as diagnostics.

## Actual distinction and its limits

Source R has rank6. Appending the equal-global-polarity constraint gives rank7
and a five-dimensional kernel in twelve cells. A deterministic source-only
theta is selected from that kernel, before application analysis. Its weighted
contrast is 1.20e-17, as required by construction, not an empirical surprise.
The paired evidence laws have L1 difference .187501.

The empirical complementary observations of the same witness are nonzero:
uniform .0080563, condition-indexed weighted .0731663, value .1798723. These
norms have different dimensions/units; their magnitudes are not comparative
accuracy. Joint and condition-indexed maps have machine rank12 on all six old
variant/wording banks. The source condition-indexed condition number is97.80;
joint is559.94. Rank is algebraic, not noise-resistant psychometric reliability.

The two source condition contributions cancel exactly when pooled. They are
not discarded, centered away or called noise. In the other five old banks,
the SAME source-fixed witness becomes visible even in pooled weighted outputs
(norm .014548--.068550). Thus observation equivalence depends on the expression
and reader; a source blind direction is not a universal invisible human trait.
Fresh materials cover only condition-minus. Their unobserved condition-plus
is not imputed, so the twelve-cell witness is not applied there.

## Geometry does not replace meaning

All six families, two wordings, three channels and eight signed actor/focus
sectors are kept. Fourteen support cases yield336 comparisons. Fresh own-person
uniform family ranks are3/720 and14/720; weighted19/720 and2/720; value43/720
and27/720. In different older instances, value matching is better instead.
Cosines, noncentered Gram alignment, permutation ranks and algebraic spectra
are finite design diagnostics, not psychological r, AUC or inferential p.

## Relation to published mathematics

[Park, Choe and Veitch2024](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/park24c.html)
separate measurement and intervention representation under causal assumptions.
This construction does not assume that a response direction is a control axis.
[Arvanitidis et al2022](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v151/arvanitidis22b.html)
pull distribution geometry back into a latent space; [FishBack2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.17231v1)
applies output Fisher pullbacks to transformer steering. Our deterministic
L^T L has no output likelihood/noise model and is NOT Fisher information.
The [OpenAI fixed matrix-jets manuscript](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/Determination-of-a-metric-and-a-unitary-connection-from-one-boundary-patch-October-5-2026/build/sections/06-matrix.tex)
motivates checking observation support before assuming a right inverse. Its
harmonic/PDE/compactness hypotheses are not transferred to text or psychology.
