and two observer focuses as a 2x2 table C. Its row margins are n, column margins
are m and total is N. Let I = n m^T/N, with I=0 for an empty group. Then

    C = I + lambda [[1,-1],[-1,1]], lambda = C[low,B] - I[low,B].

    max(0,n_low+m_B-N)-I[low,B] <= lambda

    Gamma = K[low,B]-K[low,A]-K[high,B]+K[high,A].
