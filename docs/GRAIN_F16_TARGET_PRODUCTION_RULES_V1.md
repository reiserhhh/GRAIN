# Target-specific construction rules from F16

This supplements the frozen independent handbook without changing its hash or reconstructing preregistration after the fact. Evidence: `reports/grain_measurement_foundation/GPTPRO_V3_F16_T1_SOURCE_T3_APPLICATION_20260923.md` and the preceding T2 report. Scope is mathematical production on declared synthetic worlds, not a completed psychological instrument.

## Keep three objects separate

| Object | Estimand | Relevant error |
|---|---|---|
| T1 conditional contrast | m(+1)-m(-1) | Conditional personal-response and source-estimation uncertainty |
| T2 current state | E[s_T given prefix] | State/level separation, personal dynamics and source uncertainty |
| T3 planned future average | E[mean(y_T+1:T+4) given prefix and condition plan] | Terminal-state uncertainty, shared future innovations, observation noise, personal/source uncertainty |

T3's predictive interval includes future realization noise. That interval cannot be labeled a confidence interval for the conditional mean alone. T2's inferred state is not a directly observed emotion. T1's four-cell uncertainty cannot inherit a two-coefficient model's calibration.

## Fixed production sequence

1. Declare the object, condition plan, unit, input role and observable support before scoring application data.
2. Fit source models and their uncertainty on source authors only. Preserve floor/boundary/failure metadata; a bootstrap is not a cure for model misspecification.
3. Score the source reference using the exact same target function that application inputs will receive; freeze its mean and SD. These are a fixed empirical reference, not universal population constants.
4. Serialize model ensemble, reader/world identity, code hash, integration rule and failure behavior. Empty personal inputs are prior-borrowed, not measured zeros.
5. Update using the evaluated author's legitimate observed prefix only. Neither identity nor current cohort determines the numerical integration seed or reference.
6. Cold-score in a separate process from the frozen bundle and observed inputs. Check full objects, not just the headline score.
7. Evaluate on a matching outcome and condition plan. The F05 counterfactual remapping is valid only under its explicit additive transition-independent simulator; it is not a recipe for correcting unknown human counterfactuals.
8. Attach target/seed/design-specific calibration results outside immutable original scores. Retain failures and distinguish numerical identities, finite calibration and psychological meaning.

## Decisions supported by the new evidence

- Independent-agent implementation from a detailed mathematical handbook can reproduce the required numerical mechanism on new application authors. A second agent is not independent-human reconstruction.
- The condition plan is part of the score's identity. Comparing a planned (+,-,+,-) future against an archived (-,-,-,-) future can create a large apparent failure without changing the predictor.
- Shared future innovations must be accumulated before squaring their contribution. Marginal future variances alone do not give the average's variance.
- T1 source uncertainty was computed rather than assumed absent; in this particular panel it contributes about0.208% of total working variance and does not resolve one seed's undercoverage.
- Calibration remains object-specific: finite T3 checks passed while T1/T2 each retain a failed cell. No uniform reliable-instrument stamp follows.

## Public calibrated bundle addendum: 2026-09-26

Evidence: `reports/grain_measurement_foundation/GPTPRO_V3_F16_SERIALIZE_20260926.md`;
handbook `docs/GRAIN_F16_PUBLIC_CALIBRATED_BUNDLE_HANDBOOK_V1.md`.

- Publish the calibration mixture, not a corrected point input: numeric bundles
  carry pilot fit pairs + bootstrap indices (calibrators refit at load); text
  bundles carry pilot counts + generator seed (draws regenerated at load).
- Keep estimator identity explicit: Cartesian source x pilot (numeric) versus
  paired source/channel Monte Carlo (text) are different integration rules.
- A reader change leaves the T1/T2/T3 estimands unchanged but changes the
  scoring function (the observation law used to update the posterior) and hence
  the source score-reference. Freshly score the source reference under the new
  reader, material bank and estimator; inherit a reference only when
  observations are mapped into the identical unit and scored by the identical
  function (numeric T1). Retained decomposition (`F16/serialize_reference_shift_r1`):
  inheriting the historical text reference moves application positions by mean
  .095/.245/.167 (difference of z-scores, T1/T2/T3); the reader-attributable
  comparison (same bank, rendering and estimator family) is .050/.065/.122 and
  the bank/rendering comparison (C vs H) is .124/.307/.106; these are separate
  comparisons and do not add. Report raw-unit scores separately from positions.
- Never let an inherited inner identity field authenticate a changed reader;
  reconcile it explicitly and refuse bundles that still carry it.
- The cold scorer must be demonstrably unable to read undeclared files
  (audit hook), and must reject wrong reader/target/version/clock, truth fields,
  unsupported conditions/steps and unregistered text per request.

## Natural-text reader insert and input-identity addendum: 2026-09-26 (seminar-07)

Evidence: `R14/counterfactual_reader_r1` (40 natural units, Qwen E reader plus NLI, 680 calls, controls exactly 0) and
`P05/c2_dedup_sensitivity_r1` (inventory only). These rules change construction decisions. They do not claim
psychological validity.

1. **Attribution is a measured, largely unqualified channel for these readers.** An inserted sentence attributing
   sociable behaviour to another person still raises the writer's Qwen E score by about .285 over a neutral insert;
   own-attribution raises it by .331. NLI discounts more but still credits most of it.
   - A target that uses these readers on natural text must declare how attribution is handled: either an explicit
     ownership/attribution annotation step before reading, or an "ownership unqualified" line on the reader card.
   - Neither is automatic. The choice is recorded in the target contract.
2. **Per-sentence influence is reported, not silently capped.** One explicit self-statement moves the Qwen E readout
   2.2–3.7 panel SDs (E+) and 0.8–1.3 SDs (E−); NLI moves 1.4–2.1 SDs.
   - Reader cards and passports must report measured single-sentence influence per corpus.
   - Capping or pre-aggregation is a per-target design choice justified by the target contract. It is not triggered
     by an SD threshold.
3. **Site claims stay scoped.** The L12.H0 question-row output carries −.045 of the E− insert effect but only +.005
   of the +.331 E+ effect (control head ≈ −.001).
   - No construction may treat L12.H0 as the carrier of the E readout.
   - Vector-instrument audits must test both evidence directions and report absolute transfers, not shares.
4. **Input identity precedes features.** Comment-level inputs must pass the F00 record contract before any
   choice/MCD-type feature is built.
   - Keys with conflicting versions must be resolved by a declared version rule. In PANDORA, 1,795 duplicate-key rows
     differ on consumed fields, 56.6% of the body conflicts in trait-named venues.
   - Eligibility is reported per condition, with exclusions stated, not given dummy roles.

## Same-object reader and event-identity addendum: 2026-09-26 (seminar-08)

Evidence: `R14/same_object_channels_r1` (0 neural calls; the exact F08 replay passes) and
`P05/c2_event_version_sensitivity_r1` (300 CPU cells).

1. **The historical GRAIN comment view carries no pole information on the controlled bank.** It has 150 feature
   classes and every one occurs with both poles, so the Bayes-optimal pole accuracy is .5.
   - It must not serve as a pole/stance channel.
   - F16 slots aliased to NLI stay explicitly labelled.
   - A constant pole readout is a failed channel and is never thresholded into a reading. With ≥ .5 it becomes
     "always pole 1".
2. **Qualification is chosen by measured downstream cost in the target world.** Historical GRAIN q vs NLI q is
   world-specific: xor B1 +.0098, iid B2 −.0097. GRAIN q also qualifies quoted third-party statements, which goes on
   the reader card.
3. **Standard references are declared by form.** An in-bank refit lexical reader reproduced the oracle exactly (the
   bank is lexically solvable). The transported 96-bank reader lost pole (.75). Comparator claims must name the form.
   No harder wording is manufactured to defeat the baseline.
4. **Measured-emission-law EM source fitting (pooled E-step) is not a default improvement** for these readers. The
   oracle-observation source fit stays canonical.
5. **Event identity comes before ablation.** Controls matched "per author" must be matched in distinct events after
   event-identity resolution, not in rows.
   - Repeated versions of one comment key must be resolved, or declared, before any choice feature.
   - Under row matching, r1's C2 control retained 481 more distinct events than the targeted arm, which inflated the
     targeted contrast.

## Transport and uninformative-observation addendum: 2026-09-26 (seminar-09)

Evidence: `F12/intervention_transport_r1` and `R14/soft_channel_trace_r1`, both with 0 neural calls.

1. **Reader substitution by level calibration is not valid for intervention responses.** A map calibrated on
   ordinary readings (NLI → Qwen, b = .02–.09) preserved response direction but reproduced only 7–47% of the response
   magnitude, and none of the ownership discount. Passports and reader cards must carry each reader's own
   intervention signature. A substituted reader needs its own response calibration.
2. **Never threshold an uninformative channel.** Absent or unmeasured pole information is represented by a declared
   no-information update (prior propagation only). Thresholding the constant .5 into a hard 1 accounted for B2
   +.22–.25 of the archived GRAIN-pole cost.
3. **Keep qualified-read mass and pole-informative evidence mass separate.** The archived estimator reported about 32
   units of "evidence mass" for reads with a flat pole likelihood. That is model recycling, not person evidence.
4. **Soft vs hard use of a reader is declared per reader and world.** Degraded or transported readers default to soft,
   and a hard form must carry its measured cost.

### Seminar-10 scope corrections to the seminar-09 addendum (appended)

- **Rule 1** is scoped to the tested NLI→Qwen pair and a level-only affine map. The ordinary-level map was already
  inadequate. The rule is: level-only substitution of this kind must not be used for intervention responses. It is
  not a theorem that no calibration map transports responses.
- **Rule 2's no-information update** is licensed only by an exact information certificate: here, balanced feature
  collisions plus a flat likelihood. It is not licensed by an output value of .5.
- **Rule 4.** The soft/hard choice is measured per reader, world and estimator. There is no universal soft default.
- **Monte Carlo significance** is reported as (count + 1)/(B + 1); a zero count is never p = 0.

## Measurement-error identification and fixed-core wording addendum: 2026-09-26 (seminar-10)

Evidence: `F13/covariance_identification_r1` (declared simulation plus exact equations) and `F12/fixed_core_dif_r1`
(cached readings). Both used 0 neural calls.

1. **Without an anchor, report identified mixtures only.** Repeated occasions with two readers identify only
   Var(a + b_m), the cross-reader covariance, reader contrasts and occasion error; a′ = a + c, b′ = b − c leaves every
   observation unchanged.
   - "Author variance" without an independent anchor channel, or another declared identifying design, is labelled
     assumption-dependent. It is biased by +.67 under halo and +.30 under a shared method component.
   - Reader error is never subtracted mechanically; the oracle version is off by 2Cov(a, b).
2. **With an independent repeated anchor (a declared assumption and cost),** Var(a), Cov(a, b_m), Var(b_m) and
   Cov(b_1, b_2) are identified and reported together with the covariance term.
3. **Duplicate event rows are collapsed by event ID** before variance, reliability or ICC. Copies inflate stable
   variance (+4–5%) and reliability, and deflate occasion error.
4. **Constant condition offsets are fixed effects,** never personal responses. β is separable from reader bias only
   with an anchor.
5. **Reader cards report fixed-core wording variation per role** (frame, topic and day intercepts and loadings, with
   CIs).
   - Qualification laws whose contrast varies with the wrapper (NLI loading .33; historical GRAIN q frame range .35)
     are not transported across wrappers without per-wrapper calibration.
   - Unseen frames are unsupported.
   - Fixed-core variants are not paraphrases.

### Packet-11 corrections to the seminar-10 addendum (appended)

- **Item 3 is restricted to exact copies of the same observation.** Legitimate independent readers or reports, and
  conflicting event versions, are not collapsed merely because they share an event ID (see the event and version
  rules in F13, F14-03 and `P05/c2_event_version_sensitivity_r1`).
- **The "+4–5%" wording is replaced** by the measured paired deltas at copy rate .057:
  - V +.034 to +.051;
  - σe² about −.019;
  - reliability +.007 to +.015;
  - cross-reader S12 about 0.
- **The R29 bootstrap intervals** are a working person-resampling approximation, not guaranteed 95% calibration.
- **The fixed-core replay** uses the first supported alternative with a different reading, which may change several
  facets at once. Within-role rank does not identify unseen-frame transport.

### Packet-12 uncertainty-target and sampling rules (appended 2026-09-26)

Evidence: `F13/personal_law_r2`, and the `F05/common_shock_episodes_r1` addendum.
1. **Every published interval names its target, conditioning and approximation.**
   - Latent-state probability p_u(s_t): the working latent-mixture interval, variance state + Σ w_s C_ss + source.
   - Integrated next-outcome probability given the observed data: variance wᵀCw + source, using the candidate's full
     working covariance C. B2 has C = F Σ Fᵀ and M has C = Pᵀ diag(rv) P, both non-diagonal. EB is diagonal only
     under its declared independent-Beta assumption.
   - Never substitute diagonal-only variance. Never score one target's interval against another as "calibration";
     that is a cross-target diagnostic.
2. **Conditioning on working weights.** A companion conditional on shared-law working weights is not calibrated
   for the true integrated target. Under response-dependent missingness, integrated-target coverage requires a
   selection model for the weights.
3. **Evaluation targets condition on missingness indicators under the declared mechanism.** Selection-blind targets
   are named comparators.
4. **Replicate episodes to estimate common-shock handling.**
   - Report n_eff = n/[1 + (n − 1)ρ], with asymptote 1/ρ, and the episode-cluster SE.
   - Choose the mix of authors and episodes by cost and by the variance component of interest. There is no
     universal author cutoff.
   - Episode variation includes source-law refit variation.
5. **F13 interval contract pointer.** The current target, conditioning and approximation definitions are the
   contract table in `docs/GRAIN_R24_TARGET_REPAIR_PROTOCOL_V2.md`, registered as `interval_contract` for
   `F13/personal_law_r2` in `configs/grain_v3_revision_registry.json`. Earlier F13 interval descriptions that score
   a latent-mixture interval against an integrated target are cross-target diagnostics.

### Packet-14 equivalence wording (appended 2026-09-26)

- **Merging surface forms.** Merge them when the merged states are predictively equivalent over the declared probe
  set for the construct view. Controlled lumpability additionally gives a Markov quotient. A group symmetry is one
  sufficient certificate, not a requirement. Evidence: `F15/equivalence_hierarchy_r1`.
- **Error from one observation.** A distinction between laws is resolved from aggregated observations. One
  observation errs with probability (1 − TV)/2 under equal priors.
