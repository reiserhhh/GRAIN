# GRAIN broad literature seminar: independent round

## Research brief

Work read-only. The researcher wants a foundational methodology for constructing
interpretable, context-specific measures of personality, behavioral tendencies,
and states from language, not a finished scale and not merely a text classifier.
GRAIN and vector-path prototypes are revisable. Do not optimize the latest score
or propose another indefinitely bounded experiment queue.

The discovery of MEM and BLT after months of development exposes insufficient
literature coverage. Independently search the entire system using established
terms, not our terminology. Find predecessors that already perform our proposed
next step. Search primary papers and author repositories; follow useful cited
references. Distinguish actual theorem errors from unmet assumptions and limits.

Read these public-facing local documents only:
- docs/GRAIN_SYSTEM_RETROSPECTIVE_AND_ARCHITECTURE_20260911.md
- docs/GRAIN_SCIENTIFIC_TARGET_AND_RESEARCH_PROGRAM_V5.md
- docs/GRAIN_BIDIRECTIONAL_THEORY_AUDIT_20260912.md
- docs/GRAIN_LITERATURE_REGISTER.md
- docs/SUICA_UNIFIED_THEORY_SYSTEM_V8.md (targeted sections)
- docs/SUICA_4W_PERSON_INTERPRETER_THEORY.md (targeted sections)

Do not read raw participant records, labels, secrets, environment files, private
conversation logs, lockboxes or unrelated projects. Do not edit, run code, train,
install, create agents or execute repository tools. You may use Read/Glob/Grep
for these documents and WebSearch/WebFetch for public original research.

Independently cover all mechanisms, then emphasize psychological measurement
construction, semantic/open-ended assessment, item/person/context interaction,
selection, state/trait dynamics, evidence-centered/adaptive design and validity.
Codex independently covers the whole map, emphasizing mathematical and CS
mechanisms. Neither review should merely agree with the other.

Deliver in English, about 2,000-3,000 words:
1. An ordered mechanism-to-predecessor matrix, including what is already solved
   and what is conditional/unresolved. Mark reading depth for every source.
2. At least 12 strong primary sources beyond the existing register where useful;
   do not pad the count. Title, authors, year, DOI/URL, exact relevant section or
   abstract-only status. Identify the closest 4-6 sources for detailed comparison.
3. Candidate GRAIN contributions: known component / new local empirical result /
   potentially useful combination / genuinely open mechanism. Do not certify
   priority merely because a search found no match.
4. Three strongest revisions to our next-step plan because prior work already
   provides the method, and three decisive research questions that remain.
5. What would count as a field-level theoretical contribution beyond a toolchain,
   without demanding universal accuracy or claiming guaranteed publication.
6. Disagreements with the current V5 and questions for the Codex seminar.

Report access restrictions and failed search tools explicitly. Do not substitute
remembered citations for verified papers without labeling them unverified.
