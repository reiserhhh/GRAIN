## Contrasting mechanisms and outputs

A correctly marginal-based diagnostic operation cannot distinguish the laws.
A joint diagnostic operation can. Native scores are not assumed to be calibrated
posteriors. A four-factor character expansion retains all16 terms: the expected
diagnostic term is b1*b2*b3*c. Reference main effects, lower interactions and
effects on B/unchanged action facts are retained rather than tuned away.
Nonzero interaction alone does not prove Bayesian reasoning: lookup, general
numerical inference and nonspecific reference contamination remain alternatives.
