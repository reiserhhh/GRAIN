# P1F: fresh-material transport and frozen operator transfer

2026-09-07. Chosen after P1E; freeze before new neural scores. This is a bounded
exploratory continuation, not preregistered human-personality confirmation.

## Decision and scope

P1C located counterfactual information at early evidence and late query states.
P1D/E compressed intervention responses in a previously exposed material family.
Determine whether the transport finding and that small formula survive new
actions/settings and a rewritten question, without fitting anything new.

Four new settings x two role-swapped action assignments x two question forms x
three queries (event owner, outgoing attribution, unchanged venue) =48 prompts.
Names/order alternate across settings. Role swaps preserve the word bag and must
preserve token positions outside evidence. Record all native answer margins,
paired directions and absolute binary preference. Trait attribution is authored
behavioral contrast, not real trait ground truth. Both forms retain the original
answer conventions; reversal/negation robustness remains unresolved.

For each prompt, replace stored opposite-role states at early block04 evidence,
block16 E/Q/D/full EQD, and each of four pre-content equal-token query bins.
Q is reused as the all-four-bins effect. This is48 baseline +432 intervention +2
self/restore =482 forward calls. Hard cap650 calls/600 seconds/9GiB allocated MPS.
Complete all48 or label partial; do not stop on a favorable metric. No larger grid.

Apply P1D frozen additive bin coefficients and all three P1E E/Q/D gap readouts,
unchanged, to every new event/trait contrast. Score five masks (four single bins
and full Q), separately by query form/task/view. No held endpoint gap as input to
the endpoint-free model. Also report oracle-gap shape and zero-change baselines.
The oracle comparator distinguishes a failed transfer of shape from a failed
transfer of the gap readout. Neither model is LLM-free: both recipient/donor neural
activations and task identity are required. Do not compare these five-mask error
averages directly to the previous16-mask metric.

Report relative squared error versus donor restoration and intervention no-change,
raw margin errors, source hashes, all conditions, and null venue effects. No claim
that a neural replacement identifies a unique biological or psychological cause.

## Continuation rule

If transport repeats but the frozen formula fails, retain the location mechanism
and local formula only; do not rescue by refitting on these outcomes. Prepare a
question-aware operator hypothesis and the minimal distinguishing design. If both
transfer, prioritize richer role/evidence controls rather than bulk author scoring.
If transport fails or native ownership is weak, inspect material/readout limits.
Old author queues, Claude, training, private data and external labels remain paused.
