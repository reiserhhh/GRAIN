Let n_g be SOURCE profile counts and c_q(g) remove target bit q. Define

    pi_g = n_g/386,
    L_g(x) = mean_{i:g_i=g} k_i(x),
    Lbar_q,g(x) = mean_{i:c_q(g_i)=c_q(g)} k_i(x).

For any positive 32-profile masses m,

    O_q(m) = log(sum_{g:q(g)=1} m_g)
             - log(sum_{g:q(g)=0} m_g).

    F_q(x) = O_q(pi L),

## 5. Prior art, not mathematical priority

[ERASER (DeYoung et al., 2020)](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.408/)
already supplies a rationale-evaluation framework. Deletion and sufficiency
are not this project's invention.
[Jacovi and Goldberg (2021)](https://aclanthology.org/2021.tacl-1.18/)
distinguish model attribution from social attribution; a model's evidence
use does not identify the author's psychological cause.

[Backstrom and Bjorklund (2020)](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2020.560271/full)
show that evaluative item content affects higher-order structure and
discriminant validity in human inventories. This suggests a competing
interpretation of our cross-target pattern, not a transferred human result.
Their discussion also cautions that statistical removal can discard
personality-related variance. Our accounts are retained rather than erased.

in-Gaussian-regression-September-27-2026`, section 02-replicas, lines 1-95:
the replica information bound requires matching signal-message PAIR laws
and an independent projection variable. Equal marginals are insufficient.
Adaptive SOURCE selection here does not supply those hypotheses; the
Gaussian information bound is NOT imported. Its contribution is explicit
attention to observation laws, not certification by advanced mathematics.
