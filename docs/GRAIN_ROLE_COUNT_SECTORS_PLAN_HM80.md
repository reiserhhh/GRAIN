## Mathematical observation sectors

Use S3 acting on the designed role coordinates g,h,i, with x,y fixed.
This is a group action on input addresses, NOT assumed neural equivariance.

    P_count F = (1/6) sum_(sigma in S3) F(x,y,sigma(g,h,i))
    F_role = F - P_count F.

On binary roles, the orbits are exactly N_A=1,2,3,4. Thus P_count is the
conditional class mean and the orthogonal projection onto all functions of
x,y,N_A under the declared uniform32-cell design. It has rank16; the retained
role complement also has dimension16. Do not call the latter noise.

Full signed coefficients alpha have all32 components. The degree-two role
sector at fixed content mask has coefficients

    a = (alpha_xygh, alpha_xygi, alpha_xyhi)
    a_count = mean(a)*(1,1,1)
    a_role = a-a_count.

An arbitrary nonlinear count-only law has a_role=0; unequal pair coefficients
cannot be explained by total actor count alone on this support. Slot-specific
attention/presentation can still contribute and is retained for later work.
Report score, log odds, centered logits and both hidden endpoints separately.
No full-vector alignment, factor naming or population significance threshold.
