## Mainline decision and competing mechanisms

HM248 finds SOURCE-C evidence moving reader-A more strongly than reader-C.
HM249 excludes scalar monotone calibration as a complete interface. Neither
identifies a conditional, construct-specific evidence operation.

Construct TWO probability distributions on REAL eight-comment remainders of
the SAME author's twelve comments. Keep each comment's occurrence at every
rendered slot equal between arms, and keep all F/B/D SOURCE moments of the
FOUR non-C targets equal. Maximize the remaining SOURCE D-C contrast.
No model answer, application questionnaire, hidden reader state or new
text generation enters this construction. No negative probability, vector
interpolation or counterfactual text corner is fed to the reader.

This delivers an intervention whose expected action cannot be explained by
an arbitrary separable event-at-slot reader. It tests a source-qualified
joint evidence capability, not psychological truth of a SOURCE coordinate.

Predictions:

1. Positive C reader action: SOURCE-identified joint C evidence is usable.
2. Positive A action despite balanced SOURCE-A/F/B/D: shared reader use
   persists beyond these marginal SOURCE coordinates. This is not uniquely
   a halo effect; unmeasured cues and nonlinear criterion use remain rivals.
3. C-positive and weaker A: evidence design can differentiate the targets.
4. Zero or reverse C: retain failure; no weight, prompt, kernel, rank,
   trait, panel or tolerance search to rescue the result.

## Mathematical construction

Let a_k be the saved F/B/D five-target SOURCE accounts of remainder X_k.
Let z_k contain all96 event-at-slot indicators plus the12 non-C accounts.
Let g_k=D_C(X_k). Solve ONE finite linear program per author:

    maximize sum_k (p_plus[k]-p_minus[k]) g_k
    subject to p_plus,p_minus >=0; sum p_plus=sum p_minus=1;
               sum_k (p_plus[k]-p_minus[k]) z_k=0.

Use fixed HiGHS dual simplex and keep all primal/dual diagnostics. Numerical
centering/scaling of equality rows is algebraic preconditioning, not removal
of context/personality. No clipping, re-normalization, rank truncation or
post-outcome refit. If the support exceeds its cap, stop before observations.

For any L(X_k)=b+sum_(event,slot) c_(event,slot) I_k(event,slot), the arm
contrast is zero. This includes event-separable token counts, topic/style
scores, lengths and additive embeddings, even with arbitrary slot effects.
It does not control pair interactions, complete condition distributions or
all contextual representations. Four other SOURCE coordinates are moment
balanced, NOT declared statistically independent or fully distribution matched.

For fixed reader endpoint Y_q(X), report:

    Delta_q = sum_k (p_plus[k]-p_minus[k]) Y_q(X_k).

Y is native high/low digit log odds. Average ENDPOINTS of actual complete
inputs, not hidden states or logits followed by a nonlinear readout. This
expectation is exactly realizable by randomized presentation. Twelve authors
remain twelve authors, not hundreds of independent support documents.

Primary: mean Delta_C and all individual signs. Secondary: Delta_A,
Delta_C-Delta_A, two fixed bank means, paired author bootstrap999/seed fixed.
Compare oldC operation descriptively; a same-source-amplitude scaling is
identified only when ratio(newD_C/oldD_C) lies in[0,1], never answer-fitted.
No success threshold based on r/AUC, and no psychological-fraction claim.

## Theory/prior-art boundary

Finite convex duality and positive quadrature are standard. Bayer/Teichmann,
Corollary2 and Remark2, show finite positive support for a specified family
of integrable functions, not preservation of arbitrary unseen reader laws:
https://people.math.ethz.ch/~jteichma/tchakaloff120405.pdf

Exact moment balance is mature (Hainmueller2012 Sections1/3):
https://www.mit.edu/~jhainm/Paper/eb.pdf
We do not implement entropy balancing or inherit its observational causal
identification assumptions; here the weights DESIGN two experiments.

OpenAI/math adc7f124, three-state-channel foundations, motivates checking
what an observation compression preserves WITHIN a specified experiment:
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/The-exact-reconstruction-threshold-for-the-three-state-symmetric-channel-September-25-2026/build/sections/02-foundations.tex
No tree-reconstruction or psychological sufficiency theorem transfers here.

Geng etal2026 (https://arxiv.org/html/2609.12704v1) establishes structural
trait-impression geometry, explicitly not causal use of that geometry.
Our prospective natural-text operation asks about use. We do not reuse their
7B vectors in a1.5B space, or treat fictional-character ratings as real traits.

Novelty candidate is this qualified construction and its empirical use on
natural authored evidence, not a new LP/convex theorem or first-persona vector.
