# GRAIN VM04: Geometry, Local Sensitivity and Historical Access

Date: 2026-09-06. Status: exploratory follow-up fixed before gradient readouts.
The prior natural pilot did not establish that leading geometric directions are
stable functional directions. This package checks that gap without using
questionnaire values or opening mechanism-holdout authors.

## Objects and Budget

Retain the same six discovery authors, twelve current comments, two donor rules,
two questions, blocks10/16 and ranks2/4. Do not add authors selected by effect.
The scalar outcome is the frozen Yes-minus-No margin, not a personality score.

Capture derivatives for 24 unique own-history/task prompts at two blocks.
Freeze weights, make only one block output differentiable, offload saved
activations to CPU, and preserve the original fixed-padding numerical instrument.
Require exact original forward/state reproduction and restoration.
Limit capture to128 forwards and15minutes; limit interventions to4096 forwards
and30minutes. Save source snapshots, actual calls/tokens and partial status.

## Candidate 1: Geometric Versus Task-Sensitive Directions

For a current-token matrix H, donor difference D and target margin m:

    Q_geometry = top right singular vectors of D
    G = gradient_H m
    Q_sensitivity = top right singular vectors of G
    predicted_delta = <G, injected_H - H>_F

Only the social-initiative disposition gradient defines Q_sensitivity. Apply
that same direction to both disposition and the current-guidance collateral
question. This prevents defining each task's preferred direction and mistaking
that for selective transfer.

Compare natural projected D with both bases, rank2/4 and alpha=.25/.5/1.
At alpha1 also match sensitivity directions to geometric candidate effect norms
after BF16 casting, reporting amplification and discrete matching error.
Use independent random subspaces with matched actual norms, self/full-donor
controls and restoration. If a projection is below numerical resolution,
retain it as such rather than inventing a successful direction.

Evaluate predicted versus actual logit changes, sign agreement, absolute errors,
dose response, collateral movement, and candidate/random differences. Gradients
are selected on the current task and can trivially steer that output; even a
successful result is local controllability, not independent trait discovery.

The autograd derivative is a first-order surrogate of low-precision execution,
not the mathematical derivative of an exact rounding step function. Analysis
uses actual post-cast displacements, reports unresolved effects below .125
logit for sign tests, and floors relative-error denominators at .25 logit.
Report raw absolute errors alongside these numerical conventions.

## Candidate 2: Access Through Historical Positions

Partition the actual prompt into nonoverlapping historical evidence (including
its neutral record headers), current evidence, question and remaining tokens.
Report derivative energy fractions and per-token normalization. Fractions are
not psychological variance shares and cannot establish causal mediation.

Use bounded positive/negative gradient-direction perturbations to historical
and current regions at the two fixed blocks, with actual injected norms and
same-strength random controls. A response to historical-position perturbation
would establish a remaining computational access path at that layer, not that
all natural history effects traverse it or that history is necessary.
Do not sum separately estimated regional/layer effects into a unique explanation.

## Interpretation and Next Stage

Gradient approximation is a proposed locator, not a substitute for intervention.
It can miss nonlinear effects, as studied in
[Kramar et al., AtP*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.00745).
Path-integrated derivatives are a possible later diagnostic, not a result
already obtained here; see
[Sundararajan et al., 2017](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/sundararajan17a.html).

Aggregate repeats within target author before uncertainty calculations.
Freeze follow-up endpoints before independent authors or psychological labels.
Neither a positive nor negative result licenses changing the frozen GRAIN scorer.
Return to VM05 external links after the bounded mechanism contrast and
independent-mechanism assessment, rather than tuning prompts indefinitely.
