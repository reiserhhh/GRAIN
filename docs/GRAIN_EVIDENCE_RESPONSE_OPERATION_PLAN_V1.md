## Question and decision

Does the same source delta act as a uniform answer offset, an injected
judgement whose effect depends on evidence, or an operation that changes
evidence response? Identify the response law before fitting a more accurate
surrogate. A nonzero interaction alone cannot prove a reusable procedure.

## Equations and comparison objects

Save all ten candidate logits/probabilities, candidate mass, full vocabulary
normalizer, raw score and raw high-versus-low log-odds L. For fixed sigma=+/-1:

    A_sigma(E) = L(C(E)+sigma*d) - L(C(E))
    M_sigma = A_sigma(E1) - A_sigma(E0)
    E_baseline = L(C(E1))-L(C(E0))
    E_patched = E_baseline + M_sigma.

Report signed interactions by target, actor, class and source sign. Do not
count dependent constructed contrasts as independent participants. A direct
output-only offset L_tilde(E)=L(E)+sigma*b has M_sigma=0 exactly; b is fixed
from the source natural log-odds difference, not fitted to application.
Calculate this explicit null without neural calls. Wrong-person, random,
noop and actual donor remain distinct controls, not a composite success gate.

Bounded score changes can saturate even under a constant log-odds offset;
calculate its score consequence from the two-group masses rather than using
score interaction alone to identify the mechanism. Keep within-group digit
probabilities fixed for that null and document this convention.

Nonzero log-odds M excludes only the stated constant-output-offset null. It
can still be produced by nonlinear payload injection. Compare the complete
response signature across own/other/roster and the two behaviour families,
including counterexamples, before choosing the next discriminating question.
Do not assert program identity from one mixed difference. Finite differences
and factorial characters are standard tools, not new Lie/group theorems.
