# A complete conditional observation law for jointly qualified evidence

2026-10-10. HM106 finite construction on actual HM105 text captures. This is a
measurement *construction primitive*, not a completed personality scale, a new
Fourier theorem, or a unique account of the native LLM's personality decisions.

## 1. The observation unit is a relation, not a bag of independent features

Let s in {+1,-1} index designed content and o in {+1,-1} index its attributed
actor. The complete four-corner design has characters 1,s,o,so. At one fixed
current-token support, each frozen target q reads

    F_q(s,o;c) = sum_(token,head) p_q(s,o;c) C_q(s,o;c),
    p_q = softmax(K(s,o;c) q),   C_q = V(s,o;c) d_q.

Source registration includes scale/position in q. Context c includes the whole
encoding history, current expression, token support and reader placement. The
current token vector is not a context-free representation of that word.

Keep complete expansions p=sum_r p_r chi_r and C=sum_r C_r chi_r. Character
multiplication chi_r chi_j = chi_(r xor j) implies the exact product law

    a_(q,j;c) = sum_r <p_(q,r;c),C_(q,r xor j;c)>.

The four terms of the joint reading are

    I_q/4 = <p0,Cso> + <ps,Co> + <po,Cs> + <pso,C0>.

The first is jointly encoded value under mean weights; the middle terms create
a joint observation by multiplying individually varying fields; the last
reads joint selection against mean value. Their sum is exact, but the labels
are an account of a declared reader, not unique neural causal mediation.
Signed cancellation is preserved; norm ratios are not information shares.

**Joint output need not imply joint value coding.** With p depending only on s
and C only on o, the second term may be nonzero while Cso=0. Conversely uniform
p can read nonzero Cso. These possibilities are tested as separate null worlds.
The observed text-corner law, rather than a universal additive value model,
determines which mechanism occurs on a supplied support.

## 2. Target specificity has at least two distinct locations

Within the declared six-reader battery write p_q=pbar+ptilde_q and
C_q=Cbar+Ctilde_q, averaging normalized weights and directional values. Then

    F_q = <pbar,Cbar> + <pbar,Ctilde_q>
          + <ptilde_q,Cbar> + <ptilde_q,Ctilde_q>.

All four channels are retained along with all four role/content characters.
This is not HM88's shared *logit potential*, and mean probabilities do not
equal softmax(mean logits). Common weight with distinct d_q can still give
target-specific observations. Thus finding different target scores does not
itself establish target-specific attention routes.

Target_shared is a valid normalized reader diagnostic pbar*C_q. Uniform uses
1/T per token. Specific differences need not be positive and are not independent
probability laws. None of these edits changes the encoder or proves a native
causal path. The frozen target covectors were constructed in HM86 from source
high/low behaviour mean-value contrasts, averaged over actors; reference queries
were captured on source reference prompts. Their target names are design labels.

## 3. Author composition is a separate mathematical object

Supply a nonnegative evidence law P_u over the four corners and define

    m_(u,j;c)=sum_(s,o) P_u(s,o|c) chi_j(s,o),
    S_(u,q;c)=sum_j a_(q,j;c) m_(u,j;c).

All main effects and the joint moment contribute, and moment vectors must come
from a valid P; they are not four independent unconstrained parameters. This
does not reweight authors by the model attention partition or delete choice.
Two positive laws with the same content/owner margins can have a different
joint moment and therefore a different observation. For inherited
P+/-=(1+/-0.5so)/4, S+ - S- = a_joint = I/4.

The numerical module can score new supplied laws without LLM answer generation
once K/V and frozen source reader arrays are supplied. **It still depends on
neural text encoding and on chosen source anchors.** Complete LLM independence
or psychological validity does not follow from these equalities.

## 4. What the actual finite experiment changes

In all3banks the mean-weight/joint-value term has signed alignment about
.915--.921 with the complete joint reading; the other products remain present.
For the new-expression bank target_shared retains norm ratio .999445 and
signed alignment .999148, while uniform retains norm ratio .639026. Shared
versus native relative vector difference is .024387; a near-unit norm alone
does not imply identical observations or a psychological information share.
The coupled response-table cross-bank mean cosine is .356992(native), .365899(shared),
.276476(uniform), not a predictive accuracy comparison. Carrier differences
remain after shared-weight replacement; current token count/query position may
also differ, so do not infer that a particular sentence meaning alone causes it.

This selects the next research object: **history-conditioned value encoding and
its connection to native target decisions**, rather than a better target-weight
approximation. Inventory prior HM86/87 and reverse-trace/native boundary work
before another intervention; do not rediscover a generic linear probe.

The next bridge must distinguish source contrast-probe effects from actual
native personality/behaviour judgement effects on the same public texts. A
direction that changes our declared F_q but not the native target decision is
still a useful observation, but not the sought native inference mechanism.
Keep GRAIN's author composition and contextual structure in that comparison.
Do not tune F_q to imitate the native answer as a substitute for that test.
