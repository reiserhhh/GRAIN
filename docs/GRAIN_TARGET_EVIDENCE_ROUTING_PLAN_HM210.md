## Construction and competitors

For target q and the remaining four-bit profile c, disintegrate each law L:

```text
w_L(c|x) = P_L(c|x)
r_L(c,x) = P_L(T_q=1|c,x)
m_L(x) = sum_c w_L(c|x) r_L(c,x)
delta conditional log odds_q(c,x) = d_(1,c)(x)-d_(0,c)(x).
```

Form all four cross-readings M_ab=sum_c w_a r_b, a,b in {I,J}:

```text
conditional account = M_IJ-M_II
profile-routing account = M_JI-M_II
interaction = M_JJ-M_IJ-M_JI+M_II
sum = M_JJ-M_II = original marginal probability update.
```

Retain the same three differences after evaluating the binary log score on
each corner. They sum to the original log-score change; they are not causal
mediation or independent psychological information shares. Cross-corners are
declared diagnostic readouts, not four naturally observed populations.

Competing mechanisms: (1) within-profile relational target changes carry the
small positive update; (2) a change in other-profile weights moves an otherwise
unchanged conditional target response; (3) signed interactions/cancellation
make a one-number update inadequate. They can coexist. Purely other-target
d can move a correlated target while its conditional log-odds change is zero.
Conversely, strong conditional edges can cancel to zero marginal change.
