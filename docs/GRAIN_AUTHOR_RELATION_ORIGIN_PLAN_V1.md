## Scientific decision

HM64 found weak recurrent cross-covariance C between four frozen lexical GRAIN
coordinates g and fixed E5 query proxies y. Does that recurrence follow from
author marginal variation under one shared linear link, or survive outside that
link? A surviving remainder is NOT proof of a personal rule: a shared nonlinear
rule under different author distributions can also produce recurrent remainders.
Test that counterexample as positive mechanism discovery, not as a reason to
erase author signals or demand a perfect observer before proceeding.

## Material and decomposition

Reuse the exact HM64 numeric supports/maps: 276 authors / 9,165 comments in
213 five-query and 63 four-query cohorts. No labels, private raw texts, dates,
API, new neural calls, generation or neural training. Three technical panels are
not psychological conditions. Inherited maps/factors are not an independently

In each source-author fold, compute equally weighted within-panel matrices:

    Vg_u = E[gg^T] - mu_g mu_g^T
    Vy_u = E[yy^T] - mu_y mu_y^T
    L0 = inverse(E_source Vg_u) E_source C_u
    S_u = Vg_u L0
    R_u = C_u - S_u = Cov_u(g, y - g L0).

Source authors exclude held authors; all three panels and all source authors
have equal weight. Do not substitute total/between-author covariance for E Vg.
Reject singular source E Vg at the HM64 fixed machine tolerance, no ridge rescue.
Apply one inherited W_f to EVERY source/held author under the fold, as in HM64.
Fit exactly one shared linear reference link per fold, not a supervised human
personality predictor. Save all ten references before held comparisons.

Shift y by floor(n/2) within a panel, preserving both complete marginals.
Calibrate its OWN source link L0_shift by the same rule. Never subtract actual
L0 from shifted C: that would inject a negative Vg L0 author fingerprint into
the null. Both reference links are frozen before application.

Use HM64 source-global covariance units and tensor trace distances. New shape
blocks Vg/Vy and shared/remainder blocks have source-only centers/RMS. All
levels are retained. No per-author variance inversion or chosen parameters.
An exactly constant source block has a recorded neutral unit, not fake signal.

    ||Delta C||^2 = ||Delta S||^2 + ||Delta R||^2
                   + 2 <Delta S, Delta R>.

Report the signed cross term. Do not report the two energies as disjoint
personality percentages. Coordinate transport g->gA, y->yB requires
L0->inverse(A)L0 B, Vg->A^T Vg A and C/R->A^T C/R B.

## New synthetic material and theoretical boundary

Four worlds, three seeds, 40 authors (20 source / 20 application), three panels:

- Shared linear rule, repeatable author marginal shape. C/S can recur but R=0.
- Personal rotations with identical G/Y means and covariances. R can recur.
- Independent panel rotations. Relationship recurrence should be near null.
- Same nonlinear rule y=g^2 for everyone with different author distributions.
  R can recur without any person-specific rule. This is a constructive
  non-identifiability example, not new psychological accuracy.

Save actual generated numeric g/y and statistics, not invented RNG scores.
World labels are design truth, not inferred psychology. Evaluate all six panel
pairs. The noiseless worlds test algebra/identifiability, not realistic power.
