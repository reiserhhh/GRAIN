## The operational reading

For the frozen source function psi_kq(a,b), the author pair measure is the
Cartesian average of its two empirical windows, p_X(a,b). Define

    S_kq(X)=sum (p_X-rho)*psi_kq.

This is NOT P(Y|all34events). There are289 pair evaluations but only34distinct
events from ONEauthor, not289 independent observations. No product of pair
posteriors or confidence inflation is allowed.

Let r=(a//4,b//4), m_X(r)=sum_r p_X, m_rho(r)=sum_r rho,
nu_kq(r)=E_rho[psi_kq|r]. Keep BOTH terms:

    S_choice=sum_r (m_X-m_rho)*nu
    S_within=sum p_X*(psi-nu_r)
    S=S_choice+S_within.

This subtraction defines a reference-relative ACCOUNT, not removal of context
from vectors or scores. It is different from the HM193 change of OBSERVATION
LAW: S_R-S_P / S_J-S_R. Keep both decompositions with different names.

Every event's deletion changes normalized empirical mass. Record all deletions,
not just the largest helpful ones. For early event i, late event j:

    delta_i=(mean-early_row_i)/(n_A-1)
    delta_j=(mean-late_column_j)/(n_B-1)
    mixed_ij=(psi_ij-row_i-column_j+mean)/((n_A-1)*(n_B-1)).

These are numeric evidence-selection counterfactuals, NOT a new raw text
encoding, LLM attention effect or psychological change in the author. All5
