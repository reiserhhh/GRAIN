text-based measures of human personality, behavioral tendencies and current
states. It is NOT a finished clinical scale, another prediction model, or the

early prototypes. Both may need major replacement. Available corpora and current
features must not bound our theoretical question. Old positive evidence matters
but is not an axiom to defend. Scientific novelty comes from a new capability or
explanation, not completing a plan, naming a formula or promising a top journal.
