## Mechanism questions and comparisons

1. Does a native conditional-response geometry already occur in frozen GRAIN
   event coordinates, or only in the complete E5 representation?
2. Does that correspondence depend on target observer program or actor?
3. Are behavior, condition and their interaction distinguishable, rather than
   explaining every association by overall topic/wording similarity?

For each of the three nonconstant sectors, compare the six behavior-family
response vectors by LINEAR centered kernel alignment (CKA). Record all five
representation views, programs, actors, units and wording banks. Enumerate all
720 family pairings; report the finite matching rank/tail fraction, not a human
population p-value. Also retain cross-bank correspondence, response norms,
individual account differences and every Gram matrix. No winner promotion,
threshold tuning, CCA interpolation, target-regression fitting or factor naming.

Possible outcomes change the next construction decision:

- GRAIN and native fields share a response geometry: develop a qualified
  same-unit bridge, without assuming equal coefficients or psychological truth.
- E5 correspondence exceeds GRAIN only: investigate the fixed compression, not
  reader accuracy. No automatic factor/rank search in this packet.
- Neither corresponds consistently: keep query-conditioned observation as a
  distinct operation; investigate which additional conditional information it
  uses, not why a classifier score is low.

CKA is established methodology (Kornblith et al., ICML2019). The OpenAI/math
signed-tensor full-isotypic discussion motivates keeping compatible sectors
separate; its density/angle bounds are NOT asserted for our embeddings. This
packet's potential contribution is an empirical link and construction decision,
not a new theorem or a global novelty claim.
