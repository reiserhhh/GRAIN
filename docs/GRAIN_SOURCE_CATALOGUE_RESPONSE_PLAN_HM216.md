## Finite reference family

For each profile g, permute the existing bank-membership entries across whole
authors. The product of full symmetric groups is the transformation group;
the allocations preserving bank counts are its orbit, not a selected set of
"balanced permutations". Family2 additionally fixes the exact pair of
condition counts. This is a subgroup, leaving unmovable strata intact.

Include the observed allocation, then199 independent uniform group draws in
each family, with the two declared seeds. Do not increase the draws or change
strata after seeing results. Randomize neither personality assignment nor
the vector-condition pairing. No native answer or panel outcome selects a
rule. All references and all30 law/target/family summaries are retained.

For each allocation compute inherited positive Gaussian mixture potentials,
HM215 full-profile calibration and response defects, and80 conditional
cross-ratios. Primary statistic is RMS of all5 target cross-ratios in the
single-window law, averaged over the SAME48 edits and16 conditional cells.
They are coordinates, NOT independent subjects. Report the upper plus-one
rank (1+#reference>=observed)/200 and quantiles. Treat it as a conditional
allocation diagnostic, not an unconditional population/psychological p.
The other laws and individual target ranks are descriptive, not promotions.

## Exact orbit-average construction

Within stratum h let N_h be pooled size, n_bh the fixed bank allocation, and
a_i,b_i the two Gaussian window kernels. For profile size n_bg:

```text
E[A_bv] = sum_h n_bh mean_h(k_v) / n_bg
E[J_b] = sum_h n_bh mean_h(k_0 k_1) / n_bg
E[I_b] = E[A_b0] E[A_b1]
       + sum_h n_bh(1-n_bh/N_h) S_h(k_0,k_1) / n_bg^2
```

S_h is finite-population sample covariance with divisor N_h-1; singleton
terms have zero coefficient. Keep signed covariances. Even a marginal-product
reader's orbit expectation retains a catalogue-sampling covariance because
both empirical marginals use the same sampled authors. Do not conflate
expectation of potentials with expectation of log odds or causal mediation.
For profile-only exchange the single and paired density expectations equal
the pooled catalogue in both banks; product expectations can differ with
unequal bank counts. This is standard finite sampling, not a new theorem.

## Prior art actually consulted

[Hemerik and Goeman2018 sections2.1 and3](https://arxiv.org/html/1411.7565)
requires group invariance and inclusion of the identity; the hash-derived
split has not been established as an independent population sample. We use
full symmetric-group actions, not the invalid balanced-permutation subset.
[Gretton et al.2012 pp724-726](https://jmlr.org/papers/volume13/gretton12a/gretton12a.pdf)
distinguishes empirical averages from population distributions. We do not
implement MMD, transfer a characteristic-kernel test, or assert two groups
have equal psychology. Standard finite-population covariance gives the
explicit orbit mean above.
