# HM265: conditional order measurement without transition-kernel fitting

2026-10-13. Frozen before the primary numerical run. Original18/100/44 OPEN.

## Scientific question and new ability

HM264's complete held paths have zero support under historical inventories.
Do not rescue those inventories with smoothing, shorter prefixes or larger
state alphabets. Construct a different, explicitly conditional measurement:
how a complete new path uses order beyond its observed transition structure.

For every path choose the most frequent state (ties lowest state). Preserve
the prefix through its first visit and suffix after its last visit. Permute
ALL interior complete return excursions between these visits, keeping the
first and last return blocks fixed to preserve the boundary edges. Retain event identity
in the attested input; permutations are numeric counterfactuals, not real text.
All orbit members preserve start/end, length, occupancies and directed counts.
Uniform permutations are the conditional law under Markov exchangeability,
including mixtures of unknown homogeneous first-order kernels. This is a
declared competitor, not an assumption that natural text is actually Markov.
The conditioning is finer than transition counts: within-excursion structure
and prefix/suffix/boundary excursions are also fixed. This restricted orbit
does not cover every path with the same counts. No claim of removing every
first-order cause or measuring all higher-order information.

Let M(X) be the adjacent triple mass and B(X) its exact orbit expectation.
Compile SOURCE equal-author/equal-window full32-profile triple fields psi.
Keep raw reading S=<M,psi>, order reference A=<B,psi>, contrast D=S-A.
All32 profile readings and five binary-anchor contractions are retained.
These are average field functionals, NOT whole-person posterior probabilities.
Missing SOURCE cells remain missing, including cells introduced by the orbit.

The new construction scores a new path using its OWN orbit, not a SOURCE
path's fixed future budget. This is not a full generative law or an accuracy
promotion. HM93/95 currents, HM237 gap edits, HM245 order reversals and HM263
nonadditivity already exist; none supplies this kernel-cancelling orbit score.

  not held prediction scores, independent replications or significance tests.
- Positive mechanism toy:two same-count paths with a differently ordered
  (1,0,2) triple produce opposite conditional contrasts under a fixed field.
  Any additive edge functional has zero contrast. Two fixed unknown positive
  transition matrices and their mixture give identical probabilities within
  the toy orbit. Targets in the toy are designed, not human personality.

  ordered triples, D may persist despite unchanged pairs. Natural residual
  association can be zero, negative or stronger under old permutation; retain
  all outcomes. A nonzero D alone is not a unique psychological explanation.

## Finite computation, acceptance and stopping

Exact orbit mean from internal triples, two-segment boundaries and
three-segment boundaries. A width3 observation spans at most three nonempty
segments. Uniform labeled-block permutations induce uniform distinct words:
every repeated-block word has the same multiplicity. Use integer numerators
with denominator k(k-1) for k>=2; no Monte Carlo/mixing/search parameter.
One180s nonrenewable primary pass; freeze code/config/plan first. Preserve any
failure and all previous sealed results. Do not edit primary science afterward.

Acceptance is a correct new conditional scoring interface on complete paths,
preservation identities, documented support and all signed outcomes, not a
required positive r. Validate short paths by exhaustive orbit enumeration,
all natural probability/edge-margin accounts, alternate direct field sums,
and cold CLI readback. Same-agent verification, not blind replication.

## Prior art and limits

Fortini/Petrone, predictive Markov exchangeability:
https://arxiv.org/html/1406.5421v3 (Section3). Counts-based one-step prediction
alone does NOT imply exchangeability. The full path-probability invariance is
the assumption used here, not inferred from an fitted next-token rule.
Halberstam/Hutchcroft2024 finite Euler/BEST background:
https://arxiv.org/html/2405.20276v1 (Section2.1). No infinite-process result used.
OpenAI/math pinned adc7f124 observation coarsening (foundations217-232) inspires
asking what an observation preserves; no tree reconstruction theorem applied.
Permutation invariance and finite conditional probability are mature tools.
Project-specific contribution is the complete-path SOURCE-qualified scoring
construction and empirical mechanism decision, not a new mathematical theorem.
