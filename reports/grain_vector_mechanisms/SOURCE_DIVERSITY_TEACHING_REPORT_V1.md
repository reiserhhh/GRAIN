The current teacher observes their difference, not their average. It can learn
a useful contrast while moving individual query readings asymmetrically.
Fixed common-reader plus a minimum-change contrast lift is the current
implementation; this result alone does not identify which restriction caused
the asymmetry. In fact a fixed common reader need not make joint accuracy
impossible: a contrast-null direction can still move both absolute queries.
Four post-result synthetic witnesses verify this algebra separately from the
empirical run. See [theory](../../docs/GRAIN_SOURCE_DIVERSITY_TEACHING_THEORY_V1.md).
