## What changed

**Value-only coordinate teaching provides a useful local scalar improvement;
adding native-reader motion teaching reduces it.** Fixed/moving value-only
functions reach r=.6516/.6517 and normalized error .1652/.1665 versus NEXT84
.1783. Retain these coherent candidates rather than deleting coefficients from
the full fit. This is a local component gain on eight reused discovery authors,
not a new human personality-prediction benchmark.

More importantly, the zero-fit follow-up identifies a **teacher/composition
mismatch**: a more accurate native differential reader can be less compatible
with the source-compiled evidence it actually receives. Native-reader error
decreases while its compensating interaction with evidence error weakens.
NEXT87 will teach reading in the compiled evidence coordinates, not continue
native-vector/rank/kernel precision tuning.

## Raw terms are not conditional teaching updates

For moving F03, the full-fit raw value contribution has RMS .043571, but the
coherent value-only predictor has RMS .001048. The raw context coefficient
contribution has RMS .039853, but actually adding context to VB changes the
prediction by RMS .000441, about90 times smaller. Removing a raw contribution
is therefore not the same experiment as omitting its teaching observations.

The value-only predictor already has weak coordinate amplitude. Full-fit raw
coefficient cancellation alone does not explain the absence of strong amplitude.
Source4 coverage and scale limitations from NEXT85 remain observations, not a
reason to keep searching weights. Scalar-coordinate r is .718/.739 for V;
its raw coordinate error is slightly worse than VBC despite better assembly.

## Why better component fidelity can worsen the composition

A separate post-outcome, zero-fit account reconstructs every declared contrast
from three errors: source-compiled evidence, fixed common reader, and learned
differential reader. All signed cross terms are retained. For the moving family:

| Normalized scalar error account | V | VB | VBC |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Evidence + common-reader error energy | .399920 | .399920 | .399920 |
| Differential-reader error energy | .184407 | .181627 | .176292 |
| Signed cross term | -.417847 | -.405789 | -.394788 |
| Actual assembled error | .166481 | .175758 | .181425 |

The native differential-reader oracle would set only the second component to0
and leave error .399920. Thus maximizing native-reader fidelity by itself is
not the correct compiled-system objective. The dominant other error is evidence
transport (energy1.474479); common-reader error is .006910, with cross-.111493.
These are model-component errors, not human variance shares. The oracle is an
evaluation account, never an application input.

For anchored K+V the cross term is positive instead: evidence/common error
energy1.313184 plus differential .843977 and cross1.324051 gives3.481213 for
moving V. Scalar compensation therefore does not generalize to action recovery.
